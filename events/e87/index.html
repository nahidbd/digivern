Korea’s Digital Government Model: An Evidence-Based Case Study (CADGov Forum webinar 3)

When
9 December 2026, 11:00–12:30 Lisbon time (16:00–17:30 Tashkent)
Where
Online (Online)
Organiser
United Nations University Operating Unit on Policy-Driven Electronic Governance (UNU-EGOV)
Cost
Not stated (registration required)
Notes
Third webinar in the CADGov Forum series, part of UNU-EGOV’s capacity-building diploma for Central Asian public officials on digital governance and AI readiness. Speakers from Korea’s National Information Society Agency (NIA) and Kookmin University look at how Korea moved from early e-government to AI-enabled services, and what other countries can and cannot borrow from it.
