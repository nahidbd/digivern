ITU Workshop on Telecommunication Service Quality

When
2 December 2026
Where
Hyderabad, India (In person)
Organiser
International Telecommunication Union (ITU)
Cost
Free of charge; online registration is mandatory (ITU Member States, Sector Members, Associates, academia, and individuals from ITU member countries)
Notes
Hosted by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) with ITU-T Study Group 12’s Quality of Service Development Group. Followed on 3 December by the first meeting of the SG12 Regional Group for Asia and the Pacific and a hands-on Bridging the Standardization Gap training hosted by TRAI. Delegates must pre-register; there is no on-site registration.
