ITU Regional Hands-on Conference “Accelerating Innovation in Digital Public Services”

When
30 November – 1 December 2026
Where
Minsk, Belarus (In person)
Organiser
ITU with the National Centre for Electronic Services of Belarus
Cost
Not stated
Notes
Registration is mandatory; the ITU event page says it is open until 20 November (the earlier invitation letter said 25 November). Venue: National Centre for Electronic Services, 64 Pritytskogo street, Minsk. For telecom administrations, e-government operators and international organisations; topics include GovTech, benchmarking against the UN E-Government Development Index and AI in public administration, with hands-on AI training. Sessions in Russian, with English interpretation for some.
