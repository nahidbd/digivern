14th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE 2026)

When
December 10–12, 2026
Where
Dhaka, Bangladesh (In person)
Organiser
Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET); IEEE Bangladesh Section technical co-sponsor
Cost
Author registration BDT 7,000–12,000 (local) or USD 200–350 (foreign), depending on IEEE membership and student status
Notes
Venue: ECE Building, BUET. Tracks include AI and data science, computer systems, networks and security, and communication systems. Camera-ready deadline extended to 25 November 2026; author registration extended to 26 November (accepted papers) and 30 November (conditionally accepted papers).
