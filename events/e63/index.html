ITU Workshop on IMT-2030 terrestrial radio interfaces

When
12 February 2027, about 09:00–17:00
Where
Geneva, Switzerland (Hybrid)
Organiser
International Telecommunication Union, Radiocommunication Sector (ITU-R), Working Party 5D
Cost
Not stated
Notes
Venue: ITU Headquarters, during the 54th meeting of ITU-R WP 5D. Explains the IMT-2030 (6G) submission and evaluation process; proponents and independent evaluation groups present. Request a speaker slot by 11 January 2027; register by 30 January 2027 (non-members register on the ITU site). Remote attendance is for WP 5D participants who register separately. English only.
