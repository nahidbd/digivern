Global Government Summit 2027

When
27–29 January 2027
Where
Singapore, Singapore (In person)
Organiser
Global Government Forum, hosted by the Government of Singapore
Cost
Not stated
Notes
Invitation-only, closed-door round tables for senior government leaders such as heads of civil service and cabinet secretaries; themes include public service understanding of technology and AI, performance management, policy development and government resilience. Officials apply to attend through the event site. A separate Global Government Leaders’ Forum open conference for all public servants takes place on 27 January 2027.
