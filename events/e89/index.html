Nasscom Technology & Leadership Forum 2027 (NTLF, 35th edition)

When
24–25 February 2027
Where
Mumbai, India (In person)
Organiser
Nasscom (National Association of Software and Service Companies)
Cost
Not stated
Notes
India’s main annual gathering of the IT and technology services industry, with policymakers, company leaders and start-ups. The 2027 programme is built around four pillars, three of them on AI: the intelligence dividend, enterprise AI, the human side of AI, and science and deep tech. Venue reported as The Fairmont, Mumbai; dates are taken from a trade report citing Nasscom’s event page, so check with Nasscom before booking.
