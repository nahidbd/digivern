Forum on AI for Cities: Interoperable Solutions for Climate-Resilient, People-Centred Cities

When
8 December 2026, 09:00–16:00 (Manchester time)
Where
Manchester, United Kingdom (Hybrid)
Organiser
ITU with Manchester Metropolitan University
Cost
Not stated
Notes
Venue: 6th Floor, 3 Piccadilly Place, Manchester M1 3BN; remote participation option offered. Register through the ITU online form (no deadline stated). Open to ITU Member States, Sector Members, Associates, academic institutions and individuals from ITU member countries. Sessions cover AI for urban services, open standards and interoperability to avoid vendor lock-in, and inclusive, climate-resilient cities. Held just before the 2nd meeting of the ITU-T Focus Group on AI for Smart Sustainable Cities and Communities (9–10 December).
