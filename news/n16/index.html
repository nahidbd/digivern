Cyberattack takes Belgium’s MyMinfin tax portal offline; filing deadline moved to 16 November

28–29 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Belgium’s Federal Public Service Finance took MyMinfin, Tax-on-web and Biztax offline as a precaution after intruders entered its systems overnight on 27–28 October and tried to alter parts of its digital environment. The ministry says the intrusion was contained within hours and that no data was leaked. Returns due on 31 October can now be filed until 16 November 2026. Federal police and the Centre for Cybersecurity Belgium are investigating.

Why it matters

When a single tax portal goes down days before a deadline, the whole filing calendar moves; resilience and recovery planning matter as much as prevention for core e-services.
