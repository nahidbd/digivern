Plan to link Brazil’s Pix with the euro area’s TIPS instant payments moves to an investigation phase

5–6 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · official sourcing

Work on connecting Brazil’s Pix instant payment system to TIPS, the European Central Bank’s instant payment settlement service, has moved from a pre-investigation phase to an investigation phase. The pre-investigation in September looked at technical, legal, operational and commercial feasibility and found no critical obstacles, according to a document presented to the TIPS Consultative Group on 20 October. The new phase has no deadline, but reports in Brazil expect a pilot to be ready by the end of 2027. If it goes ahead, Brazilians could pay European merchants by QR code from their own banking apps, and merchants would receive euros. In a speech on 5 November, ECB Executive Board member Piero Cipollone listed possible links with Brazil and Switzerland alongside work with India’s UPI and Nexus. TIPS already settles euros, Swedish kronor and Danish kroner, with a cross-currency service since October 2025.

Why it matters

A Pix–TIPS link would be one of the first direct connections between two large central bank-run instant payment systems on different continents.
