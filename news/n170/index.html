Maharashtra launches 174 online services across three state portals and gives departments until 26 January to go digital

26–27 Nov 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis launched 174 government services and projects on the state’s digital platforms on 26 November 2026 at a review meeting of the Electronics, IT and AI Department in Mumbai, Elets eGov reported. They are split across MAITRI 2.0, the investor facilitation portal (76 services), Aaple Sarkar 2.0, the citizen services portal (74), and MahaDBT 2.0, the direct benefit transfer platform (24 schemes). Other systems launched include RTS 2.0 for the Right to Services, MahaUplabdhi for government correspondence, which also lets pensioners and beneficiaries generate digital life certificates on their phones, and a Maharashtra Aadhaar dashboard. Fadnavis said 397 of 1,129 administrative processes identified for restructuring had been completed, and Chief Secretary Rajesh Agrawal said more than 100 unnecessary services had been scrapped and documentation requirements cut by over 50%. Every department has been told to deliver a new digital governance experience by 26 January 2027.

Why it matters

Maharashtra is India’s second most populous state; moving whole service catalogues online while cutting steps and documents, rather than digitising paper processes as they are, is the reform many governments promise but few measure.
