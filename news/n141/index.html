APEC leaders adopt a 2027–2030 trade digitalisation framework and back paperless trade at their Shenzhen summit

18–19 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

APEC leaders, meeting in Shenzhen on 18–19 November, welcomed an APEC Trade Digitalization Cooperation Framework (2027–2030) in their closing ‘Shenzhen Outlook’ and pledged to advance paperless trade and cross-border e-commerce. The text also commits members to strengthen accessible and resilient digital infrastructure, close urban–rural digital divides, support cross-border data flows alongside data protection and privacy, and use digital and emerging technologies to improve public services, including healthcare. Leaders welcomed work under the APEC AI Initiative (2026–2030) and endorsed a statement on harnessing digital and emerging technologies for employment and prosperity. The Standard reported that the summit closed on the night of 19 November and that ministers also issued a joint statement on ‘smart customs’ arrangements to speed up regional supply chains. China’s foreign minister called the Outlook a milestone, the paper said. The documents set direction rather than binding obligations.

Why it matters

Paperless trade and smart customs commitments across APEC shape the digital border systems that small exporters, including in South and Southeast Asia, must connect to.
