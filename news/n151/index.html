US Department of Agriculture confirms a cyber threat behind outages in farm payment and loan systems, but keeps its 11 December sign-up deadline

20–23 Nov 2026 · 4 sources · media sourcing

A security incident at the US Department of Agriculture took systems for farm loans, payments, mapping and contracting offline from Monday 16 November 2026, after staff were told to reset database passwords in all environments. On 20 November Richard Fordyce, Under Secretary for Farm Production and Conservation, said it was a cyber threat rather than a software update and expected most systems to be back that day or over the weekend. Reports on 22–23 November said USDA believes its shutdown stopped personal data from being taken, but it has not said who was responsible or how many farmers were affected. The 11 December deadline for Agricultural Risk Coverage and Price Loss Coverage enrolment remains in place. A GAO report on 19 November found USDA had limited resources for cyber response.

Why it matters

It shows how an attack on a ministry's back-office systems can quickly disrupt payments and loans for farmers at a busy time of year.
