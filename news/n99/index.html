India’s central government schools get an AI-assisted monitoring centre, a parent app and an alumni portal

12 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Union Education Minister Pralhad Joshi launched three digital platforms of Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS), which runs India’s central government schools, in New Delhi on 12 November 2026. The Vidya Samiksha Kendra tracks attendance, academic results and school infrastructure across Kendriya Vidyalayas, with a “Samiksha” AI bot that analyses the data, identifies gaps and suggests corrective measures. The KVS-Samvaad Parent Connect app and web portal show parents real-time attendance, syllabus progress, exam results and learning materials, hold digital student ID cards, and let parents apply online for bonafide and Children Education Allowance certificates and for student leave. The KVS Alumni Portal lets former students reconnect and offer mentoring and career guidance. The platforms were developed in-house by the IT Services Cell of KVS headquarters. The minister asked for them to reach every Kendriya Vidyalaya, to be extended beyond KVS with the Department of School Education and Literacy, and for teachers’ training records to be added.

Why it matters

In-house tools that put school data in parents’ hands are cheap to scale across a national school network; the questions are how student data is protected and whether teachers check the AI bot’s suggestions.
