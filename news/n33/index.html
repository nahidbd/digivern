EU updates the scope of its deforestation rules ahead of 30 December 2026 start

13 Jul 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The European Commission adopted measures adjusting which products fall under the EU Deforestation Regulation and setting technical rules for its information system, including simplified declarations for micro and small primary operators. The regulation applies from 30 December 2026 to large and medium operators and from 30 June 2027 to other micro and small operators. Non-EU suppliers acting as operators must still provide plot-level geolocation.

Why it matters

Exporters of cocoa, coffee, palm oil and other goods need farm-level traceability to keep EU market access.
