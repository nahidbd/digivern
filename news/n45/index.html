New Zealand trials a government-issued digital credential for businesses, the NZBN Business Passport

3 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

New Zealand’s Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE) has begun a trial of the NZBN Business Passport, a digital credential based on the New Zealand Business Number that lets a business share verified information, including proof that a person is authorised to act for it. Selected Westpac business customers hold the passport in the Govt.nz digital wallet and use it alongside a bank account credential issued by Westpac, which in September became the first New Zealand bank accredited to issue digital credentials. Biometric Update describes it as the first government-issued credential and the second accredited under the Digital Identity Services Trust Framework. MBIE says it will consider wider adoption based on feedback from businesses and banks.

Why it matters

Business credentials are a practical next step after personal digital ID: they cut repeated know-your-business checks for small firms and show how a public wallet and private issuers can work under one trust framework.
