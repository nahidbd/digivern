Italy opens its IT-Wallet to all, with a mobile driving licence and ID documents that have legal force

23–24 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

On 23 November 2026 Italy opened its public IT-Wallet to all citizens and residents through the IO app; in this first phase it is voluntary and open to IO users who hold the electronic identity card (CIE). Users can store a mobile driving licence — accepted in police checks and as official ID — the European Health Insurance Card, the European Disability Card, a certificate of residence, and school and academic qualifications. Police, businesses and other relying parties check documents with the Valida Documenti app, and an ‘Enter with IT-Wallet’ button is to be added gradually to online services. Undersecretary for innovation Alessio Butti said 13 million Italians have activated Documents on IO. The decree governing the IT-Wallet system was published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale on 3 August 2026; public administrations, public service providers and publicly controlled companies must join as authentic sources of attestations by 3 August 2027, and private operators may also join. Italy’s data protection authority approved the technical details in October. Italy is among the first EU states with a live European Digital Identity Wallet, ahead of the EU deadline of 24 December 2026.

Why it matters

Italy’s wallet is one of only a handful live before the EU deadline and already has millions of users, making it the main early test of whether Europe’s digital identity wallets will be used in daily life.
