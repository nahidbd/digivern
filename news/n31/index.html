Starlink residential service goes live in Uganda after a five-year licence

1 Sep 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Starlink began taking residential orders in Uganda on 1 September 2026, after the Uganda Communications Commission granted a five-year operating licence in May. Conditions include a national gateway, a local office and local staff. Business resellers launched in late August. Starlink had disabled Ugandan terminals in January after a regulator directive before the election.

Why it matters

Satellite broadband can reach areas fibre does not, but licence conditions keep it under national control.
