Pakistan keeps subsidising merchant fees on Raast QR payments as merchants accepting digital payments pass 2 million

12–13 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has decided to continue the subsidy on merchant discount rate (MDR) charges for Raast QR payments to encourage merchants to accept them, the Finance Ministry said on 13 November 2026. Minister of State for Finance Bilal Azhar Kayani told bank presidents, chief executives and chief digital officers, with officials of the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), Raast and Karandaaz present, that banks must play a more active role in the Prime Minister’s Cashless Pakistan Initiative, and asked the SBP to consult banks on the user experience of QR and other digital payments. At a Cashless Pakistan Committee meeting reported on 12 November, Kayani directed the SBP to prepare a working paper for a Raast QR MDR scheme. The meeting heard that active merchants accepting digital payments had risen from 0.5 million to 2.03 million and that digital payment volume had doubled in a year. From 16 November all payments for Asaan Assignment Accounts are to go through Raast. Neither report gives the cost of the subsidy or how long it will last.

Why it matters

Paying merchants’ fees on QR payments is how Pakistan is trying to make Raast acceptance worthwhile for small shops; how long the subsidy lasts and who funds it will decide whether the gains hold.
