Uganda’s business registry launches URSB Connect, a mobile app for company registration and trademarks

5 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

The Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) launched the URSB Connect App on 5 November 2026. It is on Google Play and the Apple App Store. Through the app people can register companies and business names, search the register, file annual returns and use intellectual property services such as trademark registration. It also gives information on insolvency, receivership and the Security Interest in Movable Property Registry (SIMPO). The bureau says the app complements its existing online eRegistry rather than replacing it. Reports do not say which transactions are completed inside the app and which send users to the existing web systems, and they give no fees or user numbers.

Why it matters

Moving registration to phones could help bring Uganda’s many informal businesses into the formal economy without trips to registry offices.
