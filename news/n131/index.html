South Africa opens passport applications at 480 Capitec and Absa branches, as bank Smart ID points pass 830

18 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

Home Affairs Minister Leon Schreiber announced on 18 November 2026 in Cape Town that South Africans aged 18 and over can now apply for passports at 480 bank branches – 412 Capitec and 68 Absa – without an appointment. That takes passport service points from 228 Home Affairs offices to 708, which the minister called a 208% increase in access, and he said an application now takes about five minutes thanks to digital reforms. Holders of the old green ID book who apply for a passport at a participating branch also automatically apply for a Smart ID card. The bank partnership, which began offering Smart ID services in March, now covers 833 branches, passing its 750-branch target more than four months early, with more than 1.16 million Smart ID applications processed in eight months. Home delivery of documents, first-time applications at banks and a new biometric ePassport are being prepared.

Why it matters

It shows how a government can widen access to identity documents quickly by running its digital enrolment system through private bank branches.
