Bangladesh’s Farmer Card will send farmers automatic SMS weather forecasts

2 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Bangladesh’s Ministry of Agriculture said on 2 November that farmers enrolled in the Farmer Card programme will receive automatic SMS weather forecasts, every six hours in normal conditions and more often during disasters, through the Agriculture Information Service under the Department of Agricultural Extension. Agriculture Secretary Md. Selim Khan said the alerts add a new dimension to the card’s integrated services. The programme targets about 24 million farmers over four years, with cards going to 4.3 million farmers in FY2026-27. Data on 2.2 million farmers has been collected and 900,000 linked bank accounts opened; a phase launched on 1 October in 77 upazilas of 56 districts enrolled 110,165 farmers. A March–June 2026 pre-pilot gave cards to 20,832 farmers and paid Tk 5.12 crore, at Tk 2,500 per farmer a year. The card is planned to bundle 10 services, including inputs, low-interest loans and crop insurance.

Why it matters

A farmer registry with a card and a linked bank account is becoming a channel for climate advice as well as payments, the same path India is taking with Farmer IDs.
