Uganda’s MoKash mobile loans turn ten with a campaign rewarding repayment, days after the Prime Minister attacked mobile lending costs

17 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

NCBA Bank Uganda and MTN Mobile Money Uganda launched MoKash@10, a Shs140 million rewards campaign marking 10 years of MoKash, which Nile Post describes as Uganda’s first mobile-based savings and loan service. The 12-week campaign runs from 17 November 2026 to 16 February 2027 and rewards customers who borrow and fully repay within 30 days, keep consistent savings, or clear eligible loans more than 60 days overdue, including previously written-off loans. Finance Minister Henry Musasizi spoke at the launch, and Bank of Uganda data cited show electronic money transactions rising from about Shs285.9 trillion in 2024 to Shs366 trillion in 2025. The launch follows a parliamentary exchange in which Prime Minister Robinah Nabbanja criticised short-term loans from MTN and Airtel; borrowing through the two platforms reached at least Shs2.75 trillion in 2025, and MTN’s borrowers rose from about 2.2 million to 3.4 million.

Why it matters

Mobile-money credit has become a mass product in Uganda, and its cost and repayment burden are now a political issue alongside regulation of digital lenders.
