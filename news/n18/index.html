Brazil’s telecom regulator makes face biometrics mandatory to activate and keep mobile lines

28 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Anatel issued Technical Norm GT-SEG.2026.11, drafted by a fraud and scams subgroup, requiring all mobile operators, including MVNOs, to verify individual customers with a face check and liveness detection at ISO/IEC 30107-3 level 2 or higher. Deadlines are 30 September 2027 for new prepaid lines, 31 December 2027 for postpaid activations, portability and SIM swaps, and 31 December 2028 for existing customers. From January 2029, unvalidated lines face a 120-day suspension, with emergency calls preserved, and then cancellation.

Why it matters

It ties the right to a phone line to biometric verification for millions of people, raising inclusion and data protection questions that other regulators fighting SIM fraud will face too.
