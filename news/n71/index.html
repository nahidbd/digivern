Uganda’s communications regulator considers making parents register the SIM cards their children use

5–6 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

The Uganda Communications Commission (UCC) is considering requiring parents to declare the SIM cards their children use, so that operators could keep records of child-held SIMs and apply protections against cyberbullying, grooming and exploitation, Executive Director Nyombi Thembo said on 5 November 2026. He spoke at the opening of the two-day UCC Testbed Hackathon 2026 in Nakawa, Kampala, where 40 young innovators, 13 of them women, chosen from more than 400 applicants, were building AI tools for child online safety (ITWeb Africa). UCC figures cited at the event put the share of students facing digital risks at 88.5%. Ecofin Agency describes the measure as a proposal linked to the Child Online Protection Regulatory Framework 2026 and cites a UCC survey of more than 5,700 students in which 74.2% of those harmed told no one.

Why it matters

Registering children’s SIMs would create a new database about minors; how it is held, and who can see it, will matter as much as the safety goal.
