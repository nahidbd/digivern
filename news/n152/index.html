Swiss National Bank and eight financial firms start exploring a shared ‘Swiss Financial Ledger’ for tokenised money and securities

24 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

On 24 November 2026 the Swiss National Bank (SNB) announced an exploratory project on a ‘Swiss Financial Ledger’, a distributed-ledger platform on which central bank money, commercial bank money and securities could interact. The participants are UBS, SIX, Zürcher Kantonalbank, PostFinance, Banque Cantonale Vaudoise, Basler Kantonalbank, Sygnum Bank and the settlement platform Seturion, with the Swiss Bankers Association as an observer and a new Helvetia Strategic Board to coordinate the work. The project builds on the SNB’s Project Helvetia, under which it has offered wholesale central bank digital currency on the SIX Digital Asset Platform since December 2023, and on Project Agorá, the BIS and Institute of International Finance initiative on tokenised cross-border payments. It will look at whether one ledger can serve both domestic and cross-border transactions, along with design options, governance, operations, benefits and risks. The SNB stressed that the project does not mean such a ledger will be launched, nor that it will issue tokenised central bank money to financial institutions; no timetable was published.

Why it matters

A major central bank is testing whether a single shared ledger for money and securities could become national financial infrastructure, a model other countries building payment DPI are watching.
