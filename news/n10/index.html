India issues 110 million Farmer IDs under its Digital Agriculture Mission

24–26 Oct 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

India has created 11 crore (110 million) unique Farmer IDs under the Digital Agriculture Mission of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare, five months ahead of its 31 March 2027 deadline. Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan announced the milestone on 24 October and said more than 6 crore (60 million) farmers have already received benefits through the Farmer ID-based delivery system, which is to support Kisan Credit Cards, crop insurance claims and procurement at minimum support prices.

Why it matters

Farmer registries are becoming the base layer for subsidies, credit and advisory services in agriculture-heavy economies.
