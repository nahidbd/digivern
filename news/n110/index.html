Global Microsoft 365 outage disrupts NHS emergency-call handling, hospitals and government offices in dozens of countries

13–14 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

A Microsoft 365 outage that began on Thursday 12 November 2026 disrupted Outlook, Teams and other services in 74 countries and territories, according to Downdetector data cited by AFP on 14 November. In the UK, the GMB union said NHS emergency-call staff were “unable to refer patient details in the normal way”, creating a large backlog; the NHS told patients to attend appointments as normal. Toronto’s St. Michael’s Hospital declared a “code grey”, while Queensland Health reported no known clinical impact, Insurance Business reported. Immigration New Zealand halted processing of all visa applications, the Japanese government said its shared IT platform was disrupted, and economy minister Ryosei Akazawa warned of a “significant impact”. In the US, sheriff’s offices, city governments and courts reported email outages, Gizmodo said. Microsoft said “We are deeply sorry for the disruption”, attributed the incident to “an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade” and called it “a technical issue not related to cyber or AI/agents”; by Friday it reported broad recovery.

Why it matters

It shows how much hospitals and public administrations depend on a single cloud provider, and why continuity plans for public services need non-cloud fallbacks.
