Spain’s electoral authority accepts the MiDNI digital ID app at polling stations for the 29 November general election

10 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Spain’s Central Electoral Commission (Junta Electoral Central) has confirmed that voters may prove their identity with the MiDNI mobile identity app in the general election on 29 November 2026, Biometric Update reports. Its status had been uncertain; the decision followed assurances from the Ministry of the Interior that every polling station will have a device able to read QR codes. Voters generate a temporary QR code on their phone and election officials scan it to verify the credential; the ministry is distributing more than 60,000 devices with cameras, browsers and access to the MiDNI QR verifier. The People’s Party had argued that showing a digital ID on screen was not enough and that the QR codes should be verified. The MIDGT digital driving licence remains excluded as proof of identity. EU member states face a 24 December 2026 deadline to offer European Digital Identity Wallets.

Why it matters

Using a mobile ID to vote is a demanding test: it works only if every polling station can check the credential quickly and reliably, which is why Spain made acceptance depend on QR verification rather than on looking at a phone screen.
