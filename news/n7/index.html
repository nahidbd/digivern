Intrusion attempts on state, local government and education networks up 242%, vendor data shows

22 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · expert sourcing

SonicWall data for the first half of 2026 shows a 242% year-on-year rise in intrusion attempts against state, local and education (SLED) networks, with web application attacks making up 85.1% of the activity. Malware detections rose 8.6%, while ransomware activity fell 74%. SonicWall links the rise to outdated technology, limited staff and patchy software updates.

Why it matters

Local governments run many citizen-facing services on ageing systems; the figures support funding for patching and staffing, not only new systems.
