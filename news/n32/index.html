Uganda’s data protection office says over 500,000 organisations process data without registering

19 Aug 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office says about 14,000 organisations have registered under the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019, while more than 500,000 collect personal data without registering. Its director said unregistered processing is a criminal offence and that the office is working with police and prosecutors. He urged citizens to ask why businesses photocopy their national ID cards.

Why it matters

Registration of data controllers is the first step of enforcement; the gap shows how far compliance still has to go.
