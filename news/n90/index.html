South Africa offers Smart ID replacement at 753 bank branches, passing its 750-branch target more than four months early

11–12 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

South Africa’s Department of Home Affairs now offers Smart ID card replacement at 753 bank branches, above its target of 750, Home Affairs Minister Leon Schreiber announced on 11 November 2026. The target was set for the end of the 2026/27 financial year in March 2027. The bank channel opened on 9 March 2026 at branches of Absa, Capitec, First National Bank and Standard Bank; applicants need no booking or paperwork, and biometric checks keep an application to about five minutes. With the branches, Smart IDs can now be obtained at 967 locations, against 214 Home Affairs offices at the start of the current administration, which the ministry calls a rise of more than 350% in 246 days. More than one million South Africans have used the bank channel. The department says more banks and services will join under its ‘Home Affairs @ home’ reforms, which include phasing out the green barcoded ID book.

Why it matters

Using bank branches as front offices is a cheap way to bring identity services closer to people without building new state offices, and other countries scaling digital ID can copy it.
