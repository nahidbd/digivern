Nigeria launches a Grassroots Digital Extension Corps of 1,884 tablet-equipped extension agents and food security specialists across all states

11 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Nigeria's Federal Government formally launched the Grassroots Digital Extension Corps (GDEC) in Abuja on 11 November 2026. Run by the National Agricultural Development Fund with the Federal Department of Agricultural Extension, it has trained 1,110 extension agents and 774 food security specialists from the 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory; training began in Lafia in July, and each graduate receives a tablet loaded with the approved curriculum. NADF says GDEC rests on people, tools and data, and will be judged on farmers reached, adoption of better practices and yield gains. Minister of State Aliyu Sabi Abdullahi asked that it be integrated with the ministry's National Electronic Extension Platform to avoid duplication, and proposed a ministerial committee to merge existing schemes into one national extension system. Officials noted many crop yields are only 1.3–1.5 tonnes per hectare.

Why it matters

Digital extension is one of the cheapest ways to raise smallholder yields, and the call to fold GDEC into a single national platform addresses the fragmentation common in agritech programmes.
