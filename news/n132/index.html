Philippines’ education department scales up AI tools to flag likely dropouts and screen Grade 2 pupils for learning difficulties

18–19 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

The Philippine Department of Education (DepEd) will expand its AI-based dropout early-warning system to 300 schools in 2027, Education Secretary Sonny Angara said at the second anniversary of DepEd’s Education Center for AI Research (ECAIR) in Makati City on 19 November 2026. The system analyses learner data to flag pupils at risk of leaving school and was co-designed with about 120 teachers and school heads from three schools in Las Piñas City. DepEd is also rolling out Project SABAY (Screening using AI-Based Assistance for Young Children), which uses short gamified speech exercises such as picture naming and word dictation to screen Grade 2 pupils for possible learning and communication difficulties. A screening takes about 15 minutes per pupil, compared with up to two hours for the existing manual assessment, and pilots that began in the National Capital Region have spread to the Visayas and Mindanao. Angara said technology ‘must empower people, and not replace them’.

Why it matters

It is a concrete example of a ministry moving classroom AI from pilots to scale, with teachers involved in design.
