Uganda’s mobile money flows reach UGX 263 trillion in seven months

9 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Mobile money transactions in Uganda were worth UGX 263 trillion in January–July 2026, 72% of the full-year 2025 total of UGX 365.9 trillion, according to Bank of Uganda data. First-half value rose 32.5%. Active customers reached 38 million in July 2026 and agents 1.3 million. Later Bank of Uganda figures reported by the Daily Monitor show 62.12 million registered mobile money accounts at the end of September 2026, of which 39.10 million (62.9%) had been used in the previous 90 days, leaving 23.03 million inactive; in the 12 months to September there were 10.14 billion transactions worth Shs435.70 trillion.

Why it matters

Mobile money is the most used digital service in Uganda; any disruption, such as an internet shutdown, hits it first.
