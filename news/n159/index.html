Czechia picks a Wultra-led consortium to build and run its EU digital identity wallet, Valetka, now due by the end of 2027

24 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

On 24 November 2026 the Czech Digital and Information Agency (DIA) announced that it had chosen a consortium led by Wultra, with iProov, Signicat, Youniqx Identity and Partners Bank, and Amdocs as a subcontractor, to deliver the national European Digital Identity (EUDI) Wallet under a concession. The app will be called Valetka. The state targets more than four million users and 1,500 participating businesses by 2032; the contract value and length were not disclosed, and the DIA planned to sign by the end of November. More than 200 ‘WalletPoints’ for in-person help will be run with Czech Post. Under the concession the contractor builds, operates and bears the risk of the wallet. Valetka is now due by the end of 2027; in February 2026 the DIA had still aimed to launch the wallet ecosystem by the end of 2026, with full production in early 2027.

Why it matters

Czechia is handing its national identity wallet to a private concessionaire that carries the risk, and its later date shows how hard the EU’s end-2026 wallet deadline is to meet.
