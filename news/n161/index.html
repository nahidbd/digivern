Ethiopia and the World Bank agree to review, and possibly restructure, the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project

24 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

On 24 November 2026 in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia’s Minister of Innovation and Technology, Frehiwot Tamru, met a World Bank delegation led by Division Director Maryam Salim. The two sides agreed to a joint review of the Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project (EARDIP), a World Bank-financed regional programme, which could restructure its activities to speed up implementation. They also discussed closing the Ethiopia Digital Foundations Project and a five-year strategy for the digital sector aligned with the government’s Medemer Grand Strategy. No figures for the restructuring were published.

Why it matters

A restructuring decides what the regional digital project will actually deliver in Ethiopia, from cross-border connectivity to shared digital services, and how fast.
