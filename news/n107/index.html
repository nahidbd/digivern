Romania sets 14 January 2027 launch for its RO Wallet, the national EU digital identity wallet

12–13 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Romania’s government spokesperson Dan Cărbunaru said on 12 November 2026 that the official launch of “RO Wallet”, the country’s European Digital Identity Wallet developed by the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MAI), is scheduled for 14 January 2027, Bursa.ro reported. A beta version for a restricted initial group of users is planned for 22 December 2026, running alongside certification, to meet the EU rule that member states provide at least one wallet by 24 December 2026. The first version will hold identity data, a digital driving licence, domicile and residence information and an age-verification function; qualified electronic signatures and new electronic attestations are due from February 2027. Biometric Update (13 November) adds that the MAI acts as issuer of person identification data and wallet provider, that enrolment from January will first be remote for electronic ID card holders using the card’s NFC chip and PIN, with in-person verification for holders of classic ID cards from the second quarter, and that full certification is targeted for December 2027. Romania chose Germany’s EUDI Wallet infrastructure in June 2026.

Why it matters

Romania is one of the first EU states to fix a public launch date for its EUDI wallet, testing whether national wallets will be ready around the EU’s December 2026 deadline.
