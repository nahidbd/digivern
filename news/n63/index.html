Pakistan’s health ministry launches Care1166, a digital complaints system for public hospitals in Islamabad

4 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Pakistan’s Ministry of National Health Services launched the Care1166 Health Complaint Management System on 4 November 2026. Residents of Islamabad can call the toll-free 1166 helpline to complain about the ministry, its attached departments, and government hospitals and dispensaries. Each complaint is routed automatically to the responsible officer and given a digital tracking number. A central dashboard follows each case to resolution, and the heads of 39 departments receive complaints through a mobile app and office screens. The call centre runs 24 hours a day in three shifts. The Nation puts the project’s value at about Rs400 million and says it was set up with the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority to reach remote areas. The ministry plans to expand the system nationwide later and to analyse complaint data for signs of disease outbreaks.

Why it matters

It gives patients a trackable channel for grievances and gives the ministry data on where public health services are failing.
