Rwanda approves $19m more World Bank financing for digital ID and CRVS

12 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Rwandan lawmakers ratified about US$19 million in additional IDA financing for the Rwanda Digital Acceleration Project. The State Minister reported the digital ID rollout as 76% complete and CRVS digitisation as 89% complete; the National Identification Agency reported about 13.7 million people biometrically enrolled as of 4 October. The project’s closing date moves to June 2028.

Why it matters

Rwanda is one of the few countries building a single digital ID together with digitised civil registration, a model many governments are watching.
