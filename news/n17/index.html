Türkiye plans a digital ID wallet prototype in 2027 and sets e-Devlet growth targets

27 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Türkiye’s 2027 Presidential Annual Program, published in the Official Gazette on 27 October, plans a national digital ID wallet prototype in 2027, led by the Cybersecurity Presidency with the Ministry of Interior. It would first hold basic identity documents and diplomas, piloted with a university and prioritising public employees. The programme targets at least 71 million e-Devlet users, about 10,000 services and 7,500 mobile services in 2027, up from 9,436 services from 1,130 institutions in September 2026. Separately, from 1 November social media platforms may not open accounts for children under 15, with ages checked through an e-Devlet age token.

Why it matters

Türkiye is pairing a mature e-government portal with a wallet and privacy-preserving age checks, a combination many countries outside the EU are now weighing.
