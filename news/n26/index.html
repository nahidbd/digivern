Uganda launches a five-year National Data Strategy costed at UGX 1.66 trillion

21 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Uganda’s Ministry of ICT and National Guidance launched the National Data Strategy for 2026/27–2030/31, developed with UNDP and UN Global Pulse. It aims to raise the number of government platforms integrated through the UGhub data exchange from 135 to 300, raise the share of national plan indicators backed by verifiable data from 36% to 80%, and build two Tier IV data centres with 10 petabytes of sovereign storage. The plan is costed at UGX 1,662.6 billion over five years. MPs at the launch asked how data hosted abroad will be protected and called for cutting the 10% import duty on entry-level devices.

Why it matters

A costed, targeted data strategy is rare in the region; its test will be whether annual budgets fund it.
