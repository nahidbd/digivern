Lesotho launches a national farmer registry built on a GovStack specification

30 Aug 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

Lesotho’s Ministry of Agriculture, Food Security and Nutrition launched the Lesotho Farmers Portal, its first national registry combining farmer and land data. Farmers are registered against their national ID number and land parcels with GPS boundaries, using the GovStack Registration Building Block specification with support from ITU and WFP.

Why it matters

A practical example of reusable DPI building blocks applied in a small country’s agriculture sector.
