Nigeria’s Gombe State expands digital personalised learning to 764 public schools and more than 100,000 pupils

9 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Gombe State has begun expanding digital personalised learning (DPL), supported by the education technology organisation EIDU, to 764 public schools with a target of more than 100,000 learners, Daily Trust reports. The expansion was formally launched at Mu’azu Bolari Primary School in Gombe after a pilot that reached more than 1,000 learners, over 285 teachers and 25 staff of the State Universal Basic Education Board (SUBEB). Education Commissioner Aisha Umar Maigari said the state has a legal commitment to the programme and is awaiting disbursement of funds; no amount was given. SUBEB Chairman Hassan Bello said: “We are no longer experimenting; we are expanding what works.”

Why it matters

Scaling ed-tech from a pilot to a whole state’s primary schools is where most programmes stall; Gombe’s move shows the step depends on state budgets being released, not only on the technology.
