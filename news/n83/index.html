WHO and OECD release TArGET Health, a tool to help governments govern emerging health technologies before they arrive

10 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The World Health Organization and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) launched TArGET Health, the Tool for the Anticipatory Governance of Emerging Technologies in Health, on 10 November 2026 at an event at the OECD in Paris that was also streamed online. WHO says the jointly developed tool supports more comprehensive assessments of technologies and of possible governance solutions, and turns policy principles and governance concepts into a practical step-by-step process. Its stated aims are to strengthen the capacity of those who make health technology policy, narrow the gap between technological advances and policy responses, and deal with challenges that arise when technologies converge.

Why it matters

Health ministries usually regulate AI, genomics and other new technologies after they are in use; a shared WHO–OECD method gives smaller countries a way to assess them in advance without building the expertise from scratch.
