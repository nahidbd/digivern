Moldova takes 82 e-services offline after attackers extract data linked to 750,000 ID numbers

27–29 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Moldova’s Electronic Governance Agency (AGE) said attackers exploited government e-services that needed no login between 19 and 23 October, querying about one million personal identification numbers (IDNPs) and extracting data linked to about 750,000 of them. Exposed fields include family links, health insurance status, benefits and pensions, driving licences, photographs, bank account numbers (IBANs) and tax debts; bank card data and state registers were not affected. As a precaution, 82 public services and more than 30 information systems were disconnected. Prime Minister Vasile Tofan said foreign state involvement is being examined, and AGE director Nicoleta Colomeeț is stepping down, with Artiom Cociu leading the agency on an interim basis.

Why it matters

Unauthenticated services that query interconnected registries can turn a data exchange layer into a single point of mass exposure; it is a warning for every country building once-only services.
