Himachal Pradesh puts every government school teacher on a digital profile with an AI skills baseline

22–31 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

India’s Himachal Pradesh signed an agreement on 22 October with Bharti Airtel Foundation to use its free Teacher App across government schools. On 31 October the Directorate of School Education told district officials to make sure all teachers and heads of schools register and complete a baseline survey of their digital and AI skills. The results will steer needs-based training, including AI Samarth Level 1 and 2 courses and an “AI Tools in Teaching” kit, in place of one-size-fits-all workshops.

Why it matters

Mapping teachers’ digital skills before rolling out AI in classrooms is a step many education systems skip; a state-wide baseline lets training budgets follow actual gaps.
