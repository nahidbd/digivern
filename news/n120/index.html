Bangladesh extends the October VAT return deadline to 22 November after its eVAT system was overloaded

16–17 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Bangladesh's National Board of Revenue (NBR) has given taxpayers until 22 November to file their October 2026 VAT returns online, without interest or penalties, after its eVAT system ran into trouble. In a notice issued on 16 November, the NBR said an unusually high number of people used the eVAT system at the same time on 15 November, congesting its servers and leaving many taxpayers unable to submit returns on time. It said it was extending the deadline in the public interest, using its power under Section 64(2) of the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012. The Daily Star described the change as a seven-day extension. Neither report said how many taxpayers were affected or how many returns had been filed.

Why it matters

Online filing deadlines bring sudden peaks in traffic, and this outage shows that Bangladesh's tax platform needs more capacity as the government moves more payments and filings online.
