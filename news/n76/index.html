Israel publishes a draft national digital identity strategy built around a national digital wallet, open for comment until the end of November

9 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

A committee of Israeli public bodies has published a draft national strategy for digital identification for public comment. The draft was released by the National Cyber Directorate with the National Digital System, the Population and Immigration Authority, the Ministry of Finance’s Budget Division, the Accountant General and the Bank of Israel. It proposes a digital identity based on the national ID card that would become the default way to access services, with a digital version of the card, legally equivalent to the physical one, held in a national digital wallet alongside other credentials. The draft also covers preparation for post-quantum cryptography, interfaces for private-sector relying parties, personal AI agents and, in the long term, mutual recognition of digital IDs with other countries. Comments are open until the end of November. Biometric Update notes that Israel is in post-election coalition talks, which may affect the plan’s priority.

Why it matters

Israel already has a biometric ID card; the draft shows a country moving from a card to a wallet that private firms can rely on, the same step the EU and others are taking.
