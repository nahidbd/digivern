Nigeria’s agriculture minister calls for one national digital farmers register anchored on the NIN

30 Sep 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

At a national dialogue in Abuja, Agriculture Minister Abubakar Kyari proposed a single national digital farmers register built on existing work with the National Identity Management Commission, IFAD and FAO. States would enumerate and verify farmers; the federal ministry would set one interoperable standard. The aim is to target fertiliser, loans and other support to genuine farmers and support export traceability. No registration total was given.

Why it matters

Nigeria’s attempt to merge duplicate farmer databases is a test case for agriculture registries across Africa.
