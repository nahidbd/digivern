China links its digital yuan to Laos’ national payment network; AMRO sets out the lessons

2 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Since 18 September 2026, Chinese visitors in Laos can pay from their e-CNY digital yuan apps by scanning the QR codes of 17 members of LAPNet, Laos’ national payment network (15 banks and two e-money providers), while Lao merchants receive kip through domestic channels. The People’s Bank of China and the Bank of the Lao PDR launched the link in Nanning. In a commentary published on 2 November, the ASEAN+3 Macroeconomic Research Office (AMRO) said it is unusual in connecting a retail central bank digital currency to a domestic payment network, and that settling in kip helps preserve monetary sovereignty. It said wider functions such as remittances and person-to-person transfers should follow only after sandbox testing, and that authorities should watch currency substitution, capital-flow rules and money-laundering risk.

Why it matters

It is one of the first live links between a retail CBDC and another country’s payment switch, and the indicators AMRO proposes (active users, success rates, fees, refund and fraud rates, exchange-rate spreads) are a useful checklist for any cross-border QR link.
