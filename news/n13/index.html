Digital Citizen Summit in Hyderabad debates making India’s DPI more accountable

28 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · expert sourcing

IIIT Hyderabad hosted the Digital Citizen Summit regional dialogue on digital public infrastructure for governance, organised with the Digital Empowerment Foundation and Code for Good Tech. Participants discussed how to make DPI such as Aadhaar, UPI and DigiLocker more inclusive, accountable and accessible.

Why it matters

Civil society scrutiny of India’s DPI matters globally because the model is being promoted abroad.
