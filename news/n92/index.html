Bangladesh Bank data show digital payments concentrated in a few players, with bKash handling about two-thirds of mobile money

12 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

A report by Bangladesh Bank’s Payment Systems Supervision Department, reported by The Business Standard on 12 November 2026, shows that a few institutions carry most of the country’s digital payments. Over January 2025 to June 2026, five banks carried about 37% of the banking sector’s digital transactions and the top ten 55.21%, led by Dutch-Bangla Bank with Tk1.68 lakh crore (9.50%) and BRAC Bank with Tk1.65 lakh crore (9.32%); Sonali Bank is the only state-owned bank in the top ten. Mobile financial services (MFS) handled Tk6.60 lakh crore in April–June 2026, of which bKash, Nagad and Rocket together carried 99.15%. bKash alone had about 67%, roughly Tk4.41 lakh crore, against Tk1.23 lakh crore for Nagad. MFS digital transactions grew 68.69% between the second quarters of 2025 and 2026. For Bangla QR payments routed through the National Payment Switch Bangladesh in the same quarter, bKash, Rocket and BRAC Bank together accounted for 82.70% of value. The underlying central bank report has not been published online.

Why it matters

With one wallet carrying two-thirds of mobile money, interoperability through Bangla QR and the planned Inclusive Instant Payment System is Bangladesh Bank’s main lever to open up competition.
