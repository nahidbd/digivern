Tripura says all its government hospitals are now on one digital health system

31 Oct – 6 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

Health Secretary Kiran Gitte said on 31 October that 100% of government hospitals in the Indian state of Tripura are onboarded to its Integrated Health Management Information System (IHMIS), meeting the 31 October target he set in August. IHMIS covers outpatient registration and tokens, admission and discharge, prescriptions and pharmacy, and lab reports sent to patients’ phones. Records are to be linked through each patient’s Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) number so that referrals between hospitals carry the patient’s history. The number of records already linked to ABHA was not disclosed; getting doctors to use the system every day is the next step. At the National Health Authority’s review meeting on Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission in Kolkata on 5–6 November, Gitte announced that all government hospitals in the state will be fully paperless from 10 November 2026, and that private facilities are to be brought onto the platform within about two months.

Why it matters

Onboarding hospitals is the easy part; daily use by clinicians and linking records to a national health ID decide whether a digital record actually follows the patient.
