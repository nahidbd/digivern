WHO/Europe reports on four years of its digital health action plan, including a first regional review of AI for health

30 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

WHO’s Regional Office for Europe published its biennial progress report on the Regional digital health action plan 2023–2030, covering June 2024 to March 2026. WHO says it helped 20 countries strengthen health data collection and use, supported national digital health strategies in Albania, Armenia, Bulgaria, Latvia, North Macedonia and Spain, helped Bosnia and Herzegovina bring telemedicine into primary care, and worked with Georgia, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan on interoperability. It also issued a first report on AI for health in the region and the EU, and an analysis finding that equity is applied inconsistently in digital health practice. A member-state working group fed into the regional roadmap for AI in health 2026–2033, which all 53 member states adopted at the 76th session of the Regional Committee, and is contributing to the next global digital health strategy for 2028–2033.

Why it matters

WHO’s own conclusion that more must be done on digital literacy and access for patients and health workers is a lesson for every country building national health records.
