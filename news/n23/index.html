New York City lists 38 ed-tech products caught by its ban on student-facing AI in public schools

23 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Chalkbeat obtained the list of 38 previously approved products whose generative AI features New York City public schools must switch off or drop under restrictions announced by Mayor Zohran Mamdani. It covers widely used reading and maths programmes, assessment tools and the education department’s own chatbot teaching assistant. The AI reading tutor Amira, used in more than 200 schools, was banned outright, and Google’s AI assistant is being turned off for students. Some vendors dispute that their tools use generative AI; the department is reviewing products case by case, with a carve-out for tools that help English language learners.

Why it matters

The largest US school system is showing how hard it is to regulate AI inside ed-tech procurement when vendors’ definitions of AI differ; education ministries face the same vetting problem.
