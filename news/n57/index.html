Philippine social security cards are to join the eGovPH app as a digital ID

21 Oct – 5 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

The Social Security System (SSS) and the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) will integrate the digital ID feature of the MySSS Card and selected member services into the eGovPH super app, under a data-sharing agreement signed on 21 October in Quezon City by DICT Secretary Robert Joseph de Claro and SSS President and CEO Victor Alfonso Limlingan. Members will be able to show a digital version of their MySSS Card as one of the IDs in the app and see their contribution history, total number of contributions, latest membership type and most recent employer. Limlingan said members should not have to visit several offices or use several apps to reach basic services.

Why it matters

Adding social insurance credentials and records to the national super app moves the Philippines towards one front door for government, but it also concentrates more personal data in one channel, raising the bar for its security.
