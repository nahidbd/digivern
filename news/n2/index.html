CIPESA report warns DPI is reshaping state–citizen relations in 11 African countries

13 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · expert sourcing

The State of Internet Freedom in Africa 2026 report by CIPESA, launched at FIFAfrica26 in Mauritius, reviews digital public infrastructure in Egypt, Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Senegal, South Africa, Tunisia, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe. It finds that design flaws, technical failures and inaccurate records in foundational ID and mandatory digital gateways often exclude vulnerable groups.

Why it matters

It adds African evidence to the case for rights-respecting DPI safeguards before systems become entrenched, a priority for donors funding ID and registry programmes.
