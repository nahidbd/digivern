Global agrifoodtech funding holds at US$16.2 billion, with money shifting to farms

18 Mar 2026 · 2 sources · expert sourcing

AgFunder’s 2026 report counts US$16.2 billion of agrifoodtech venture funding in 2025, flat on 2024, with deal numbers down 12%. Upstream funding for farms and food production rose 7% to US$9 billion, climate technology in the sector recovered to US$3.9 billion and debt reached 18.2% of funding.

Why it matters

Private capital is concentrating in deep technology; public registries and payments remain the main channel for smallholders.
