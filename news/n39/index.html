Jamaica to require national ID registration for hurricane relief; opposition calls it coercion

27–30 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

At a Hurricane Melissa press briefing on 27 October, Prime Minister Andrew Holness said everyone seeking recovery benefits, including grants, must register with the National Identification System (NIDS), calling it indispensable because Jamaica lacks a database that uniquely identifies citizens. On 30 October opposition justice spokesperson Zuleika Jess called the plan “completely unacceptable”, saying it turns a voluntary ID into a compulsory one through economic pressure and goes against the spirit of the 2019 court ruling that struck down the original NIDS law. She said thousands of people assessed and verified after the October 2025 hurricane are still waiting for aid.

Why it matters

Tying emergency aid to ID enrolment can speed payments and cut duplication, but it can also exclude the people most in need; Nepal reversed a similar requirement for social security benefits in 2024.
