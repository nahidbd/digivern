Malaysia’s cyber agency puts MyDigital ID at the centre of national cyber defence

31 Oct – 3 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Speaking at the rawSEC MiniCon 2026 conference in Kuala Lumpur on 31 October, Dr Megat Zuhairy Megat Tajuddin, chief executive of the National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA), said digital identity must be built around trusted elements and that the Malaysia Cyber Security Strategy 2025–2030 depends on cooperation between government, business and the public. Azrin Aris, deputy chief executive of MyDigital ID Sdn Bhd, said the move from single-factor to multi-factor authentication has significantly strengthened digital identities. MyDigital ID has passed 14.1 million users and is integrated with 204 applications and websites; enrolment uses face biometrics with liveness detection, and the team says it is preparing for deepfakes, software supply-chain attacks, post-quantum cryptography and AI agents that transact for people.

Why it matters

Malaysia frames digital ID as security infrastructure rather than only a convenience, a view that shapes how voluntary schemes become hard to avoid as more services depend on them.
