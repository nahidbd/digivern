Bangladesh unveils a digital health push built on an e-health card and a national health database

18 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

At an event in Dhaka organised with the World Health Organization, State Minister for Health and Family Welfare Dr M A Muhit set out plans for a universal electronic health card and a national health database for nearly 200 million people. The database would link consultations, prescriptions, medicine purchases and test results. Plans also include app-based appointment booking, digital tracking of medicines supplied to public hospitals to curb illegal sales, and a national e-Hackathon. No budget or rollout timetable was announced.

Why it matters

A shared health record linked to a national card would be the backbone for e-prescriptions and referrals; the missing timetable and budget will decide whether it moves beyond announcements.
