Singapore merges its public hospital apps into one national HealthHub app, with single-payment bills and live polyclinic slots

25 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

On 25 November 2026 Singapore’s Minister for Health, Ong Ye Kung, launched an enhanced HealthHub app at Ng Teng Fong General Hospital. It replaces the three apps run by the public healthcare clusters: the NUHS app will stop on 14 February 2027, the NHG Health app on 22 February 2027 and SingHealth’s Health Buddy app on 28 February 2027. Patients can pay bills from different public institutions in one transaction with Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayNow, see a green, yellow or red indicator of polyclinic appointment availability, have eligible chronic medicines delivered, and download digital medical certificates and records. The minister said HealthHub already has well over a million users; a helpline and the government’s digital ambassadors are helping people move across. The Ministry of Health had told Parliament in September that a single consolidated national health app would be launched in November.

Why it matters

Folding separate hospital apps into one national front door, with records and payments in the same place, is a model for countries whose citizens juggle many health portals.
