Kenya’s Farmerlifeline wins the Smart Africa–FAO challenge on AI for climate-smart farming, with Ugandan teams among the finalists

13 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Smart Africa, with FAO as partner and support from Norad, named Kenya’s Farmerlifeline the winner of the Innovate Africa Challenge III, which focused on artificial intelligence for climate-smart agriculture, TechAfrica News reported on 13 November 2026. Eleven finalist teams from Ghana, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda and Uganda took part in a bootcamp and final pitch in Kigali. The winner receives a US$50,000 implementation grant. Its solution uses solar-powered AI field cameras to detect pests and crop diseases and sends farmers advice by SMS in local languages; it is to be deployed over six months.

Why it matters

Pest and disease alerts that reach farmers by plain SMS in local languages target the gap that keeps most smallholders out of app-based advisory services; the six-month deployment will show whether the approach works beyond a pitch.
