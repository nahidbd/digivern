Malaysia launches ‘AI TEMU AI’, a national AI education initiative with AI literacy guidelines for schools

25 Nov 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim launched Malaysia’s National AI Education Initiative, ‘AI TEMU AI’, in Putrajaya on 25 November 2026. According to a joint statement by the Ministry of Education and YTL AI Labs, it has three parts: DELIMa 3.0, the ministry’s digital learning platform, enhanced with AI for personalised learning; the MOE AI Literacy Guidelines, covering four areas — AI governance, teaching and learning, lifelong learning and AI ethics; and Cikgu ILMU, an AI learning platform built by YTL AI Labs on ILMU, a Malaysian AI model, that helps parents with homework and exam preparation by generating revision guides, quizzes and flashcards. The initiative is aligned with the Malaysia Education Blueprint 2026–2035 and the Digital Education Policy, and the statement says AI adoption must not come at the expense of human values or the important role of teachers. No budget or user figures were given.

Why it matters

Setting national AI literacy rules for schools alongside the platforms pupils actually use gives teachers a common basis for AI in class — a step many education ministries have yet to take.
