Bank of Canada opens consultation on accrediting firms for open banking

2 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The Bank of Canada published draft supervisory guidelines on accreditation on 2 November, the first in a series of guidelines for its supervision of participants in Canada’s Consumer-Driven Banking Framework, under which people and businesses can share their financial data with approved providers of their choice. The draft covers how the Bank plans to accredit firms that want to take part, including third parties that manage consumer consent, handle authentication information or provide or receive consumer data. The Bank will set standards for governance, risk management and operational resilience; the Department of Finance leads the regulations, which have been published in draft in the Canada Gazette. Written comments are due by 8 December 2026.

Why it matters

Accreditation rules decide which fintechs and payment firms can get into an open banking system and on what terms; Canada’s draft is a fresh reference for central banks and regulators designing their own data-sharing regimes.
