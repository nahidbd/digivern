UK Budget funds a £40m unit to detect AI systems that slip their controls

28 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

The UK Budget of 28 October 2026 includes £40 million for a National Agentic Security Unit to detect and respond to AI systems that go beyond their intended controls, and £24 million for cyber defence in hospitals, schools and councils, according to reports citing City AM.

Why it matters

It is one of the first government units aimed specifically at agentic AI risk, alongside new money for public-sector cyber defence.
