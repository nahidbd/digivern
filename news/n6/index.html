Panama sets out a 10-year national AI strategy built on five pillars

12 Aug – 20 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Panama’s Cabinet Council approved the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy by Resolution No. 89-26, SENACYT announced on 12 August 2026; the Ministry of the Presidency and SENACYT sponsored it. Developed by SENACYT with Georgia Tech, it rests, according to Georgia Tech, on five pillars: governance and regulation, talent and institutional capacity, data and infrastructure, ethics and inclusion, and international cooperation. Its design drew on a national survey with more than 6,700 respondents.

Why it matters

A structured, consultative strategy from a smaller economy is a useful template for countries drafting their first AI strategy.
