India lets banks put digital rupee wallets inside their regular mobile banking apps, as retail e-rupee in circulation falls

16 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has allowed banks to offer digital rupee (e₹, India’s retail central bank digital currency) wallets inside their existing mobile banking apps, so customers no longer need a separate wallet app, Outlook Money reported on 16 November, citing Mint. Customers can move money from a savings account into the e₹ wallet and pay from that balance. According to RBI data cited in the report, 19 banks support digital rupee wallets, including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Union Bank of India and Bank of Baroda. Retail CBDC in circulation fell to ₹771.66 crore at the end of 2025–26 from ₹1,016.50 crore a year earlier. The retail pilot began in December 2022, and the RBI allowed UPI QR payments from e₹ wallets in 2023.

Why it matters

Folding the e-rupee into apps people already use is a test of whether a retail CBDC can win users in a market where UPI already makes payments instant and free.
