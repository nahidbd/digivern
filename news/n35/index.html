India launches Bharat-VISTAAR, an AI farm advisory service described as public infrastructure

17 Feb 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

India’s agriculture minister launched Bharat-VISTAAR in Jaipur. Farmers can get weather, market price, pest, soil and scheme information by phone helpline, chatbot or app, drawing on AgriStack, weather and market data. It launched in Hindi and English with plans for 11 languages within six months.

Why it matters

Voice-first AI in local languages is how advisory services can reach farmers who do not read well or use apps.
