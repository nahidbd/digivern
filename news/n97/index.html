India’s NPCI sets up the UPI Vyapar Saksham scheme, paying banks ₹300 per QR code and ₹750 per soundbox to bring small merchants onto UPI

11–13 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has created the UPI Vyapar Saksham Scheme to spread UPI acceptance among small merchants, focusing on Tier III to Tier VI towns and giving priority to the north-eastern states, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. Acquiring banks receive ₹300 for each physical QR code and ₹750 for each soundbox installed for a small merchant. Eligible merchants have annual turnover of up to ₹40 lakh, including those under PM SVANidhi and PM Vishwakarma, and need at least 25 eligible transactions worth at least ₹5,000 a month in at least two of the three months of a quarter. The fund is built from 5% of the merchant discount rate (MDR) share of banks, payer apps and UPI app providers, and is split 30% for onboarding, 50% for transaction volume (0.03% of eligible value, subject to 5% quarter-on-quarter growth) and 20% for growth in targeted areas. IBTimes, citing an NPCI circular of 11 November, reports that the scheme runs for three years from when revised UPI MDR rules take effect: no MDR on merchant payments up to ₹2,000, 0.40% above that, capped at ₹300 for payments of ₹75,000 or more, and free person-to-person transfers. Neither start date has been confirmed. NPCI expects 6–10 million new QR codes and 0.5–1 million new soundboxes a year. MediaNama reported on 16 November, citing the NPCI circular of 11 November, that the scheme is funded from the merchant discount rate on person-to-merchant UPI payments above Rs 2,000: issuing and acquiring banks, UPI apps and payment service providers each contribute 5% of their share every month, for three years from the date the fee starts, which has not yet been notified.

Why it matters

Using fees from larger merchant payments to fund acceptance among small shops is India’s way to pay for UPI’s expansion while keeping most payments free; the effect depends on when the MDR change actually starts.
