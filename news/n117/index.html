World Bank Group to test regulated dollar stablecoins, with Citi and Circle, for paying out development funds

17 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The World Bank Group said on 17 November that it will explore whether regulated US dollar stablecoins and blockchain payment rails could work alongside, or improve, the correspondent-banking system it now uses to disburse funds to clients. Citi will provide connections to established banking rails, and Circle will provide infrastructure to mint and settle funds in its USDC stablecoin. The first phase will test an intermediated, interoperable payment model and will be limited to borrower countries where digital assets are supported and permitted; the Bank said it plans to work with other banks over time. The effort builds on FundsChain, the Bank's blockchain platform for tracing disbursements, which has more than 350 projects in 96 countries onboarded. President Ajay Banga said faster, cheaper money movement means real development impact. The release gave no transaction volumes or budget.

Why it matters

If a multilateral lender routes project money over stablecoin rails, it could shape how borrower governments and their central banks regulate and connect to such payment systems.
