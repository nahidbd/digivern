Singapore’s central bank will build a ‘Know Your Agent’ protocol so banks can check the AI agents acting for customers

19 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · official sourcing

Chia Der Jiun, managing director of the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), used his keynote at the Singapore FinTech Festival on 19 November 2026 to announce a new phase of SAFR (Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime) that will develop a ‘Know Your Agent’ protocol. The planned standard would let financial institutions exchange information on an AI agent’s identity, the authority it holds, its mandate, and its provenance and reliability. Chia said he would not advise people to give personal AI agents log-in credentials to their financial accounts, as there is ‘insufficient assurance of safety’. MAS will also run an Agentic Money Sandbox with the Future of Finance Institute, combining AI agents with programmable money for corporate treasury and payment use cases. In the same speech MAS said $100m of tokenised MAS Bills had been issued and settled against a Singapore-dollar wholesale CBDC in November, published a Guardian Tokenised Collateral Framework and a BLOOM Field Guide on digital money, and said its SGD Testnet will be renamed SG Digital Ledger and run as an operational utility.

Why it matters

It is one of the first attempts by a central bank to set common identity and authority checks for AI agents that move money, an issue every payment system will soon face.
