Uganda’s statistics bureau launches its fourth national statistical development plan and urges agencies to use AI and big data

13–16 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · official sourcing

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS) launched the Fourth Plan for National Statistical Development (PNSD IV), its Strategic Plan IV and new Service Delivery Standards on 13 November 2026 at Statistics House in Kampala during Africa Statistics Week, according to the UBOS event notice. The Daily Monitor reports that PNSD IV is a five-year, Shs1.4 trillion plan covering 263 institutions, including 135 district local governments and 64 ministries, departments and agencies. State Minister for Planning Amos Lugoloobi warned that inflated figures misdirect public resources, while UBOS Executive Director Dr Chris Mukiza called for investment in artificial intelligence and big-data analytics, the Monitor reported; Nile Post quoted Mukiza describing the board’s role as “overseeing the production of quality official statistics”. UBOS is expanding its online data dissemination portals. The plans follow the National Data Strategy for 2026/27–2030/31 that Uganda launched in October.

Why it matters

Official statistics are the data layer under Uganda’s planning and its new data strategy; the AI ambitions depend on registries and data-sharing that are still being built.
