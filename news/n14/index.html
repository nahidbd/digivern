Cambodia rolls out a national health information system on DHIS2

15 May 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Cambodia’s Ministry of Health launched a new electronic Health Management Information System on the DHIS2 platform, to be rolled out in all 25 provinces with support from WHO, UNICEF, CHAI and Gavi.

Why it matters

DHIS2, a digital public good, is the backbone of health data in many lower-income countries.
