Pakistan selects 10 public services for digital delivery under World Bank-backed DEEP

29 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Pakistan’s US$78 million Digital Economy Enhancement Project, overseen by NITB and NADRA, has chosen 10 priority services for digital delivery and linked single sign-on to the National Data Exchange Layer. NADRA’s PakID Vault in the Pak App now holds six official documents, including the CNIC.

Why it matters

It shows a country joining digital ID, a data exchange layer and service delivery in one programme, the core DPI pattern.
