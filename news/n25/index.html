Bangladesh opens person-to-person payments by Bangla QR across banks and wallets

1 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

From 1 November 2026, people in Bangladesh can send money to another person by scanning that person’s Bangla QR code in any bank or mobile financial service app, without typing a phone or account number, Bangladesh Bank said in a press release. The service extends the national QR standard, which became mandatory for all merchants on 1 July and now works at about 39 lakh merchant points, to transfers between individuals across banks and wallets. Fees and transaction limits were not announced at launch.

Why it matters

QR-based person-to-person transfers are how UPI and Pix became everyday tools; their success in Bangladesh will depend on whether fees stay below the 0.85% cap on MFS-originated transfers.
