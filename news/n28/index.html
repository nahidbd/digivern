Uganda and Korea’s aid agency explore AI cooperation, including translation of government information

15 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Uganda’s ICT Permanent Secretary Dr Aminah Zawedde met a KOICA delegation to discuss cooperation on AI, digital infrastructure, ICT skills and digital government services. Ideas included using AI to translate government information into local languages and digital tools for the Parish Development Model. The talks were exploratory, with no projects or funding announced.

Why it matters

Local-language AI could widen access to public information in a country with many languages.
