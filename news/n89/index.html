MTN Mobile Money offers Uganda's industrial parks payment devices on instalments as the investment ministry presses for farmer and transport uptake

10 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

MTN Mobile Money Uganda has proposed taking cashless payments into the country's industrial parks, offering manufacturers, farmers, transport operators and other firms mobile money-enabled devices, SIM cards and pre-installed payment apps that can be paid for in instalments. A delegation led by General Manager Phrase Lubega presented the plan to Aminah Mukalazi, Minister of State for Privatisation and Investment, citing bulk payments across networks, free withdrawals for amounts up to Shs300,000 and an existing cashless fare partnership with Kiira Motors on electric buses. The minister said trust in digital payments still has to be built among farmers and market vendors, and asked MTN to reach farmers' cooperatives, taxi operators and refugee settlements and to provide a safeguard for reversing mistaken transfers. The plan is a proposal, with no prices, dates or park names announced.

Why it matters

It shows Uganda's government trying to steer mobile money from consumer transfers into business, agricultural and transport value chains, where cash still dominates.
