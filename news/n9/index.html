Italy’s privacy regulator backs the decree setting the rules for its EU digital identity wallet

23–29 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Italy’s data protection authority, the Garante, gave a favourable opinion (decision no. 631 of 23 September 2026, publicised in its 23 October newsletter) on a draft decree from the Department for Digital Transformation that sets the technical and organisational measures for the IT-Wallet, Italy’s implementation of the EU Digital Identity Wallet. It asked for a clear definition of each party’s role, specific measures for special categories of data such as biometrics, and proper handling of users’ requests to delete their data.

Why it matters

EU wallets must launch soon; Italy’s review shows what regulators expect from wallet architectures.
