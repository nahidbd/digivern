India’s Consent Manager rules under the DPDP framework take effect on 13 November

Takes effect 13 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules notified on 13 November 2025, Rule 4 on registering and running Consent Managers takes effect on 13 November 2026. The main obligations on data fiduciaries, including consent, notices, security safeguards and breach notification, follow on 13 May 2027. SCC Online agrees that Rule 4 applies one year after notification but dates the notification, and so the start, a day later (14 November).

Why it matters

Consent Managers are India’s answer to user-controlled data sharing and a reference point for other countries designing consent layers.
