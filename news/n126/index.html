World Bank lends $100 million for Samarkand primary care, expanding Uzbekistan’s D-MED digital health platform

17 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · official sourcing

The World Bank’s Board approved a $100 million concessional IDA loan on 17 November 2026 for the SHIFO-1 programme to strengthen primary healthcare in Uzbekistan’s Samarkand region. The programme has a total budget of $525 million, with the government covering $425 million, and targets about 4.3 million residents. It will expand the national D-MED digital health platform with an electronic patient register and population health management tools at primary care level, expected to screen 330,000 adults for cardiovascular disease risk and help monitor treatment for 250,000 patients. More than 400 family clinics will be modernised and over 6,400 health workers trained and certified, including 1,250 general practitioners and 5,000 nurses. The programme runs until December 2028.

Why it matters

It ties new development finance to a national digital health record and screening system rather than just buildings and staff.
