Uganda’s national ID drive reaches 35 million people, but new registrations lag

9 Feb 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Uganda’s registration authority NIRA closed the parish phase of its mass ID registration on 8 February 2026 with about 35 million people in the system. All 28.6 million legacy records were migrated and 90.5% of planned renewals completed, but only 37.3% of 17.2 million targeted new registrations were achieved, and 2.58 million of 7.26 million cards shipped to districts had been collected.

Why it matters

Registration is only half the job; uncollected cards and missing newcomers limit what the ID can do.
