African Union member states validate the first continental framework for governing health data

22 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Meeting in Addis Ababa, AU member states validated the African Continental Health Data Governance Framework, developed by Africa CDC with Transform Health through consultations since 2025. It sets common rules for lawful, secure and ethical use of health data and proposes a federated model, so countries can share data across borders for disease surveillance and care while keeping control of their national systems. It builds on the Malabo Convention, the AU Data Policy Framework and the Digital Transformation Strategy for Africa. Africa CDC will now finalise the text, which is expected to go through AU policy processes in 2027.

Why it matters

Cross-border health data sharing in Africa has stalled on trust and sovereignty concerns; a shared framework gives ministries a common baseline for national health data laws.
