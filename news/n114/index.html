Kenya starts a World Bank-supported land digitisation project with an assessment of 18 land registries in eight counties

16 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Kenya’s Lands Principal Secretary Generali Nixon Korir launched implementation of the Housing Finance, Lands and Sustainable Investments (HOFLASI) Project, a five-year project supported by the World Bank, on 16 November 2026, the Ministry of Lands, Public Works, Housing and Urban Development said. Field teams will carry out a reconnaissance exercise from 16 to 27 November at 18 land registries in Nairobi, Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, Baringo, Isiolo, Marsabit and Mombasa counties to establish their condition. According to Citizen Digital, the project covers digitising spatial and non-spatial land records, upgrading registry ICT, enhancing the Ardhisasa online land system, a geodetic reference framework and survey infrastructure, and a review of land laws. Lands Secretary Sarah Maina is the project coordinator. “Every shilling must be accounted for, and we have timelines to meet,” Korir said. Neither source gives the amount of financing.

Why it matters

Complete digital land records are what make Ardhisasa, mortgage lending and property tax work; the registry assessment will show how much paper is still left to convert.
