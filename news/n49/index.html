Banglalink launches Mukto Pay, a payments app under its new payment service provider licence

3 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Banglalink, the Bangladesh operator owned by VEON, launched Mukto Pay at a press conference in Dhaka on 3 November. It runs under the Payment Service Provider licence that Bangladesh Bank granted in August 2026. The app is open to customers of any mobile operator, and Banglalink customers can also send money, pay utility bills and recharge from basic phones. Users can pay online at more than 9,500 merchants, a separate app serves merchants, and Banglalink says it will promote Bangla QR, the national interoperable QR standard. Banglalink chief executive Johan Buse and VEON Group chief executive Kaan Terzioğlu presented the service; the company says it plans to open a digital bank within six months, following the approval in September 2026 of a VEON–Square Group digital bank. Bangladesh is VEON’s fourth market for financial services after Pakistan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.

Why it matters

A telecom-backed payment service provider that works across operators and promotes Bangla QR adds competition to a market dominated by two mobile money providers, and is a test of the interoperability Bangladesh Bank is building.
