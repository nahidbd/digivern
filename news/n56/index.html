Grameenphone and Robi start accepting Bangla QR payments in their stores

4 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

Grameenphone, with Mutual Trust Bank (MTB) as partner bank, began accepting Bangla QR payments at its own retail stores on 4 November, starting with its experience centres in Gulshan and Bashundhara in Dhaka and at GEC Mor in Chattogram, according to a company press release. Customers scan the code with any compatible banking, mobile financial services or payment service provider app. Grameenphone describes itself as the first telecom operator in Bangladesh to adopt the interoperable national QR standard, which supports Bangladesh Bank’s “One Country, One QR” vision. Bangladesh Bank Deputy Governor Dr Md Habibur Rahman was chief guest at the launch; the release notes the central bank’s target of one crore (10 million) digital transactions a day. On the same day Robi Axiata, also with MTB, launched Bangla QR payments across all its walk-in centres and retail network, with the same deputy governor as chief guest, which weakens Grameenphone’s “first” claim.

Why it matters

Large consumer brands accepting the national QR, rather than a single wallet’s code, is what makes Bangla QR interoperable in practice.
