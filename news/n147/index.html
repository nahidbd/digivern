Egypt’s central bank brings tokenised card payments to Android phones, after 292 million transactions through Apple Pay

23 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

The Central Bank of Egypt announced on Monday 23 November 2026 that payment card tokenisation is now available on Android smartphones, developed with banks and fintech companies. Users store a digital version of their card in a mobile app and pay at point-of-sale terminals or online, confirming payments with a fingerprint or facial recognition instead of a PIN. The first phase, through Apple Pay, began in December 2024. According to the central bank, about 292 million transactions worth EGP 228 billion had been made through the service by the end of September 2026. Governor Hassan Abdalla said the Android launch would widen access to digital payments and support the move towards a society that depends less on cash.

Why it matters

It opens phone-based contactless card payments to Android users, not only iPhone owners, as Egypt tries to reduce its reliance on cash.
