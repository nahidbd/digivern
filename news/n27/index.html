NASA and the US Department of Agriculture agree to use satellite data in crop estimates

24 Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

NASA and the US Department of Agriculture signed a memorandum of understanding on 24 October 2026 to apply Earth observation data, geospatial analytics and modelling to crop monitoring, acreage and yield estimates and drought assessment. It forms part of USDA’s plan to modernise its agricultural data systems.

Why it matters

Official crop statistics move world prices; better use of satellite data is a model for statistics offices elsewhere.
