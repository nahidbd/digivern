Maharashtra has given AgriStack digital farmer IDs to 1.44 crore farmers, the second-highest number of any Indian state

26 Nov 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

Maharashtra’s Agriculture Minister, Dattatraya Bharne, said on 26 November 2026 that 1.44 crore of the state’s 1.71 crore farmer account holders (2021 Agriculture Census) now hold a digital Farmer ID under AgriStack, India’s digital public infrastructure for agriculture, placing the state second in the country. Of 85.58 lakh PM-KISAN beneficiaries in the state, 84.6 lakh have an AgriStack ID. The roll-out began in October 2024, registration through Common Service Centres opened on 21 January 2025, and a Farmer ID has been required to claim benefits under agriculture schemes since 15 April 2025. More than 3.10 crore plots were covered in the digital crop survey for the 2026 kharif season. Of nearly two lakh forest-rights title holders, 78,000 are registered, and a Revenue Department order of 6 November 2026 sets rules for issuing Farmer IDs in exceptional cases.

Why it matters

Making a digital farmer ID the key to every scheme shows how a farmer registry becomes the backbone of agricultural support — and why the remaining gaps, such as forest-rights holders, decide who is left out.
