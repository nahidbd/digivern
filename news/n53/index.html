Ugandan hospital group C-Care moves patient payments to MTN mobile money

3 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · media sourcing

C-Care Uganda launched a cashless payment project at C-Care International Hospital Kampala in Namuwongo on 3 November with MTN MoMo Uganda and the payments firm NexPay. Cash makes up almost half of the group’s collections; the project aims to speed up payment, improve transparency and cut revenue leakage. When a patient receives a bill they give an MTN number, approve a prompt on their phone, and the payment appears at once in the hospital system; relatives or sponsors can also pay. The partnership was announced on 29 October by MTN MoMo chief product officer Jemima Kariuki-Njuguna and the hospital’s general manager, Dr Miriam Mutero-Musinga, and is to be extended to other C-Care facilities.

Why it matters

Paying for care by mobile money is a small step for one private group, but it shows how Uganda’s mobile money rails are moving into health payments, where cash still dominates.
