Ghana’s MTN MoMo adds monthly wallet caps of GH¢150,000 and GH¢300,000 after a Bank of Ghana review of limits

24–26 Nov 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

MobileMoney Fintech Limited, which runs MTN MoMo in Ghana, has introduced monthly transaction caps of GH¢150,000 for Medium KYC wallets and GH¢300,000 for Enhanced KYC wallets, effective 24 November 2026, according to a company statement dated 25 November. Until now neither tier had a monthly limit. Daily and balance limits are unchanged: Medium KYC wallets can hold up to GH¢40,000 and send up to GH¢15,000 a day, Enhanced KYC wallets GH¢75,000 and GH¢25,000. The company linked the change to a Bank of Ghana review of wallet limits under its financial stability agenda; its chief regulatory risk and compliance officer, Godwin Kwami Tamakloe, said MoMo and other operators are implementing the revised limits. The statement does not name a specific Bank of Ghana notice, and it is not yet confirmed that every operator applies the same caps. The company also warned customers that MoMo never asks for a PIN, one-time password or fee to review limits.

Why it matters

Monthly caps on mobile money wallets are a lever central banks use against fraud and illicit flows; how they are set decides whether heavy users such as traders are pushed back to cash or into bank accounts.
