Bangladesh Bank sets July 2027 for its Inclusive Instant Payment System linking banks and mobile money, with remittances under study

9 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Bangladesh Bank is preparing the Inclusive Instant Payment System (IIPS), a common payment channel for banks, mobile financial service (MFS) providers and other financial institutions, with launch targeted for July 2027, The Report says. IIPS would carry person-to-person, merchant, business and government payments, including salaries, allowances and social safety net transfers. It is being built on the open-source Mojaloop software with technical support from the Gates Foundation, and builds on the National Payment Switch Bangladesh (NPSB), running since 2012; an earlier attempt to connect providers fell short on coverage. Bangladesh Bank Executive Director Md Sirajul Islam said the main aim is one channel for all institutions and acknowledged that a more comprehensive framework is needed. The Policy Research Institute (PRI), also with Gates Foundation support, began research on IIPS in July 2026, including cross-border remittances; PRI Chairman Zaidi Sattar noted that remittances are about 6% of GDP. Cross-border use would need further international, foreign-exchange and settlement arrangements. Bangladesh Bank had first announced the plan in November 2025, when it signed an agreement with the Gates Foundation to build the system on the open-source Mojaloop software and the governor set July 2027 for full operation.

Why it matters

Today bKash, Nagad, banks and new operator wallets are separate networks; an interoperable instant switch is what would let any Bangladeshi pay any other at low cost and route government payments and remittances to whichever account people use.
