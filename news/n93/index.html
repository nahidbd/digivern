Uganda tells the ITU Plenipotentiary it has reached 96% mobile network coverage as it seeks re-election to the ITU Council

11–12 Nov 2026 · 3 sources · official sourcing

Uganda’s Minister of ICT and National Guidance, Justine Kasule Lumumba, delivered Uganda’s policy statement on 11 November 2026 at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference (PP-26) in Doha, which runs from 9 to 27 November. Citing a 2025 GSMA report, she said 96% of Uganda’s population is now covered by a mobile network, and that more than 100,000 young people have received digital skills training over the past three years. She pointed to projects paid for by the Universal Access Fund, including ICT public access centres, masts in underserved areas and internet links to schools, and to the Uganda Communications Commission’s e-Booster programme for home-grown ICT solutions. Uganda is seeking re-election to the ITU Council for Africa (Region D); UCC Executive Director Nyombi Thembo is in the delegation. Coverage is not the same as use: UCC figures for December 2025 showed 47.1 million active mobile subscriptions but only 18.5 million active internet subscriptions.

Why it matters

The gap between near-universal coverage and far fewer internet subscriptions shows that Uganda’s challenge has moved from building networks to making internet use affordable.
