EU delays high-risk AI Act rules; two new bans apply from 2 December

27 Jul 2026 · deadline 2 Dec 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

The EU’s "Digital Omnibus on AI" (Regulation 2026/1744) entered into force on 27 July 2026. It moves the AI Act’s high-risk obligations for Annex III uses, such as employment, credit, education and biometrics, from August 2026 to 2 December 2027. Obligations for AI in regulated products move to 2 August 2028. Two new prohibitions, on non-consensual intimate deepfakes and child sexual abuse material, apply from 2 December 2026.

Why it matters

Public bodies that deploy AI in the EU, and suppliers selling to them, get more time for high-risk compliance, but the transparency and new prohibition deadlines this December still stand.
