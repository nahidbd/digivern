Benin moves biometric ID card renewals online

Sep–Oct 2026 · 2 sources · official sourcing

Benin’s identification agency ANIP now lets citizens apply for and track renewals of the national biometric ID card (CNIB) online, around the clock, through its e-services portal and the ANIP BJ app; the agency announced the change in September for International Identity Day, according to the state broadcaster SRTB. The card costs 6,000 FCFA and is valid for five years. Applications are still validated manually and cards are usually collected in person. Enrolment reportedly covers nearly 99% of the population.

Why it matters

Moving ID services online is a common next step once enrolment is near-universal.
