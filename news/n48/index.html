Bank of Uganda takes its RTGS and clearing systems offline for four days for an upgrade tied to East African cross-border payments

3 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

The Bank of Uganda said on 3 November that it will suspend the Real Time Gross Settlement system (the Uganda National Interbank Settlement System) and the Automated Clearing House from 5 pm on Thursday 5 November until 9 am on Monday 9 November 2026. The central bank describes a planned upgrade to improve the efficiency, reliability, resilience and interoperability of the national payments infrastructure, in line with the East African Community Cross-Border Payment System Masterplan. While the systems are down, high-value interbank transfers, bulk salary payments, direct debits and cheque clearing stop; banking sources told the Daily Monitor that mobile money, cards, ATMs and transfers within the same bank should keep working. The Monitor puts RTGS traffic at 215,831 transactions worth Shs79.6 trillion in May and ACH traffic at 1.45 million transactions worth Shs6.7 trillion in August.

Why it matters

Linking national settlement systems is the groundwork for cheaper payments across the East African Community; the four-day outage also tests how far Uganda’s economy can run on mobile money alone.
