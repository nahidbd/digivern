India launches SAMAVESH, one login for 33 social justice portals, and a new e-Anudaan system for NGO grants

24 Nov 2026 · 1 source · media sourcing

On 24 November 2026 India’s Minister of Social Justice and Empowerment, Dr Virendra Kumar, and Minister of State B. L. Verma launched two platforms in New Delhi. SAMAVESH (Single Access Mechanism for All Verticals of Empowerment and Social Harmony) brings 33 portals of the department and its bodies together behind a common login and a beneficiary dashboard, is linked to DigiLocker and the Public Financial Management System (PFMS), and has an AI chatbot, Samajik Sahayak, that works in 22 Indian languages through BHASHINI. The new e-Anudaan portal handles grant-in-aid for four schemes (NAPDDR, SMILE, AVYAY and SHRESHTA) with a common project ID, automatic checks against the NGO Darpan and UDISE+ databases, e-inspections and PFMS fund tracking. Secretary Sudhansh Pant said the number of application forms had been cut from 283 to 182. Agreements were also signed with BHASHINI and with the National Skill Development Corporation for PM-AJAY beneficiaries.

Why it matters

Joining dozens of welfare portals behind one login and checking grant applicants automatically against national databases is the kind of integration that makes social protection systems easier to use and harder to abuse.
