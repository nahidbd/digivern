Bangladesh Bank tells banks to warn exporters about cyber fraud using altered export documents and payment instructions

16 Nov 2026 · 2 sources · media sourcing

Bangladesh Bank’s Foreign Exchange Policy Department-1 issued a circular letter (FEPD-1 Circular Letter No. 22) on 16 November 2026 telling all authorised dealer banks to alert exporters to cyber fraud in business communications, The Financial Express and The Business Standard reported. The central bank pointed to media reports of growing risks such as manipulated export documents and altered payment instructions. Banks must pass on four precautions: send export documents directly to foreign buyers only through secure channels; check any change in bank details or payment instructions through contacts already on record; avoid suspicious links and unverified apps and never share passwords, PINs or one-time passwords; and report suspected fraud to the bank promptly. Banks are “directed to promptly communicate the central bank's guidelines to exporters through appropriate channels,” TBS reported. The circular sets no deadline or penalty.

Why it matters

Payment-instruction fraud hits export earnings directly; the circular makes banks responsible for warning exporters, but it is advice rather than new security requirements.
