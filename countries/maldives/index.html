Maldives: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-17 · UN E-Government Development Index: 94 of 193 · 0.675 · 2024

Lead agency
Maldives Digital Service (MDS), set up on 15 Jan 2026 under the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology (renamed Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology in Apr 2026), replacing the National Centre for Information Technology (NCIT); Digital Transformation Council chaired by the President (since Sep 2025)
Strategy
Maldives 2.0 Roadmap 2025–2028 (“Journey to Digital Transformation”), with MDS as the main executing body; Maldives 2.0 Digital Transformation Bill and Digital Identity Bill submitted 15 Jun 2026, accepted by Parliament and with its ICT Committee since early Jul 2026 (not yet passed as of Nov 2026)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityeFaas national digital identity and single sign-on (since 2012, now run by MDS) with liveness check and face match; about 272,000 enrolled, some 80% of people aged 10 and over (Dec 2025–Jan 2026); eFaas 2.0 announced for mid-2026; Digital Identity Bill (Maldives Digital Identity System) with Parliament’s ICT Committee; President said in Aug 2026 a chip-and-fingerprint smart ID card would come within 18 months, and the 2027 budget provides for itOperational
Digital paymentsFavara instant payment system of the Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA), launched 26 Aug 2023: about 5 million transactions worth US$972 million in its first year; national Pay MV QR code system being rolled out with Payment Maldives (MMA company set up Dec 2025); Favara–UPI link live since 30 Jul 2026 for outbound person-to-person transfers to India via Bank of Maldives and Maldives Islamic Bank (up to INR 24,000 a month); merchant QR phase still in preparationOperational
Government services portaloneGov portal (launched 10 Jul 2023 with 111 services, built by TradeNet under the Single Window initiative) with sign-in through eFaasOperational
Business registryCompany registration and changes offered online through oneGov by the Ministry of Economic Development, Transport and TradeOperational
Civil registration (CRVS)Births and deaths recorded in the GEMEN vital registration system of the Department of National Registration; about 98.2% of births and 100% of deaths registered within one year (2023), but no direct link to the national ID system (NARES), so data move manuallyPartial
Data exchangeNo national data exchange platform yet; the Maldives 2.0 Digital Transformation Bill (in committee) would create a Data Exchange Platform, authoritative registries and secure data-sharing between agenciesPartial
Data protectionNo law yet: Personal Data Protection Bill submitted 11 May 2026, accepted by Parliament (66–0) and sent to the ICT Committee in Jun 2026; public consultation 24 Jun–8 Jul 2026; would make the Information Commissioner the Privacy Commissioner, with fines of MVR 250,000–10 million; no committee report as of mid-Nov 2026Partial
CybersecurityNational Cyber Security Agency set up 18 Mar 2024 by presidential directive; Cyber Security Bill to give it a legal basis (five-member board, licensing of cybersecurity providers) accepted by Parliament on 15 Jun 2026 and in committee; computer crimes added to the Penal Code (passed Dec 2024)Partial
AI governanceNo AI policy or law yet; President Muizzu announced on 20 Apr 2026 a bill to regulate AI, to go to Parliament alongside the digital transformation, digital identity and cyber security bills; no AI bill found before Parliament as of Nov 2026Partial
