Rwanda: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-16 · UN E-Government Development Index: 118 of 193 · 0.580 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of ICT and Innovation (MINICT), with the Rwanda Information Society Authority (RISA) as implementing agency; National Identification Agency (NIDA) for digital ID; National Bank of Rwanda for payments; National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) for cybersecurity and data protection
Strategy
ICT Sector Strategic Plan 2024/25–2028/29 (under NST2 and Smart Rwanda Master Plan II); Rwanda Digital Acceleration Project (World Bank / AIIB), extended to June 2028 with a €17 million IDA top-up ratified Oct 2026; national DPI strategy unveiled 9 Mar 2026

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitySingle Digital ID (SDID), National Identification Agency (NIDA): enrolment began Oct 2025; more than 8 million people registered and more than 7 million photographed (Aug 2026); citizen participation in registration 76% (Oct 2026); physical ID cards to be phased out in June 2027, with full rollout targeted for 2027Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)CRVS digitisation 89% complete (Oct 2026), funded under the Rwanda Digital Acceleration ProjectPartial
Government services portalIremboGov: more than 240 public services, nearly 500,000 requests a month, about 80% completed fully online; named “Best Government Service in the World” (GovTech Prize, World Governments Summit, Feb 2026); 258 public services introduced or improved by Dec 2025Operational
Digital paymentseKash national instant payment system, run by the National Bank of Rwanda through the RSwitch national switch, live since 14 Jul 2026: bank and mobile-wallet transfers in all directions, fee capped at Rwf 20, limit Rwf 10 million; more than 10.5 million transactions worth over Rwf 960 billion by early Aug 2026; 68.5% of adults use a mobile wallet (2024)Operational
Business registryRwanda Development Board One Stop Center portal (onestopcentre.rdb.rw): business registration fully online since 5 Jan 2026Operational
Data exchangeNational data-sharing platform (RISA) not yet live: RISA tender for a Shared Government Data Hub and a Data Interoperability Platform for API-based exchange between agencies closed 5 May 2026 (24-month contract); funded by the €17 million IDA top-up ratified Oct 2026Partial
Data protectionLaw No. 058/2021 on the protection of personal data and privacy; supervisory authority: National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA), Data Protection Office launched 31 Mar 2022Law or policy
CybersecurityLaw No. 26/2017 establishing the National Cyber Security Authority (in force 3 Jul 2017); National Cyber Security Strategy 2024–2029 (NCSA, adopted 8 Aug 2024); further cybersecurity work funded by the 2026 World Bank top-upLaw or policy
AI governanceNational AI Policy, approved by Cabinet in April 2023 (about US$76.5 million over five years); Cabinet approved a National Artificial Intelligence Agency on 8 Jun 2026; its structure and timeline not yet publishedPartial
