Germany: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-27 · UN E-Government Development Index: 12 of 193 · 0.938 · 2024

Lead agency
Federal Ministry for Digital Transformation and State Modernisation (Bundesministerium für Digitales und Staatsmodernisierung, BMDS; minister Karsten Wildberger), created in spring 2025 and responsible for digitalising and modernising the state, cutting red tape, digital infrastructure and AI in administration; it commissions the national EUDI wallet, developed under the lead of the Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND). Federal Office of Administration (BVA) runs the National Once-Only Technical System (NOOTS) with the Federal IT Cooperation (FITKO); Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) for cybersecurity and NIS2 supervision; Federal Network Agency (Bundesnetzagentur) as default AI market surveillance authority; Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (BfDI); Deutsche Bundesbank for payments statistics and oversight; registry offices (Standesämter) of the municipalities for civil status
Strategy
Modernisation Agenda of the Federation (Modernisierungsagenda des Bundes), adopted by the Cabinet in early Oct 2025: more than 80 measures including 23 lever projects; by Oct 2026, 5 of the 23 lever projects and 7 measures were complete, 62 measures under way and 11 in preparation, and annual compliance costs had been cut by more than €10 billion. Federal Modernisation Agenda agreed with the Länder in Dec 2025 (more than 200 measures with deadlines, e.g. child benefit without application from 2027). A Deutschland-App bundling administrative services is in municipal testing from summer 2026, with rollout to citizens planned for 2027 [To verify: no separate current federal Digitalstrategie document found]

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational ID card with online (eID) function, offered since 2010: 35% of adults had activated it (Aug 2025); in an Apr 2026 Bitkom survey only 18% had it activated and knew their PIN, and a free PIN-reset letter has been available again since early Oct 2026. BundID (federal citizen account) logs in with the eID or an ELSTER certificate. Digital Identities Act (Digitale-Identitätengesetz, DIdG), supplementing the amended eIDAS Regulation: Cabinet May 2026, passed by the Bundestag on 15 Oct 2026, approved by the Bundesrat (reported 20 Nov 2026). It gives a mobile electronic identification means at assurance level 'high' and qualified e-signatures; use is voluntary. The state EUDI wallet 'd-you' (name unveiled Sep 2026) is to start on 2 Jan 2027, free of charge, with about 40 launch partners; the ID card comes first, the driving licence later. Open issues: BSI technical guideline TR-03189 only due end-2026 and the government may postpone the start over residual security risksPartial
Digital paymentsDeutsche Bundesbank statistics for 2025 (published 22 Jul 2026): about 680 million SEPA instant credit transfers (+102%) worth about €610 billion (+141%), 9.4% of electronically initiated transfers (4.9% in 2024); card payments about 14.5 billion (+11%, 43% of non-cash payments); about 1.3 million card terminals. Under the EU Instant Payments Regulation banks must receive instant payments since 9 Jan 2025 and send them since 9 Oct 2025, credited within 10 seconds, 24/7, at no more than the price of a standard transfer, with Verification of Payee. Wero (European Payments Initiative; founders in Germany include Sparkassen, Volksbanken and Deutsche Bank): 9 million registered users in Germany and 57 million in Europe (Aug 2026), online shopping since Nov 2025, in-store payments planned later in 2026; only about 7% use it activelyOperational
Data exchange / registry modernisationRegister Modernisation (Registermodernisierung) under the Register Modernisation Act published 6 Apr 2021: a uniform identification number (IDNr) under the Identification Number Act (IDNrG) links a person's records across 50 register types listed in its Annex 1. The NOOTS state treaty makes connection mandatory for these registers and for all OZG services; BVA runs NOOTS and the intermediary platform to the EU Once-Only Technical System. The Federal Employment Agency became the first register-keeping body to use the identity-data retrieval (IDA) procedure in regular operation (Jul 2026); a Data Protection Cockpit lets citizens see which data was exchanged. Binding connection deadlines are set per register type by the federal-state ministerial conferencesPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Births, marriages and deaths are recorded in electronic civil status registers kept by the local registry office (Standesamt) under the Civil Status Act (PStG §§ 61 f.); certificates can be applied for online (e.g. Berlin: €12 per birth certificate, free for social-insurance purposes, paid online and sent by post), but an electronic civil status certificate cannot yet be issued. From 2027 registry office, tax office and family benefits office are to exchange birth data directly so that child benefit is paid without an application (siblings first, first-born children from the end of the year)Partial
Business registryCommercial, partnership, cooperative and association registers searchable free of charge and without registration at handelsregister.de since 1 Aug 2022 (DiRUG, implementing EU Directive 2019/1151); annual financial statements filed with the Unternehmensregister instead of the Bundesanzeiger. Since 1 Aug 2022 a GmbH or UG can be formed online through a notarised video procedure (cash formations first, in-kind contributions from 2023) and register filings can be certified onlineOperational
Government services portalOnline Access Act amendment (OZG 2.0) in force since 24 Jul 2024 after mediation (both chambers agreed 14 Jun 2024): it dropped the original target of 575 services online by end-2022, gives citizens a legal claim to electronic access to federal services from 2029, requires business services to be digital-only by 2030 and makes end-to-end digital processing the federal standard. BundID is the central citizen account, to become a nationwide 'DeutschlandID': 4.9 million active accounts (11 Aug 2025, after deletion of inactive accounts), about 2 million logins a month and nearly 1,800 connected online services. A Deutschland-App for Germany's about 8,500 administrative services is planned for citizens in 2027Partial
Data protectionEU GDPR with the Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG); Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (BfDI). Activity report for 2025 (May 2026): 11,824 complaints and enquiries (close to the 2018 record of 11,912), 9,170 data-breach notifications, 129 supervisory measures and fines of €15 million and €30 million on a telecoms provider. Prof. Moritz Hennemann was elected BfDI by the Bundestag on 25 Jun 2026 (391 votes to 122), succeeding Louisa Specht-RiemenschneiderLaw or policy
CybersecurityNIS2 Implementation and Cybersecurity Strengthening Act, revising the BSI Act, promulgated 6 Dec 2025, about 15 months after the EU deadline: supervised entities rose from about 4,500 to about 29,500. Registration in the BSI portal opened 6 Jan 2026; after only about 11,500 had registered by the original 6 Mar 2026 deadline, BSI extended it to 31 Jul 2026 (18,500 registered by end-May); breaches of the registration duty can be fined up to €500,000Law or policy
AI governanceEU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) implemented by the AI Market Surveillance and Innovation Promotion Act (KI-MIG): Cabinet Feb 2026, passed by the Bundestag on 11 Jun 2026, Bundesrat did not call the mediation committee on 10 Jul 2026, in time for the AI Act rules applying from 2 Aug 2026. The Bundesnetzagentur is the default market surveillance and notifying authority and single point of contact, with a coordination centre (KoKIVO), an independent AI market surveillance chamber, a complaints office and at least one AI regulatory sandbox; BaFin covers AI in financial services and BSI supports on cybersecurity. Germany missed the Aug 2025 deadline for designating authorities; the EU Digital Omnibus postpones high-risk obligations to end-2027Law or policy
