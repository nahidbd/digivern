Canada: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-15 · UN E-Government Development Index: 47 of 193 · 0.845 · 2024

Lead agency
Digital Transformation Canada (DTC), launched 3 Sep 2026 under CEO Patrick Pichette and reporting to the Minister of Government Transformation, Public Works and Procurement, combines Shared Services Canada, the Canadian Digital Service and selected Treasury Board Secretariat and PSPC functions; the Treasury Board Secretariat’s Office of the Chief Information Officer sets policy on service, data, privacy, security and AI; Payments Canada and the Bank of Canada oversee payments and open banking; the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (part of CSE) leads on cyber
Strategy
Canada’s Digital Ambition 2024–25 (TBS, Feb 2025); AI Strategy for the Federal Public Service 2025–2027 (Jun 2025); national AI strategy announced Jun 2026; National Cyber Security Strategy (Feb 2025); Budget 2026 (5 Nov 2026) proposes about $1 billion over five years for a Government Transformation Fund and a Digital Transformation Canada Act

Digital paymentsReal-Time Rail instant payment system launched 9 Nov 2026 by Payments Canada with mandatory central fraud controls including confirmation of payee, starting with 8 participants and big banks expected in the following months; Lynx high-value system live since 1 Sep 2021; Interac handled about 1.6 billion e-Transfers and about 7 billion debit transactions in 2025Operational
Open bankingConsumer-Driven Banking Act passed in 2026 (phase 1: read-only data sharing; payment initiation later); Bank of Canada consultation on draft accreditation guidelines opened 2 Nov 2026, comments due 8 Dec 2026Law or policy
Civil registration (CRVS)Provincial and territorial vital statistics registries, reporting to Statistics Canada; 365,737 live births registered in 2024, up 3.7% on 2023 (preliminary)Operational
Business registryCorporations Canada and provincial registries; Multi-Jurisdictional Registry Access Service launched 29 Jun 2020 (BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba first) to connect all 14 registries; federal corporations must file individuals with significant control since 22 Jan 2024Operational
Government services portalCanada.ca and My Service Canada Account: one sign-in for Employment Insurance, the Canada Pension Plan, Old Age Security, the Canada Disability Benefit, the Canadian Dental Care Plan and student loans; Budget 2026 funds Benefits Delivery Modernization ($705 million over 6 years)Operational
Data protectionPrivacy Act (federal institutions) and PIPEDA (private sector), with substantially similar laws in Alberta, BC and Quebec; Bill C-36, Protecting Privacy and Consumer Data Act, tabled 15 Jun 2026 to replace PIPEDA (fines up to the greater of $10 million or 3% of global revenue), at second readingLaw or policy
CybersecurityNational Cyber Security Strategy “Securing Canada’s Digital Future” (6 Feb 2025, $37.8 million over 6 years); Critical Cyber Systems Protection Act (Bill C-8) received Royal Assent 15 Jun 2026, requiring cyber programmes from designated operatorsLaw or policy
AI governanceNo AI statute: the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act (Bill C-27) died on prorogation in Jan 2025; Minister of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Innovation Evan Solomon announced a national AI strategy in Jun 2026; the federal Directive on Automated Decision-Making governs government use of AIPartial
