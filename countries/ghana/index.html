Ghana: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-26 · UN E-Government Development Index: 108 of 193 · 0.632 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations (MoCDTI); the National Information Technology Agency (NITA), the Data Protection Commission and the Cyber Security Authority implement the AI strategy; the National Identification Authority (NIA) runs the Ghana Card
Strategy
National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2025–2035 (Cabinet approval Feb 2026, launched 24 Apr 2026); Ghana Digital Acceleration Project (World Bank IDA, US$200 million, approved Apr 2022, effective Jun 2023, to Jun 2027); One Million Coders Programme

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityGhana Card, National Identification Authority (NIA): 17,918,135 people registered and 17,133,993 cards issued between Apr 2019 and 15 Mar 2024; over 18 million registered by Jun 2025, with 822,440 printed cards not yet collected; registration of children aged 6–14 resumed on 5 May 2026, targeting about 3.1 million children. L.I. 2523, the National Identity Register (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, in force since 9 Jun 2026, makes biometric verification through the NIA Identity Verification System Platform compulsory and bans verifying the card by sight, photocopy or scan; the grace period ended on 2 Nov 2026 and an NIA task force began enforcement on 3 Nov 2026, with fines of 500–2,000 penalty units (up to GH¢24,000) and possible removal from the platform; the NIA has built a yes-or-no verification app for individuals and small businesses (Nov 2026)Operational
Digital paymentsBank of Ghana oversight. Mobile money: about 1,046 million transactions worth GH¢518.8 billion in Aug 2026 (value up 47% year on year), while GhIPSS Instant Pay carried GH¢95.1 billion in the same month (up 61%); 26.4 million active accounts (85.8 million registered) and about 546,000 active agents (Jun 2026); mobile money interoperability carried 33.5 million transactions worth GH¢6.2 billion in Jun 2026. GhIPSS Instant Pay handled 202.8 million transactions worth GH¢711.96 billion in 2025; GhanaPay had 1.45 million clients. Ghana kept first place in the GSMA Mobile Money Regulatory Index 2025. The e-Cedi pilot is complete and cross-border and wholesale uses are being designed (May 2026); Virtual Assets Act, 2025 (Act 1154)Operational
Data exchangeNational Data Exchange Hub under the Ghana Digital Acceleration Project at international review stage, alongside modernisation of the national data centres in Accra and Kumasi (Sep 2026); planned to connect 48 government data registries (Nov 2026)Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)Births and Deaths Registry: “Ghana Card Number at Birth” (launched 14 Mar 2024) links health-facility birth notifications to birth registration and the NIA; about 6 million of more than 30 million paper records digitised by Jul 2023; UNICEF says only about 61% of children have birth certificates (Nov 2026)Partial
Business registryOffice of the Registrar of Companies online portal temporarily disrupted from 26 Mar 2026 after an audit found unauthorised use of access points; requests for company details handled in writing within three working days. Government services portalGhana.gov: about 1,500–2,000 services (Jul 2025), with a target of 16,000 services under the Ghana Digital Acceleration Project; Ghana Card used for authentication; to be upgraded into a single integrated gateway for public services (Nov 2026)Partial
Data protectionData Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), supervised by the Data Protection Commission, which declared 2026 a “year of enforcement” with more inspections; a new Data Protection Bill to strengthen enforcement and cover AI, automated decisions and cross-border data transfers announced by the minister in Feb–Mar 2026 and still being draftedLaw or policy
CybersecurityCybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038) established the Cyber Security Authority; draft Cybersecurity (Amendment) Bill, 2025, with about 50 changes including investigation powers and cyber-hygiene certification, was consulted on from 1 Oct to 14 Nov 2025 and has not been enactedLaw or policy
