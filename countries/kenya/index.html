Kenya: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-16 · UN E-Government Development Index: 109 of 193 · 0.631 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy (MICDE), with the ICT Authority (ICTA), which runs eCitizen and sets interoperability standards; State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services (Ministry of Interior) for identity and civil registration; Central Bank of Kenya for payments; Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC); Communications Authority of Kenya (National KE-CIRT/CC) and the new National Cybersecurity Agency for cybersecurity
Strategy
Kenya National Digital Master Plan 2022–2032 (launched Apr 2022, 19 flagship projects); Kenya Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2025–2030 (launched 27 Mar 2025); Government Interoperability Framework, 3rd edition (ICTA, Feb 2026)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityMaisha Namba, a lifelong unique identifier, and the chip-based Maisha Card, issued since 1 Nov 2023 (first to people turning 18 and replacements), voluntary, with the second-generation ID still valid; 972,630 Maisha Cards issued by Sep 2024; High Court lifted an order blocking the rollout in Feb 2024 while the Katiba Institute petition continues; Social Health Authority campaign (Jun 2026) to give dependants aged 7–17 a Maisha Namba linked to health records; more than 1.5 million ID cards produced Jan–May 2026, target 3 million by end-2026Partial
Government services portaleCitizen single gateway run by the ICT Authority (transfer from private vendors began 2022/23): 22,510 services from 583 agencies (Dec 2025), more than 26,000 (Jun 2026); daily collections up from about Sh50 million to Sh600 million; but only about 12% of some 6,000 government services are fully digital end to end and 58% still mainly manual (CS Kabogo, Jun 2026)Operational
Service centres (Huduma)Huduma Centres offer over 200 government services in person; 59th centre opened 4 Jul 2025 (Mogotio); 14.12 million in-person visits in the year to Jun 2025, up from 13.5 millionOperational
Digital paymentsCentral Bank of Kenya oversight of mobile money (including M-Pesa): 97.04 million registered mobile money accounts and 568,518 active agents (Sep 2026); draft National Payment System Bill 2026 (CBK and National Treasury, published 22 Sep 2026, comments to 9 Oct 2026) would replace the 2011 Act, let CBK order interoperability, add payment-initiation and account-information licences with consent-based open finance, and floats a national instant payment switchOperational
Civil registration (CRVS)Civil Registration Services (Ministry of Interior): birth registration completeness 70.3% and death registration 44.8% in 2024 (down from 77.1% and 45.1% in 2023); more than 42 million vital records digitised in 2025/26; digital birth e-notification pilot from Apr 2026 (Nairobi Hospital first) issues a Unique Personal Identifier later linked to Maisha NambaPartial
Business registryBusiness Registration Service (BRS): automated system for director appointments, resignations and share transfers launched 13 Jan 2026, with OTP verification and a consent-first rule for directorsOperational
Data exchangeICT Authority Government Interoperability Framework (3rd edition, Feb 2026) defines a federated Government Integration Platform as the interoperability backbone for ministries, counties, departments and agencies, on a phased roadmap; only about 12% of government services fully integrated across systems (Jun 2026)Partial
Data protectionData Protection Act No. 24 of 2019 (assent 8 Nov 2019, in force 25 Nov 2019) established the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner; by Jan 2026 the ODPC had received 9,061 complaints, issued 357 determinations, 184 compensation orders, 134 enforcement notices and 20 penalty notices, and registered more than 15,000 data controllers and processorsLaw or policy
CybersecurityComputer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Cap. 79C), amended by the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes (Amendment) Act 2025 (assent 15 Oct 2025, in force 4 Nov 2025): blocking of unlawful sites, identity-theft offence, court-ordered takedowns; National Cybersecurity Agency Order 2026 (Legal Notice 89, under the State Corporations Act), approved by Parliament Jun 2026, creates a central cyber regulator; National KE-CIRT/CC (Communications Authority, since 2014) detected about 3.37 billion threat events in Jan–Mar 2026 and about 2.7 billion in Jul–Sep 2026Law or policy
AI governanceKenya Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2025–2030, launched 27 Mar 2025 at KICC by MICDE (CS William Kabogo): three pillars (AI digital infrastructure, data and AI governance, research, innovation and commercialisation); developed with EU and German (BMZ/GIZ) supportPartial
Digital healthDigital Health Act No. 15 of 2023 (assent 19 Oct 2023, in force 2 Nov 2023) established the Digital Health Agency as custodian of health data, running the Comprehensive Integrated Health Information System and client, facility, provider and product registriesLaw or policy
