Israel: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-18 · UN E-Government Development Index: 23 of 193 · 0.901 · 2024

Lead agency
National Digital Agency (Israel Digital), created in 2021 by merging the Government ICT Authority and the Digital Israel national initiative; it runs the government identification system and the myGov personal and business areas; Population and Immigration Authority (population registry, ID cards, digital birth certificates); Bank of Israel, whose Payment and Settlement Systems Department runs the Zahav RTGS system and oversees Masav and the other payment systems; Privacy Protection Authority (statutory independent status under Amendment 13 since 14 Aug 2025); Israel National Cyber Directorate (INCD, head Yossi Karadi), which also led the 2026 draft digital identity strategy; National Artificial Intelligence Directorate in the Prime Minister's Office (head Brig. Gen. (res.) Erez Askal, launched Aug 2026); Israel Corporations Authority / Registrar of Companies
Strategy
No single current national digital strategy was found; policy runs through government resolutions and plans: the Ask Once policy (Government Resolution 1933, Aug 2016) and the digital-services acceleration in Resolution 260 (Jul 2020); the National Digital Agency's annual government ICT workplan (2024 priorities: digital services, cloud migration through Project Nimbus, data and AI; NIS 5.77 billion planned government ICT spending in 2024); a draft national digital identification strategy published 9 Nov 2026 by the INCD with the National Digital Agency, the Population and Immigration Authority, the Finance Ministry and the Bank of Israel, built around a national digital identity wallet, with public comments until 30 Nov 2026 and a five-year implementation plan to follow; and a national AI plan approved by the government in 2026, after Government Decision 3375 (Sep 2025) adopted the Nagel Committee's call for an AI directorate in the Prime Minister's Office

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityGovernment identification system (launched 2019): about 4.6 million registered citizens at end-2024 (State Comptroller, May 2026), about 5.5 million by Nov 2026 (draft strategy as reported); only 16% of mapped government services (650 of about 4,000) and 15 of 258 local authorities were connected, and the Tax Authority, National Insurance Institute, Defense Ministry and Employment Service run separate log-ins; the draft national digital identification strategy (9 Nov 2026) proposes a national digital identity wallet developed by the National Digital Agency for the ID card, driving licence and other official documents, legislation to give the digital ID the same legal standing as the physical card, selective disclosure, and voluntary, staged use of the government log-in by banks, insurers, health and communications providers for customer identification; physical ID cards stay; comments close 30 Nov 2026Partial
Government services portalgov.il with the myGov personal and business areas, organised by life events; the State Comptroller (May 2026) found online-service policy 'partial and slow' despite resolutions since 2014: only 233 government services (about 3%) available through the personal area, about 23% of identified online forms managed through the identification system, and many forms still paper-only (89% at the Foreign Ministry, 79% at the rabbinical courts); the National Digital Agency says it has implemented most recommendationsPartial
Digital paymentsBank of Israel's Zahav RTGS (since 2007) completed migration to ISO 20022 on 30 Jun 2025; Masav, the automated clearing house, runs a faster-payments service crediting beneficiaries within 5–7 seconds; the Bank has opened the payment systems to nonbank firms, and three more (GAMA, Max-IT Finance, Rapyd) joined Masav or the SHVA card system in Apr 2026; payment cards worth NIS 451 billion in 2023, checks about NIS 640 billion (about 5% of activity) in 2025, with mobile deposit of future-dated checks from Sep 2026; digital shekel (CBDC) still under study after a Mar 2025 preliminary design, with a recommendation on issuance due to the Governor by end-2026Operational
Data exchangeAsk Once policy (Resolution 1933, 2016) with the central 'Information Highway' for data transfer between ministries and the Moed and newer Cheeta request systems; the State Comptroller's follow-up audit (Nov 2024) found 2,742 of 3,888 mapped services (70%) validated, but data-transfer requests took 342 days on average in 2023 against a 60-day target, and the IDF, Tax Authority, National Insurance Institute and Defense Ministry were not connected to the Information Highway; a May-2026 Comptroller audit again noted prolonged approvals and fragmented systemsPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Population registry kept by the Population and Immigration Authority; since Jun 2023 parents of newborns can receive a digitally signed birth certificate by email or app without visiting an officeOperational
Business registryRegistrar of Companies (Israel Corporations Authority): since 27 Jun 2024 every company must file reports and applications online only ('Corporations Online'), under Amendment 2 to the Digital Communications with Public Bodies Law and Amendment 35 to the Companies Law (Jun 2022); every company must register an official digital address with the Registrar (new companies since 27 Jan 2023)Operational
Data protectionProtection of Privacy Law 1981, with Amendment 13 (5784-2024) in force 14 Aug 2025, the largest reform since 1981: statutory independent status for the Privacy Protection Authority, administrative fines (e.g. NIS 320,000 per breach, up to NIS 640,000 for very large databases, capped at 5% of revenue), mandatory data protection officers for public bodies and large processors such as banks, insurers and hospitals, and statutory damages of up to NIS 10,000 without proof of harm; the EU affirmed Israel's adequacy status in Jan 2024Law or policy
CybersecurityNational Cyber Defense Bill 5786-2026 passed first reading in the Knesset on 8 Jun 2026 (not yet law): duties for all organisations and baseline controls for 'essential organisations', including government bodies, mandatory reporting of significant attacks to the INCD, and fines of NIS 640,000; INCD 2025 report: 26,500 reports to its 119 hotline (+55%) and 2,304 targeted alerts to organisations (up nearly 350%), peaking in Jun 2025; the State Comptroller (May 2026) found weaknesses at the Foreign and Housing ministries and that about 31 ministries kept using a vulnerable remote-work tool for ten months after an INCD warning, until Jan 2025Partial
AI governanceGovernment Decision 3375 (Sep 2025) set up a National AI Directorate in the Prime Minister's Office, launched in Aug 2026 with a 2026 budget of NIS 3 billion and a goal of buying up to 100,000 Nvidia processors; the government approved a national AI plan in 2026 (sovereign AI infrastructure, a national quantum computer, a National AI Institute, AI in citizen services); no AI-specific law: rules were being drafted separately by the Privacy Protection Authority, the INCD and the Ministry of Justice (Globes, Jul 2025); a State Comptroller audit (Jun 2026) found 68% of 144 public-sector AI projects still in development or pilot and 58% of bodies without an AI budgetPartial
ConnectivityOver 90% broadband coverage nationwide and 63% of the population using fibre-based internet at end-2024, with on average more than two fibre networks per household (Israel's statement to ITU WTDC-25, Nov 2025); 5G pilots funded by the Ministry of Communications and the Innovation AuthorityOperational
