India: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-12 · UN E-Government Development Index: 97 of 193 · 0.668 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
Strategy
Digital India; IndiaAI Mission (approved by Cabinet 7 Mar 2024, ₹10,371.92 crore)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityAadhaar, Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI): 145.32 crore (1.45 billion) Aadhaar numbers generated (UIDAI enrolment dashboard, last updated 28 Sep 2026)Operational
Digital paymentsUnified Payments Interface (UPI), National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI): record 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October 2026, up 23% in volume and 16% in value year on year (NPCI data)Operational
Data exchangeAccount Aggregator framework, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India since 2016: 17 licensed aggregators, 284.6 million accounts linked and over 2.88 billion financial accounts able to share data with the customer's consent (Department of Financial Services, 31 Mar 2026)Operational
Document wallet and services appDigiLocker: over 72.43 crore registered users and 5,437 document types; UMANG app: 11.66 crore users and about 2,575 services (MeitY reply in Rajya Sabha, Aug 2026, services as of 31 Jul 2026)Operational
Data protectionDigital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; DPDP Rules 2025 notified Nov 2025, phased in over up to 18 months; Data Protection Board of India to work as a fully digital officePartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act 2023, in force 1 Oct 2023: all reported births and deaths registered digitally on the central Civil Registration System (CRS) portal; digital birth certificate usable as a single document for admissions, Aadhaar, passports and more; CRS mobile app launched by the Home Ministry (29 Oct 2024)Operational
Business registryMCA21 V3, Ministry of Corporate Affairs: LLP filings moved to V3 from Mar 2022; the final 38 company forms followed on 14 Jul 2025, completing the move from V2Operational
Agriculture registryAgriStack Farmer IDs, Ministry of Agriculture: 11 crore (110 million) target reached, announced 24 Oct 2026, five months ahead of the 31 Mar 2027 deadlinePartial
CybersecurityCERT-In, national nodal agency under section 70B of the IT Act: over 29.44 lakh incidents handled in 2025; RBI Master Directions 2026 on cyber resilience and digital payment security for non-bank payment system operators (5 Nov 2026): incidents to be reported to the RBI within six hours of detectionOperational
AI governanceIndia AI Governance Guidelines (MeitY, 5 Nov 2025): seven principles ('sutras'), an AI Governance Group supported by a technology and policy expert committee and the AI Safety Institute; guidelines, not a lawPartial
