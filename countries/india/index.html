India: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-25 · UN E-Government Development Index: 97 of 193 · 0.668 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
Strategy
Digital India; IndiaAI Mission (approved by Cabinet 7 Mar 2024, ₹10,371.92 crore)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityAadhaar, Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI): 145.69 crore (about 1.46 billion) Aadhaar numbers generated (UIDAI enrolment dashboard, content last updated 11 Nov 2026; page reviewed 20 Nov 2026)Operational
Digital paymentsUnified Payments Interface (UPI), National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI): record 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October 2026, up 23% in volume and 16% in value year on year; average daily value passed ₹1 lakh crore for the first time (NPCI data released 1 Nov 2026; November figures not yet published at review)Operational
Data exchangeAccount Aggregator framework, regulated by the Reserve Bank of India since 2016: 17 licensed aggregators, 284.6 million accounts linked and over 2.88 billion financial accounts able to share data with the customer's consent (Department of Financial Services, 31 Mar 2026)Operational
Government services app and document walletDigiLocker: over 72.43 crore registered users and 5,437 document types; UMANG app: 11.66 crore users and about 2,575 services (MeitY reply in Rajya Sabha, Aug 2026, services as of 31 Jul 2026)Operational
Data protectionDigital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; DPDP Rules 2025 notified 14 Nov 2025 with an 18-month phased rollout. Some provisions applied on notification; the consent manager registration framework took effect on 13 Nov 2026, with applications made online to the Data Protection Board of India (Indian-incorporated firms, minimum net worth ₹2 crore); the main obligations on data fiduciaries apply from 13 May 2027. The Board works as a fully digital office, with appeals to TDSATPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act 2023, in force 1 Oct 2023: all reported births and deaths registered digitally on the central Civil Registration System (CRS) portal; digital birth certificate usable as a single document for admissions, Aadhaar, passports and more; CRS mobile app launched by the Home Ministry (29 Oct 2024)Operational
Business registryMCA21 V3, Ministry of Corporate Affairs: LLP filings moved to V3 from Mar 2022; the final 38 company forms followed on 14 Jul 2025, completing the move from V2Operational
Agriculture registryAgriStack Farmer IDs, Ministry of Agriculture: 11 crore (110 million) target reached, announced 24 Oct 2026, five months ahead of the 31 Mar 2027 deadlinePartial
CybersecurityCERT-In, national nodal agency under section 70B of the IT Act: about 29.44 lakh incidents handled in 2025. RBI Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank payment system operators (5 Nov 2026): incidents to be reported to the RBI within six hours of detection (and to CERT-In); in force at once for large and medium operators, from 1 Apr 2028 for small ones. On 23 Nov 2026 the RBI issued draft amendments on preventive safeguards for electronic banking transactions, giving customers more control over how far digital channels can be used and widening what counts as bank negligence in fraud cases (comments until 14 Dec 2026)Operational
AI governanceIndia AI Governance Guidelines (MeitY, 5 Nov 2025): seven principles ('sutras'), an AI Governance Group supported by a technology and policy expert committee and the AI Safety Institute; guidelines, not a law. Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules 2026, notified 10 Feb 2026 and in force 20 Feb 2026: platforms must clearly label synthetically generated (AI) content and embed permanent metadata, users must declare AI content, and certain takedown orders must be met within 3 hours (down from 36). IndiaAI Mission: over 38,000 GPUs available at about ₹65 an hour (Feb 2026); by Oct 2026 ₹1,275 crore of compute subsidy had been committed, about 83% of it to nine foundation-model builders. On 8 Oct 2026 the IT minister said a regulation-focused consultation paper on AI, covering deepfakes and safety, would follow within about a monthPartial
