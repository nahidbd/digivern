Jordan: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-22 · UN E-Government Development Index: 89 of 193 · 0.685 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship (MoDEE; minister Sami Smeirat), which runs the Sanad app and portal and the Personal Data Protection Directorate; National Cybersecurity Center (under Prime Ministry oversight) for cyber security
Strategy
National Digital Transformation Strategy 2026–2028 (five pillars: digital infrastructure, connected government services, digital identity and e-signatures, privacy and trust, digital inclusion); Jordanian AI Strategy and Implementation Plan 2023–2027; Economic Modernisation Vision executive programme (half-yearly progress reports) [To verify: dates of the second executive programme, 2026–2029]

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitySanad digital ID: more than 3 million activated, over 1 million of them in the first eight months of 2026 (MoDEE, 2026), up from 2.6 million in May 2026; the activated digital ID is needed to apply online for the e-passport (pilot since 1 Sep 2025)Operational
Legal status of digital IDAmended Civil Status Law 2026 (Article 39), published in the Official Gazette: digital IDs in apps approved by MoDEE have the same legal validity as the physical national ID card for all proceduresLaw or policy
Digital paymentsJoPACC via Petra: CliQ instant payments, Jan–Aug 2026, 162.18 million transactions worth JD16.64 billion; record August (24 million transfers, JD2.43 billion; 2.39 million users). E-wallets: 2.97 million users at end May 2026; outgoing e-wallet transfers of JD3.09 billion in 63.7 million transactions, Jan–May 2026Operational
Data exchangeNo named national data-exchange platform identified; ESCWA’s 2025 GEMS index gave Jordan 97% for interoperability among government institutions and cited systems integration and data exchange as progress [To verify: name and coverage of the government integration platform]Partial
Civil registrationCivil Status and Passports Department: 8,907,356 Jordanian citizens registered (2025); since 1 May 2025, birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates for registered events are issued free as QR-verifiable PDFs via Sanad (about 100,000 by Jul 2025)Operational
Government services portalSanad app and portal: 85.8% of government services digitised by end H1 2026 (82.5%, 1,980 services, at end Q1); in H1 2026 Sanad added 16 services and improved 13; ESCWA GEMS 2025 score 71% (63% in 2024), the highest in four yearsOperational
Business registryCompanies Control Department: electronic company registrations rose 18.7% in 2025; 493,751 e-applications completed and 96,367 company information records issued electronically without human interventionOperational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, in force since 17 Mar 2024 with a 12-month compliance period for existing data; Personal Data Protection Council and Directorate at MoDEE; Cabinet approved the Data Subject Rights Regulation in Aug 2025 (consent per purpose, objections and complaints procedures)Law or policy
CybersecurityCybersecurity Law No. 16 of 2019 created the National Cybersecurity Council and National Cybersecurity Center; ten critical infrastructure sectors designated (Mar 2025); Q1 2026: locally recorded incidents down 16% year on year, 0.5% critical, with government the second most affected sector (20.9%)Operational
AI governanceJordanian AI Strategy and Implementation Plan 2023–2027 (five goals, 68 projects, 12 priority sectors) and National AI Code of Ethics (2022); 15 projects completed and 23 in progress by Apr 2025; ranked 49th of 135 in the 2026 Global Index on Responsible AI; MoU with Saudi Arabia’s HUMAIN on Arabic AI models and government AI adoption (LEAP 2026)Partial
