Jordan: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-11 · UN E-Government Development Index: 89 of 193 · 0.685 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship (MoDEE), with the Prime Ministry’s Digital Transformation Unit
Strategy
National Digital Transformation Strategy 2026–2028; Economic Modernization Vision, second executive programme 2026–2029

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitySanad digital ID: more than 2.6 million citizens had activated a digital ID on the Sanad app by May 2026, over 600,000 of them since the start of 2026 (Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship)Operational
Legal status of digital IDAmended Civil Status Law 2026 (Article 39), published in the Official Gazette: digital IDs in apps approved by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship have the same legal validity as the physical national ID card; the Civil Status and Passports Department says public and private institutions will have to accept itLaw or policy
Digital paymentsJordan Payments and Clearing Company (JoPACC), 2025: CliQ instant payments 167.92 million transactions worth JOD 19.97 billion; JoMoPay mobile wallets 102.04 million transactions worth JOD 6.34 billion; eFAWATEERcom bill payments 75.43 million transactions worth JOD 15.99 billionOperational
Government services portalSanad app and portal; 85.8% of government services digitised by the end of the first half of 2026, when Sanad added 16 services and improved 13 (progress report on the Economic Modernization Vision executive programme)Operational
Business registryCompanies Control Department e-registration since late 2020: 39,766 companies registered online; 3,753 new companies in January–June 2026, up 7%Operational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023, in force since 17 March 2024 with a 12-month compliance period for existing data; a Personal Data Protection Council and a Personal Data Protection Directorate at the Ministry of Digital Economy and EntrepreneurshipPartial
CybersecurityNational Cybersecurity Center under Cybersecurity Law No. 16 of 2019; ten critical infrastructure sectors designated, including government services, finance and banking, ICT, energy and healthOperational
AI governanceJordanian AI Strategy and Implementation Plan 2023–2027: five goals and 68 projects across 12 priority sectors; National AI Code of Ethics published by the ministryPartial
