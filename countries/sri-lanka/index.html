Sri Lanka: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-14 · UN E-Government Development Index: 98 of 193 · 0.667 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Digital Economy (portfolio held by the President); GovTech Sri Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, the state-owned lead implementation agency (CEO appointed from 19 Aug 2026), replacing ICTA, which Cabinet ordered wound down (liquidation under way Apr 2026); a Digital Economy Authority is planned for policy (bill promised for Q1 2027 in the 2027 Budget); Department for Registration of Persons owns SL-UDI; Data Protection Authority; Sri Lanka CERT
Strategy
Digital Sri Lanka 2030, the National Digital Economy Strategy (unveiled Apr 2024; Phase 2 from 2026); Sri Lanka Digital Transformation Project (World Bank, US$50 million, approved 19 Dec 2025, financing cleared by Cabinet Feb 2026, implemented by GovTech); Cabinet approved drafting a Digital Economy Bill in Jun 2026; 2026 Budget allocates Rs 25.5 billion to the digital economy; 2027 Budget (presented 12 Nov 2026) lists digital transformation as a priority, with Appropriation Bill digital-economy spending of Rs 26.0 billion for 2027 and a Digital Economy Authority Bill due in Q1 2027

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitySri Lanka Unique Digital Identity (SL-UDI), built on MOSIP, owned by the Department for Registration of Persons with GovTech technical support; an Indian Master Systems Integrator would build the platform and a Sri Lankan managed service provider run it. India’s INR 3 billion grant covers the first phase, but bids exceeded it and about another INR 3 billion is needed; Infosys is the lowest bidder, but selection has been delayed by legal challenges and the grant was not finalised (Nov 2026). The Ministry of Digital Economy is instead building an interim minimum viable product, initially for government employees, on existing locally developed software and issued a request for quotations for biometric devices on 14 Oct 2026, prompting concerns about double spending. 16-year-olds getting their first ID will receive an e-NIC carrying the SL-UDI number; full implementation targeted for Aug 2027; about 17 million NIC holders to migrate eventuallyPartial
Digital paymentsCentral Bank of Sri Lanka oversight. LankaPay CEFTS instant transfers: 78.56 million transactions (+28.3% year on year) worth Rs 6,939 billion (+25.4%) in Q2 2026. LANKAQR national QR standard: 414,770 transactions (+39.3%) worth Rs 1.68 billion (+48.5%) in Q2 2026. 12 licensed customer-account-based mobile payment operators (Q2 2026). Service charges on government e-payments waived from Jan 2026Operational
Government paymentsGovPay, run by LankaPay with GovTech under the Central Bank: launched Feb 2025; 259 institutions and over 3,800 services (Feb 2026); over 300 institutions and more than Rs 5 billion processed (Sep 2026)Operational
Data exchangeNational Data Exchange (NDX) to link state databases (digital ID, tax, public registries): Cabinet paper to set it up submitted May 2026; NDX and the SL-UDI eKYC platform used for prototypes at the GovTech Sandbox Build Day (Sep 2026); secure cross-agency data sharing funded under the World Bank Digital Transformation ProjectPartial
Government services portalSingle “Super App” for state services: first procurement phase complete and moving into development (Feb 2026); single-window portal still being developed (Aug 2026); integrated citizen service portal and digital locker funded by the World Bank projectPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)e-BMD, Registrar General’s Department: certified birth, marriage and death copies ordered online, with copies from any district at any Divisional Secretariat since 2019; about 36 million scanned recordsOperational
Business registryeROC online company registration, Registrar of Companies (ICTA project, operational acceptance Jun 2021; one-stop business, tax and EPF registration); major outages in July 2025. Beneficial ownership disclosure mandatory from 30 Mar 2026 under the Companies (Amendment) Act No. 12 of 2025, with changes reported through eROCOperational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, amended by Act No. 22 of 2025; Data Protection Authority operating since 2023. Gazette Extraordinary No. 2498/16 (22 Jul 2026) brings the core controller and processor obligations (sections 2–3, Parts I and III) into force on 1 Jan 2027; data-subject rights (Part II), solicited messages (Part IV) and administrative penalties (Part VII) deferred to a later date. DPA Circular No. 01/2026 (2 Aug 2026) requires ministries and government departments to appoint data protection officers and start compliance programmesLaw or policy
CybersecuritySri Lanka CERT; no cybersecurity act yet. Cabinet approved a bill to create a National Cyber Security Regulatory Authority (Apr 2026); in Aug 2026 the draft was about 90% complete, with the Legal Draftsman before going to the Attorney General, and had not been gazetted. Government promoting an AI-powered National Cybersecurity Framework (May 2026). Online Safety Act (2024) in force; Cabinet appointed a committee to recommend amendments (May 2025), and a private member’s bill to repeal it was presented on 8 May 2025Partial
AI governanceDraft National Strategy on AI (“AI Sri Lanka 2028”) published for consultation in Jul 2024 and not formally adopted; ICTA tasked (Jun 2025) with formulating a National AI and Data Strategy, to move to GovTech and the Digital Economy Authority; AI is a focus of the planned Digital Economy Bill (Jun 2026); Sri Lanka AI Week held 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026Partial
