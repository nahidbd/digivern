Estonia: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-17 · UN E-Government Development Index: 2 of 193 · 0.973 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs (digital society, e-government and cybersecurity since 1 Jan 2025), with the Information System Authority (RIA) running X-tee, eID, the eesti.ee state portal and the EUDI wallet project; RIA is also the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-EE), with CERT-EE handling incidents
Strategy
Estonian Digital Society Development Plan 2035 (Digital Agenda 2035), approved by the government on 7 May 2026: 2026–2035, about €1.78 billion for the Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs’ area; succeeds the Digital Agenda 2030 drawn up in 2021

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational eID: about 1.2 million valid ID cards and 219,000 Mobile-ID users (RIA, Jul 2026); Smart-ID (private, SK ID Solutions) rated assurance level high by an RIA-convened expert group; Eesti app proof-of-identity feature since 7 Jul 2025Operational
EU digital identity walletRIA launched an international tender for a wallet developer and five-year service provider on 18 May 2026 (applications closed 29 Jun 2026); no provider announced yet and RIA expects contracting to take about a year; wallet will be voluntary and free; public-sector e-services must accept EU wallets from end-2026, larger private providers from end-2027Partial
Data exchangeX-tee (X-Road), launched in 2000 and maintained by RIA; core software developed by the Nordic Institute for Interoperability Solutions (NIIS), owned by Estonia, Finland, Iceland and the Faroe IslandsOperational
Government services portaleesti.ee state portal run by RIA: 7.4 million visits from about 700,000 people in 2025, 51% on mobile; Digital Agenda 2035 makes eesti.ee and the Eesti app the state’s central digital gateway by 2028Operational
Civil registration (CRVS)Population register kept by the Ministry of the Interior (SMIT as authorised processor) under the Population Register Act; people see their data online through the e-population register and eesti.eeOperational
Business registrye-Business Register (RIK) with an online Company Registration Portal since 2007: over 85% of new private limited companies registered and about 99% of annual reports filed through itOperational
Digital paymentsPart of the euro area and SEPA; 83% of card payments contactless (Eesti Pank, Apr 2026)Operational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Act (passed by the Riigikogu 12 Dec 2018) implementing the GDPR; Data Protection Inspectorate (AKI) as supervisory authorityLaw or policy
CybersecurityCybersecurity Act; NIS2 amendments in force since 1 Jan 2026, widening coverage from about 3,500 to nearly 6,500 organisations, with RIA as competent authority and National Cyber Security Centre; Cybersecurity Strategy 2024–2030; record 10,185 incidents with impact in 2025 (6,515 in 2024)Law or policy
AI governanceEU AI Act applies; Digital Agenda 2035 targets AI use by 75% of businesses by 2030 (90% by 2035) and €425 million in public-sector savings; Eesti.ai initiative launched 27 Jan 2026; government backed ‘AI ID codes’ for AI agents on 17 Jun 2026 (no legislation yet); AI Leap (TI-Hüpe) in schools since 1 Sep 2025Partial
