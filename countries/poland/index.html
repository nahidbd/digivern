Poland: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-17 · UN E-Government Development Index: 37 of 193 · 0.865 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Digital Affairs (Ministerstwo Cyfryzacji), with the Centralny Ośrodek Informatyki (COI), whose infrastructure carries ePUAP and which led its restoration after the Nov 2026 cyberattack; National Bank of Poland (oversight of payment systems, including KIR's Elixir and Express Elixir, and BLIK); President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO); CERT Polska within the national cybersecurity system under the KSC Act; Komisja Rozwoju i Bezpieczeństwa Sztucznej Inteligencji (KRiBSI), the AI market supervisor created in 2026; the courts' KRS business register
Strategy
Strategia Cyfryzacji Państwa (State Digitalisation Strategy) to 2035, adopted by Council of Ministers Resolution No. 130 of 9 Jun 2026 (Monitor Polski 2026 poz. 620); replaces the Integrated State Informatisation Programme; about PLN 100 billion of funding needed; targets all key public services fully online and 100% of households within reach of fibre and 5G by 2030, and 85% of citizens with basic digital skills, 80% of public offices using AI and digital spending at 5% of GDP by 2035. Draft AI Development Policy to 2030 (Ministry of Digital Affairs, Apr 2026) not yet adopted

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitymObywatel app: about 12 million users (May 2026, up from 5.7 million at end-2023), about 1.5 million logins a day, 12 million mobile ID (mDowód) activations, 6.8 million mobile driving-licence users; Poland has a notified e-ID scheme (EU Digital Decade report, Jun 2026); EU digital identity wallet pilot (‘mObywatel Europa’) planned for Dec 2026 as a separate app linked to mObywatel, to be fully merged in early 2028, activated during the pilot only with a chip e-ID card and its PINOperational
Government services portalePUAP, the national e-administration platform, was taken offline as a precaution after a cyberattack detected and stopped on 10 Nov 2026 (Internal Security Agency investigating; no user-data leak found initially); on 16 Nov the ministry said the main phase of restoring e-services was complete, but COI said full ePUAP restoration was still under way; since Jan 2026 e-Doręczenia (e-delivery) has been the main channel for official correspondence; EU Digital Decade 2026: digital public services for citizens 83.9 (EU 84.6), for businesses 88.8 (EU 88.6)Partial
Digital paymentsBLIK: 822 million transactions worth PLN 131.8 billion in Q3 2026 (up 10% by number and 17% by value year on year), 21.9 million active users at end-Sep 2026; NBP placed BLIK in its highest oversight category (systemically important) on 9 Sep 2026; in 2024 KIR's Elixir handled 47% of retail payment transactions by number (81% by value), BLIK 39% and KIR's Express Elixir instant-payment system 11% by number (NBP oversight report)Operational
Data exchangeAct of 25 Jul 2025 amending the Informatisation Act (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1158) creates the State Information Architecture, makes the digital minister the competent authority under the Interoperable Europe Act and requires an interoperability repository (register metadata, APIs) and national interoperability assessments, applying 18 months after publication; a draft National Interoperability Framework regulation (consultation, Sep 2026) would make REST APIs the defaultPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Since 5 Mar 2026 parents can register a birth in mObywatel: the form goes to the civil registry office, which draws up the birth certificate and a PESEL number application, with delivery through e-Doręczenia; residence registration, civil-status record extracts and PESEL data checks were among the first e-services restored on 16 Nov 2026 after the ePUAP shutdownOperational
Business registryCEIDG sole-trader registration online-only from 1 Nov 2026, with all CEIDG procedures electronic from 1 Nov 2028; one application is passed automatically to the social insurance office (ZUS), statistics office (GUS) and tax office; registration also possible in mObywatel since spring 2026; KRS company register: institutional eKRS accounts since 11 Aug 2025 and KRS notifications in mObywatel from 31 May 2026Operational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Act of 10 May 2018 (Dz.U. 2018 poz. 1000), in force 25 May 2018, implements the GDPR, with the President of UODO as supervisory authority; in 2025 UODO received about 13,000 complaints (about 8,000 in 2024) and imposed 32 fines totalling about PLN 64.5 millionLaw or policy
CybersecurityAct of 23 Jan 2026 amending the National Cybersecurity System (KSC) Act to implement NIS2: in force 3 Apr 2026, covering key and important entities in 18 sectors, with 24-hour initial incident reporting and fines up to EUR 10 million or 2% of turnover; the President referred the high-risk-supplier provisions to the Constitutional Tribunal; CERT Polska received 658,320 reports in 2025 (+10%) and registered 260,783 incidents (+152%), 97% of them computer fraudLaw or policy
AI governanceAct on Artificial Intelligence Systems of 3 Jul 2026 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1003, published 27 Jul 2026) implements the EU AI Act and creates KRiBSI, a collegial supervisor with representatives of the competition, financial, broadcasting and electronic communications regulators that also runs regulatory sandboxes; the Sejm elected its chair on 18 Sep 2026; draft AI Development Policy to 2030 (Apr 2026, not yet adopted) targets at least 80% of public bodies using AI and pilots of the Polish PLLuM language model in at least 1,000 public bodiesPartial
Digital healthEU Digital Decade 2026: access to electronic health records scores 91.8 against an EU average of 86.5Operational
