Spain: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-12 · UN E-Government Development Index: 17 of 193 · 0.921 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry for Digital Transformation and the Civil Service (MTDFP), through its Secretariat of State for Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence; State Agency for Digital Administration (AEAD), constituted in February 2025 and attached via the Secretariat of State for the Civil Service, which took over from the General Secretariat for Digital Administration (SGAD)
Strategy
Digital Spain 2026 (España Digital 2026); Public Administration Digitalisation Plan 2021–2025; Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2024; National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityElectronic national ID card (DNIe) with certificates on its chip; MiDNI mobile app issued by the National Police since 2 April 2025, with three display modes (age only, basic data, full ID) and short-lived QR codes verified against police servers; Cl@ve login also used for public services [To verify: current MiDNI and Cl@ve user numbers]Operational
Legal status of digital IDRoyal Decree 255/2025 of 1 April regulates the DNI in physical and digital form; after a 12-month transition, from April 2026 the MiDNI version has the same legal standing as the card and must be accepted by organisations that require ID, though the card or a passport is still needed for travel abroadLaw or policy
Digital paymentsBizum: 30.6 million users and 1,237 million operations worth €67,751 million in 2025 (+13.2% and +53.3%), including 105.6 million e-commerce payments; Bank of Spain, second half of 2025: 636 million instant transfers (+33.1%) worth €216 billion, and 6,831 million card payments (+11.4%)Operational
Data exchangePlataforma de Intermediación de Datos (PID), run by the central digital administration body, lets state, regional and local administrations query data held by other bodies (e.g. 13 Directorate-General for Traffic services on drivers, vehicles and sanctions) under Article 155 of Law 40/2015; a 2026 update to Mi Carpeta Ciudadana alerts citizens when an administration consults their data through the PIDOperational
Civil registrationElectronic civil registry system DICIREG: the Central Civil Registry Office moved to it in May 2025 with electronic files, digital signatures and a single database; the network links 431 general offices in Spain with more than 100 consular offices abroad (Ministry of Justice)Operational
Business registryCIRCE electronic system (Ministry of Industry) for online company incorporation and closure and for registering the self-employed via a single electronic document (DUE); more than 4,500 Entrepreneur Service Points (PAE) nationwide; Law 18/2022 on business creation added commercial registries as PAEs and set a five-day registration deadline for incorporations without model statutesOperational
Government services portalMi Carpeta Ciudadana app and web (launched 2022, run by the State Agency for Digital Administration): 9.2 million users and over 10 million downloads by May 2026, giving access to education, property, vehicle, health, benefits and employment records, appointments and renewal reminders, with DNIe login via NFC and an eIDAS node for other EU citizensOperational
Data protectionOrganic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), with the EU GDPR; Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), an independent authority: record 30,931 complaints in 2025 (+64%) and fines totalling €48.1 millionLaw or policy
CybersecurityINCIBE handled 122,223 incidents in 2025 (+26%), 401 of them affecting essential and important operators; CCN-CERT (National Cryptologic Centre) responds for the public sector under the National Security Framework (ENS, Royal Decree 311/2022), which binds the public sector and its suppliers; National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019. NIS2 not yet transposed: draft cybersecurity coordination and governance law approved in January 2025, and the European Commission referred Spain to the EU Court of Justice in July 2026Partial
AI governanceSpanish AI Supervision Agency (AESIA), created in 2023 and based in A Coruña; AI Strategy 2024 with €1.5 billion for 2024–2025, including the ALIA public language models; Organic Law bill on the good use and governance of AI, implementing the EU AI Act, approved by the Council of Ministers on 26 May 2026 and sent to parliament, with AESIA as lead supervisor alongside the Bank of Spain, AEPD and the General Council of the JudiciaryPartial
EU Digital Identity WalletCartera Digital Beta, an eIDAS2-aligned wallet holding an age-of-majority credential (specifications published July 2024); the European Commission picked it in 2025 as a basis for its age-verification pilot with France and Greece [To verify: launch date of Spain's full EUDI wallet for all citizens]Partial
