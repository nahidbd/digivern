Spain: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-24 · UN E-Government Development Index: 17 of 193 · 0.921 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry for Digital Transformation and the Civil Service (MTDFP), through its Secretariat of State for Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence; State Agency for Digital Administration (AEAD), constituted on 21 February 2025 at a governing board meeting chaired by the Secretary of State for the Civil Service, which took over the functions of the General Secretariat for Digital Administration (SGAD)
Strategy
Digital Spain 2026 (España Digital 2026); Public Administration Digitalisation Plan 2021–2025; Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2024; National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityElectronic national ID card (DNIe) with certificates on its chip; MiDNI mobile app issued by the National Police since 2 April 2025, with three display modes (age only, basic data, full ID) and short-lived QR codes generated by the police: nearly 2 million people registered and more than 1,755,000 with the digital DNI activated (Nov 2026); the Central Electoral Board accepted MiDNI as ID at polling stations for the 29 Nov 2026 general election, with more than 60,000 QR-reader devices sent to polling tables; Cl@ve login (more than 24 million registered users) also used for public servicesOperational
Legal status of digital IDRoyal Decree 255/2025 of 1 April regulates the DNI in physical and digital form; after a 12-month transition, since 2 April 2026 the MiDNI version has the same legal standing as the card for in-person identification and must be accepted by organisations that require ID, though the card or a passport is still needed for travel abroadLaw or policy
Digital paymentsBizum: 30.6 million users and 1,237 million operations worth €67,751 million in 2025 (+13.2% and +53.3%), including 105.6 million e-commerce payments; Bank of Spain, second half of 2025: non-cash payment operations up 10.8% year on year, worth €6.5 trillion in total, with cards accounting for 65.9% of operations and transfers 15.8%, and 126 million payment cards in circulation (+10.2%)Operational
Data exchangePlataforma de Intermediación de Datos (PID) lets administrations consult data held by other bodies instead of asking citizens for documents; since a May 2026 update, Mi Carpeta Ciudadana users can switch on alerts that tell them when an administration has consulted their data through the PID and which types of data were checkedOperational
Civil registrationElectronic civil registry system DICIREG: the Central Civil Registry Office moved to it in May 2025 with electronic files, digital signatures and a single database, linking 431 general offices in Spain with more than 100 consular offices abroad (Ministry of Justice); the Ministry of Foreign Affairs completed the rollout across the whole consular network (177 offices) on 30 June 2025Operational
Business registryCIRCE electronic system (Ministry of Industry) for online company creation and start-up through the single electronic document (DUE), filed via a network of public and private Entrepreneur Service Points (PAE) set up under Law 14/2013, which must provide these procedures free of charge; new PAE agreements continue to be published (e.g. BOE, 31 Oct 2026)Operational
Government services portalMi Carpeta Ciudadana app and web (launched 2022, run by the State Agency for Digital Administration): 9.2 million users and 10 million downloads by May 2026, giving access to education, property, vehicle, residence, health, benefits and employment records, appointments and renewal reminders (DNI, passport, driving licence, vehicle inspection); DNIe login via NFC and an eIDAS node for other EU citizens were announced as forthcoming improvements in May 2026Operational
Data protectionOrganic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), with the EU GDPR; Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), an independent authority: record 30,931 complaints in 2025 (+64%) and 325 fines totalling €48.1 million (+35%)Law or policy
CybersecurityINCIBE handled 122,223 incidents in 2025 (+26%); CCN-CERT (National Cryptologic Centre) supports and coordinates incident response for the public sector under the National Security Framework (ENS, Royal Decree 311/2022), which binds the public sector and suppliers providing services to it; its Cyber Threats and Trends 2025 report ranked Spain the third most targeted country for hacktivist campaigns, after Ukraine and Israel; National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019. NIS2 not yet transposed: the draft cybersecurity coordination and governance law, approved at first reading on 14 Jan 2025, was never sent to the Cortes, and the European Commission referred Spain to the EU Court of Justice on 8 Jul 2026; the Cortes were dissolved in Oct 2026 (Royal Decree 806/2026) for the 29 Nov 2026 general electionPartial
AI governanceSpanish AI Supervision Agency (AESIA), created in 2023 and based in A Coruña; AI Strategy 2024 with €1.5 billion for 2024–2025, including the ALIA public language models; Organic Law bill on the good use and governance of AI, implementing the EU AI Act, approved by the Council of Ministers on 26 May 2026 and published in the Cortes bulletin in June 2026, lapsed when the Cortes were dissolved in Oct 2026 for the 29 Nov 2026 general election; a new government would have to restart it, while the EU AI Act applies directly in the meantime [To verify: whether the new parliament revives the bill]Partial
EU Digital Identity WalletCartera Digital Beta, an eIDAS2-aligned wallet holding an age-of-majority credential (specifications published July 2024); Spain is using it in the European Commission's age-verification pilot and it underpins a joint Spain–France–Greece proposal on protecting minors online (June 2025). Under Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 each member state must offer an EU Digital Identity Wallet by 24 Dec 2026; as of 20 Nov 2026 Spain had not announced a public launch date or roadmap for its full wallet ('Cartera Digital', which builds on Cl@ve), and the digital transformation minister described it in Sep 2026 as still under developmentPartial
