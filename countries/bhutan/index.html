Bhutan: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-16 · UN E-Government Development Index: 103 of 193 · 0.651 · 2024

Lead agency
GovTech Agency (Government Technology Agency), Royal Government of Bhutan; Royal Monetary Authority (RMA) for payments; Department of Civil Registration and Census for civil registration; National Statistics Bureau for data governance
Strategy
Digital Strategy: Intelligent Bhutan (GovTech, May 2024), with targets for 2029 and 2034; Nu 10 billion Digital Transformation Programme in the 13th Five-Year Plan (training target cut from 400,000 to 100,000 in the Apr 2026 midterm review); joined the 50-in-5 DPI campaign on 4 Jun 2026

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityBhutan National Digital Identity (NDI), a self-sovereign identity wallet, under the National Digital Identity Act 2023 (passed in joint sitting 7 Jul 2023, Royal Assent Jul 2023); app launched Oct 2023; 337,938 registered users (Jul 2025); biometric liveness checks and in-app OTP (Jul 2025), digital signature platform (9 Sep 2025); integration with Ethereum completed Oct 2025, with full credential migration expected by Q1 2026Operational
Digital paymentsRoyal Monetary Authority (RMA) oversight: nearly 40 million mobile banking and over 37 million QR payments in Apr–Jun 2025 (QR up 27.86%); BHIM UPI QR payments with India since 13 Jul 2021; RMA two-phase plan for a cash-lite economy, with a possible surcharge on bank cash deposits and withdrawals in phase twoOperational
Data exchangeNational Data Exchange (NDE), GovTech’s interoperability platform and the standard channel for interagency data exchange, built on WSO2 open-source middleware; 130+ production APIs; began as the Datahub project in the 11th Five-Year PlanOperational
Civil registration (CRVS)Department of Civil Registration and Census (Ministry of Home Affairs): birth and death registration online in every Dzongkhag through the Integrated Citizen Services platform and the Bhutan NDI appOperational
Government services portalCitizen services portal (citizenservices.gov.bt) on the Integrated Citizen Services platform, with access through the Bhutan NDI appOperational
Business registryIntegrated Business Licensing System (Ministry of Industry, Commerce and Employment, since 2022), replacing licensing on the older G2C system in phases; 8,552 new trade licences in two years (May 2026)Partial
Data protectionNo standalone data protection law: personal data is defined in the Information, Communications and Media Act 2018; the National Data Governance Framework (National Statistics Bureau, in effect 11 Jul 2025) sets interim minimums such as a personal data inventory and 72-hour breach notification, and schedules a Data Privacy and Protection Regulation/Guideline within 3–5 years; Parliament backed a call to strengthen data privacy law in Oct 2023Partial
CybersecurityBhutan Computer Incident Response Team (BtCIRT, since 2016) under the GovTech Cybersecurity Division; National Cybersecurity Strategy 2024–2029, which calls for a cybersecurity law (none yet); maturity raised from level 1 to level 2 (Apr 2026); RMA Financial Sector Cybersecurity Strategy 2026–2028, prepared with the IMF, in implementation since 27 Aug 2026Partial
AI governanceGuideline for Generative AI Usage in the Civil Service 2024 (interim, from the Royal Civil Service Commission and GovTech); National AI Strategy 2025, “AI for GNH”, published by GovTech, with no dedicated AI budget; AI maturity level 2 of 5 (Apr 2026)Partial
Digital healthElectronic Patient Information System used by 90% of healthcare providers (Apr 2026)Operational
