Japan: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-19 · UN E-Government Development Index: 13 of 193 · 0.935 · 2024

Lead agency
Digital Agency (デジタル庁), led by Minister for Digital Transformation FURUKAWA Toshiharu (appointed 17 Sep 2026 in Prime Minister Takaichi’s cabinet reshuffle; also Minister in charge of Promoting AI and Digital Reform and Minister in charge of Cybersecurity), with State Minister SHIOZAKI Akihisa, Parliamentary Vice-Minister AKAMATSU Ken and Vice-Minister/Chief Officer MISUMI Ikuo; Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (My Number Card statistics); Ministry of Justice and the Legal Affairs Bureaus (koseki and commercial registration); Bank of Japan (BOJ-NET, CBDC experiments); National Cybersecurity Office (NCO, set up 2025); Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters; Personal Information Protection Commission
Strategy
Priority Policy Program for Realizing a Digital Society (デジタル社会の実現に向けた重点計画) 2026, Cabinet decision 21 Jul 2026 (six pillars unchanged; AI transformation (AX) of national and local government; benefit-payment infrastructure from FY2026); Artificial Intelligence Basic Plan (Cabinet decision 14 Jul 2026); Cybersecurity Strategy (Cabinet decision 23 Dec 2025); Digital Administrative and Fiscal Reform Summary 2026 (7 Jul 2026), whose council was reorganised into the AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council (Cabinet decision 20 Oct 2026)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityMy Number Card: 104,938,223 cards held (including cards used abroad), 84.6% of the population (end Oct 2026, up from 82.7% at end Apr 2026); paper health insurance cards expired in Dec 2025, accepted until end Mar 2026, making the card the main health insurance credential; card on iPhone since Jun 2025 and on Android under the new smartphone service since 20 Oct 2026, with about 9.79 million smartphone users (end Sep 2026)Operational
Digital paymentsZengin System (interbank transfers): 9.388 million transactions worth about ¥20.9 trillion per business day, and BOJ-NET funds transfers: 101,980 transactions worth ¥239.7 trillion per business day (Sep 2026, Bank of Japan); “Cotra” (ことら送金) small-value transfer service usable in 35 apps (from 15 Jul 2026); Bank of Japan CBDC pilot experiment running since Apr 2023, progress report Jun 2026: 50,000 transactions per second reached and no technical “knockout factor” foundOperational
Data exchangePublic Service Mesh (公共サービスメッシュ), the Digital Agency’s data-linkage platform: local governments trial functions on the Government Cloud from FY2025 and a common inter-agency information-linkage service for ministries was planned from Jan 2026; the 2026 Priority Policy Program starts strengthening it and the Information Provision Network System in FY2026 for benefit payments; base registries for real estate (map data by end FY2027, registration data mid-FY2028)Partial
Civil registration (koseki)Amended Family Register Act (Act No. 17 of 2019): since 1 Mar 2024 koseki certificates can be obtained at any municipal office (wide-area issuance), My Number linkage lets koseki copies be dropped from social-security procedures, and a koseki electronic certificate exists for online procedures; furigana readings of names recorded in koseki from 26 May 2025 (corrections by 25 May 2026, including via Mynaportal, then entered by mayors ex officio); online requests for koseki e-certificates by prefectures and other bodies targeted for FY2028Operational
Business registryCommercial and corporate registration at the Legal Affairs Bureaus (Ministry of Justice); gBizID, the Digital Agency’s common business login for e-Gov, jGrants and other services: about 950,000 corporations, about one third of all, held accounts (Mar 2026), 224 connected services (Aug 2025); remote signing with commercial-registration e-certificates linked to gBizID targeted from Jul 2026 [To verify: actual launch and corporate number (National Tax Agency) figures]Operational
Government services portalMynaportal: about 85 million registered users (end Mar 2026); a new “Myna App” planned for 2026 to bring the personal digital service portals into oneOperational
Data protectionAct on the Protection of Personal Information (Act No. 57 of 2003); leak reports from businesses: 17,139 in FY2025, the second highest after 19,056 the year before (annual report cited by the Digital Minister, 7 Jul 2026); the 2026 Priority Policy Program prepares for enforcement of an act amending the APPI [To verify: amendment’s passage and enforcement date]Operational
CybersecurityNational Cybersecurity Office (国家サイバー統括室, NCO) set up in 2025 to coordinate government cybersecurity policy; Cybersecurity Strategy adopted by Cabinet 23 Dec 2025; “Project YATA-Shield” package of cybersecurity measures responding to more capable AI (18 May 2026); attack-related traffic up about 11 times from 2015 to 2024 [To verify: Active Cyber Defense law of 2025, enactment and enforcement dates]Partial
AI governanceAI Promotion Act (Act on the Promotion of Research, Development and Utilisation of AI-Related Technologies, Act No. 53 of 2025); Artificial Intelligence Strategy Headquarters (人工知能戦略本部); Artificial Intelligence Basic Plan adopted by Cabinet 14 Jul 2026; AI Safety Institute (AISI) collects and evaluates information on the cybersecurity capabilities of AI models; AI and Digital Reform Promotion Council created by Cabinet decision 20 Oct 2026 (first meeting 22 Oct); government AI platform “Gennai” (源内) for use inside governmentPartial
Local government systemsStandardisation of local government core systems and move to the Government Cloud (Local Government Information Systems Standardisation Act 2021): most in-scope systems migrated by end Mar 2026 (about 70% of systems, per the Priority Policy Program 2026 outline)Partial
