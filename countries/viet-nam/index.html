Viet Nam: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-25 · UN E-Government Development Index: 71 of 193 · 0.771 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Science and Technology (MST), which absorbed the former Ministry of Information and Communications in the 2025 government restructuring and is now the lead ministry for science, innovation, digital transformation and telecoms; it reports national platform figures such as NDXP traffic and digital signature certificates. The Ministry of Public Security (MPS) runs VNeID, the national population database and Project 06, is the standing agency of the Government Steering Committee on Science and Technology Development, Innovation, Digital Transformation and Project 06, and since 1 March 2025 has held state management of information security and personal data protection (through its Department of Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention, A05). A National Data Committee began operating in early 2026; the State Bank of Vietnam oversees payments and Mobile Money
Strategy
Politburo Resolution 57-NQ/TW (22 Dec 2024) on breakthroughs in science, technology, innovation and national digital transformation, with 2030 targets of a digital economy worth at least 30% of GDP, R&D spending of 2% of GDP and all central and local state management run in a digital environment with connected national databases; by March 2026, 911 of 1,633 implementation tasks were reported complete. It builds on the National Digital Transformation Programme to 2025 with orientation to 2030 (Decision 749/QĐ-TTg, 3 Jun 2020) and Project 06 on population data, e-identification and authentication (Decision 06/QĐ-TTg, 6 Jan 2022)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityVNeID, the MPS e-identification app linked to the chip-based citizen ID card (căn cước): more than 102.1 million digital identity profiles and 71.5 million activated accounts by July 2026. Decree 320/2026/NĐ-CP (issued 13 Aug 2026, in force 28 Sep 2026) amends Decree 69/2024: any foreigner lawfully in Viet Nam may now get a VNeID account regardless of residence card, and national digital platforms must accept e-ID accounts for login and transactions by 31 Dec 2026 [To verify: number of chip-based ID cards issued]Operational
Legal status of digital IDLaw on Electronic Identification and Authentication, passed 20 Nov 2026 (473 of 478 deputies; 7 chapters, 45 articles), in force 1 Jul 2027: accounts in high-risk sectors (banking, securities, telecoms, e-commerce and social media) must be authenticated against and linked to the holder's national e-identity, on a timetable the government will set. E-identities are created automatically from complete database records, existing ones stay valid, self-controlled digital identities must be stored in Viet Nam, and the national authority starts issuing public digital signature certificates on 1 Mar 2027Law or policy
Digital paymentsNAPAS, the national switch: 2025 transaction volume up 23.7% and value up 8.7%, averaging over 32 million transactions a day; almost 90 million mobile banking accounts could scan VietQR by Oct 2025, with VietQR volume up over 52% (Jan–Oct 2025), and QR links run with Thailand, Cambodia and Laos, plus China via UnionPay from Dec 2025. SBV reports 2025 cashless payments worth about 28 times GDP, 232 million individual payment accounts and 146.1 million customer records biometrically verified against ID data (Jan 2026)Operational
Mobile moneyTelecom-operator Mobile Money, piloted under SBV licence from late 2021 and extended to end-2025, now has a full rulebook in Decree 368/2025/NĐ-CP (Jan 2026), which raised the monthly cap tenfold to VND 100 million and allows one account per provider, tied to a mobile subscription. About 10.89 million accounts, roughly 70% in rural and remote areas, had made over 290 million transactions worth more than VND 8.5 trillion by Sep 2025Partial
Data exchangeThe National Data Integration and Sharing Platform (NDXP) had carried more than 4.6 billion transactions by July 2026 (MST), alongside 32.5 million digital signature certificates issued. Resolution 57 calls for all national and sectoral databases to be connected and shared, and a National Data Committee began work in early 2026; the Data Security Law passed on 20 Nov 2026 (in force 1 Jul 2027) adds five risk tiers for datasets and bars unauthorised AI training on high-tier defence and security data [To verify: number of systems and provincial LGSPs connected to NDXP]Operational
Civil registration (CRVS)Interlinked online procedures under Decree 63/2024 bundle birth registration, permanent residence registration and health insurance cards for children under 6 (and death registration with related benefits), filed through VNeID or the National Public Service Portal. Decree 301/2026/NĐ-CP (30 Jul 2026, in force 1 Sep 2026) adds an optional ID card for the child in the same process, integrates the birth certificate and health insurance card into the child's e-ID account, and sets birth registration at 24 hours from a complete dossierOperational
Business registryDecree 168/2025/NĐ-CP on enterprise registration (30 Jun 2025) allows online registration of companies and household businesses through the National Business Registration Portal, with filers' identities checked electronically against the national population database or e-ID platform. About 111,700 enterprises were newly established in H1 2026 (+22.5% year on year), with registered capital of VND 1,352.6 trillion (National Statistics Office) [To verify: share of registrations filed online]Operational
Government services portalNational Public Service Portal (dichvucong.gov.vn), the single front door for online procedures, which received 8,163,307 applications between 30 Jun and 30 Jul 2026 and handled 2,880,752 successful online payments worth over VND 843.5 billion in July 2026; 82 groups of essential procedures are being rolled out on it. VNeID login is used for access, and Decree 320/2026 requires national platforms to accept e-ID accounts by 31 Dec 2026Operational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 (passed 26 Jun 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026) with Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP, which replaced Decree 13/2023; the regulator is MPS through its A05 department. Fines reach 5% of prior-year revenue (or VND 3 billion, if higher) for cross-border transfer breaches and ten times the proceeds for selling personal data, but depend on a sanctioning decree still in draft in 2026; impact assessments go to A05 within 60 days and serious breaches must be reported within 72 hoursLaw or policy
CybersecurityLaw on Cybersecurity 2025, passed 10 Dec 2025 (434 of 443 deputies present) and in force 1 Jul 2026, merges the 2018 Cybersecurity Law and the 2015 Law on Network Information Security into one text of 45 articles; MPS is the standing coordinator of a National Cybersecurity Incident Response Alliance of authorities and firms, and the law reflects the UN Convention against Cybercrime signed in Hanoi. State management of information security moved from the former MIC to MPS on 1 Mar 2025Operational
AI governanceLaw on Artificial Intelligence (No. 134/2025/QH15), passed 10 Dec 2025 and in force 1 Mar 2026 with 35 articles: it requires human oversight of important decisions, provides for a national AI computing centre, a National AI Development Fund, AI vouchers for businesses and a controlled sandbox, and covers AI-generated content and cross-border AI platforms [To verify: implementing decrees issued under the AI law]Law or policy
