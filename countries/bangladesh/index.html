Bangladesh: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-15 · UN E-Government Development Index: 100 of 193 · 0.657 · 2024

Lead agency
ICT Division (Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Information Technology) and a2i (Aspire to Innovate)
Strategy
National Digital Transformation Strategy 2025–2030 (draft by ICT Division with UNDP, released 16 Feb 2025); National AI Policy 2026–2030 in draft

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational ID (NID), Bangladesh Election Commission, now issued from age 16; Unified Digital Identity (One-ID), issued from birth, planned under the D-STAR project (Tk 91.93 billion, of which Tk 15.30 billion a World Bank loan; ICT Division with Bangladesh Computer Council, 2026–2031), sent to the Planning Commission for approval (Sep 2026); NID, Family Card and Farmer Card services would move to it; on 18 Nov 2026 the ICT minister discussed the ‘One Citizen One ID’ plan with Estonia’s ambassador, with an EU-funded project involving Estonia’s e-Governance Academy and the British Council also supporting a national interoperability frameworkOperational
Digital paymentsBangla QR national QR mandatory for merchants since 1 Jul 2026 (about 350,000 transactions worth Tk 143.54 crore a day, Sep 2026); person-to-person Bangla QR across banks and MFS from 1 Nov 2026; Inclusive Instant Payment System (Mojaloop), full interoperability targeted Jul 2027Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)Birth and Death Registration Information System (BDRIS), Office of the Registrar General, nationwide; completeness 63% for births and 59% for deaths (2025), target 80% by 2027Operational
Business registryRegistrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC): 12,786 new entities in FY2025–26, up 25%; 316,150 entities registered (Jun 2026)Operational
Government services portalmyGov integrated service platform (a2i): 168 services added in 2025, in all 64 Deputy Commissioner offices, 80 more planned for 2026; 3.13 million registered usersOperational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Act 2026, passed by Parliament 9 Apr 2026 keeping the provisions of the Personal Data Protection Ordinance 2025 (No. 61, 6 Nov 2025, amended Feb 2026)Law or policy
Data governanceNational Data Management Act 2026, passed 10 Apr 2026 from the National Data Governance Ordinance 2025; sets up a National Data Management AuthorityLaw or policy
Data exchangeNational Responsible Data Exchange (NRDEX), built on X-Road by the ICT Division with Estonia’s e-Governance Academy and run by the Bangladesh Computer Council, launched 11 Aug 2026 with test and production environments; one connected member so far, with the Registrar General, DGHS, BRTA, Immigration and Passports and NBR in the priority pipelinePartial
CybersecurityCyber Security Act 2026, passed 10 Apr 2026 from the Cyber Security Ordinance 2025 (No. 25, in force 21 May 2025, which repealed the Cyber Security Act 2023); amended 30 Jun 2026, removing section 20 on online gambling; a further Cyber Security (Amendment) Act 2026 is in draft: the revised draft published Nov 2026 (comments to 25 Nov) adds a “rumour” offence of up to 5 years and defamation of up to 2 years, with tribunal approval before arrests over registered media contentLaw or policy
AI governanceDraft National AI Policy 2026–2030 (Jan 2026): risk-based rules, a Bangla LLM and an AI Innovation Fund; still being drafted in Jul 2026Partial
NGO registrationNGO Affairs BureauOperational
