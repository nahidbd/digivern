Nepal: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-13 · UN E-Government Development Index: 119 of 193 · 0.578 · 2024

Lead agency
Office of the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers (IT and digital governance under allocation-of-business rules gazetted 13 May 2026; the Department of Information Technology, Integrated Data Management Centre and National Cyber Security Centre were dissolved and merged into a new information technology and governance office under it; previously the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology)
Strategy
Digital Nepal Framework (2019); Digital Nepal Framework 2.0 drafted in 2025 and still awaiting Cabinet approval (May 2026); National AI Policy 2082 (Aug 2025)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational ID, Department of National ID and Civil Registration: 19.57 million enrolled, 7.50 million cards printed and 3.78 million distributed (6 Jan 2026); about 20 million enrolled, 7.8 million cards printed and about 4 million distributed by mid-2026; tender for system operation cancelled on 8 May 2026 in favour of in-house management; downloadable provisional eNID launched Jun 2026; authentication failures hit linked services in Jul 2026; Nepal Rastra Bank requires wallet providers to verify the national ID, and existing wallet users had to add their ID number by 17 Oct 2026Partial
Digital paymentsNepal Rastra Bank oversight; connectIPS by Nepal Clearing House (NCHL): 112.36 million transactions in FY2024/25; QR payments: 325.97 million transactions in FY2024/25 and 75.58 million worth NPR 167.9 billion in Bhadra 2083 (Aug–Sep 2026) (NRB); NEPALPAY QR interoperable under the NepalQR standardOperational
Data exchangeNo national data exchange layer in operation yet: a government-wide data exchange is part of the Nepal Digital Transformation Project (World Bank $50m, approved 9 Feb 2026, effective 25 May 2026; ADB $40m, approved 10 Mar 2026, effective 10 Jul 2026), but no government system was connected as of Jun 2026, ADB had disbursed nothing by Aug 2026, and implementation is delayed by the move of IT functions to the Prime Minister’s OfficeTo verify
Civil registration (CRVS)Online vital-event registration by DoNIDCR, not yet in every ward (151 wards without it in 2023); birth, marriage and divorce certificates being added to the Nagarik App (Apr 2026)Partial
Business registryOffice of the Company Registrar: company registration through to cancellation handled online (since Feb 2025)Operational
Government services portalNagarik App, the citizen services app: police clearance reports, Nepal Engineering Council certificates and Public Service Commission features (Apr 2026); real-time blood stock information (Jul 2026); bank account opening with the national ID card, starting with Global IME Bank (Jul 2026)Partial
Data protectionPrivacy Act 2075 (2018) and Privacy Regulations (2020); no separate data protection authority; draft Personal Data Protection Policy 2082 released for consultation (Jul 2025); a data protection bill setting up a Data Protection Board is being drafted, with Cabinet approval and tabling in Parliament targeted within six months (Nov 2026)Law or policy
CybersecurityNational Cyber Security Policy 2080 (2023), endorsed by Cabinet in Aug 2023; the Information Technology and Cyber Security Bill 2082 passed the House of Representatives on 14 Aug 2025 but lapsed when the House was dissolved in Sep 2025; no cybersecurity act yet; the National Cyber Security Centre moved under the Prime Minister’s Office in 2026Law or policy
AI governanceNational AI Policy 2082, approved by Cabinet 11 Aug 2025: a National AI Centre (inaugurated Nov 2025, with an AI Council), regulatory sandboxes and AI centres of excellence in every province; AI compute centres announced in the FY2026/27 budget (May 2026)Partial
