Egypt: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-26 · UN E-Government Development Index: 95 of 193 · 0.670 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT; minister Raafat Hendy, sworn in Feb 2026), which runs the Digital Egypt platform, with the Information Technology Industry Development Agency (ITIDA) and the National Telecom Regulatory Authority (NTRA), home of the national CERT (EG-CERT); Ministry of Interior for the national ID card and civil status records; Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) for payments; Personal Data Protection Centre (PDPC); Supreme Cybersecurity Council; National Council for Artificial Intelligence (now also covering quantum computing and emerging technologies); General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) for company incorporation
Strategy
Digital Egypt, the government digital transformation programme: 242 services on the Digital Egypt platform (Jul 2026), with targets of more than 270 by end-2026 and about 450 by 2030; National AI Strategy, 2nd edition 2025–2030 (Jan 2025); National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023–2027

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational ID card (Ministry of Interior), required to log in to Digital Egypt; MOIEG-PASS, the Interior Ministry’s biometric verification app (pilot on Android from Feb 2026), registers users by scanning the national ID card and facial recognition; a second version of the Digital Identity app with biometric verification was launched by Jul 2026; first-time national ID cards still need an in-person visit to a civil status officePartial
Digital paymentsCentral Bank of Egypt oversight. Instant Payment Network (InstaPay), launched Mar 2022, with QR payments from Jun 2024: more than 24 million users and 2.4 billion transactions worth about EGP 7 trillion by Sep 2026, linking bank accounts, Meeza cards and mobile wallets; 67 million mobile wallets (Sep 2026). Meeza, the national payment scheme, had over 41.5 million cards (Dec 2024). Card tokenisation on mobile apps launched Dec 2024 with Apple Pay as the first phase (Visa, Mastercard and Meeza), with Android to follow (Sep 2025)Operational
Data exchangeGovernment Digital Hub connecting 105 government entities, and 100 national databases linked to an integrated data management system that is still under development (Jul 2026); a Unified Government Services Card linking food subsidies, health insurance and social protection is being rolled outPartial
Civil registration (CRVS)Civil status records (birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates) held by the Ministry of Interior; previously registered certificates can be requested online through Digital Egypt if the record is computerised (delivered or collected), via the Interior Ministry website, or at Egypt Post branchesPartial
Business registryGAFI Company e-Incorporation Service (launched 2023): sole proprietorships, partnerships and limited liability companies under Investment Law No. 72 of 2017 can be set up fully online, with electronically signed articles and notarisation and all fees paid in one card transaction; the first company was registered online in Aug 2023. Commercial registry extracts and renewals are filed and paid online through Digital EgyptOperational
Government services portalDigital Egypt platform (MCIT): 242 government services and about 20 million transactions since the start of 2026 (Jul 2026); targets of more than 270 services by end-2026 and about 450 by 2030; fees payable by bank card or Fawry; e-signature, e-seal and a Digital Mailbox being addedOperational
Data protectionPersonal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020; executive regulations issued by Decree No. 816 of 2025 on 1 Nov 2025 (made public on 25 Dec 2025), starting a one-year compliance grace period that ended at the start of Nov 2026; regulator: Personal Data Protection Centre (PDPC), which opened an e-portal for licences, permits and accreditation in Oct 2026 (licences usually valid for three years; separate permits for cross-border transfers, direct marketing and CCTV)Law or policy
CybersecurityAnti-Cyber and Information Technology Crimes Law No. 175 of 2018; Supreme Cybersecurity Council oversees the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023–2027 and has approved cybersecurity guidelines for government bodies; EG-CERT, the national CERT, sits in NTRA; Egypt is in the top tier of the ITU Global Cybersecurity Index 2024, one of 12 countries with a full score of 100Law or policy
AI governanceNational AI Strategy, 2nd edition 2025–2030, unveiled by the National Council for Artificial Intelligence in Jan 2025: targets AI adding more than US$42.7 billion (7.7% of GDP) by 2030, about 30,000 AI professionals, over 250 AI companies and 6,000 AI publications a year; the council, now covering quantum computing and emerging technologies, released a Guide to Egypt’s National AI Governance Framework on 14 Mar 2026Partial
