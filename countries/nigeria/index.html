Nigeria: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-20 · UN E-Government Development Index: 144 of 193 · 0.482 · 2024

Lead agency
Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy (FMCIDE), with the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) as implementing regulator and Galaxy Backbone as government ICT infrastructure provider
Strategy
National Digital Economy Policy and Strategy (NDEPS) 2020–2030; Ministry Strategic Agenda 2023–2027; NITDA Strategic Roadmap and Action Plan (SRAP 2.0) 2024–2027; National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill at third reading in the Senate (Jul 2026), not yet law

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational Identification Number (NIN), National Identity Management Commission (NIMC): NIMC reports more than 142 million enrolments (National Identity Day, 16 Sep 2026; 136 million in Jul 2026) and the government targets 95% coverage by Dec 2026; NIMC Act 2026 repeals the 2007 Act, makes the NIN the foundational identifier and NIMC the root certification authority; World Bank-supported ID4D project (US$430m with AFD and EIB) counted about 121 million NINs against a 180 million target and about US$196m (45.6%) undisbursed at end-Sep 2026, and Nigeria has asked for a third extension to 31 Dec 2027; BVN: 66.23 million (Jul 2025)Operational
Digital paymentsNIBSS Instant Payments: e-payments worth ₦1.07 quadrillion in 2024 and ₦284.99 trillion in Q1 2025 (up 17.7%); new ISO 20022 National Payment Stack had processed 26.55 million transactions worth ₦1.4 trillion across 48 institutions (Aug 2026); eNaira CBDC (2021) little used (about ₦22 billion processed in total) and recast by the CBN’s Payments System Vision 2028 (Jun 2026) as infrastructure for cross-border paymentsOperational
Data exchangeGalaxy Backbone designated as the Government Data Exchange Platform and interoperability layer under an OSGF circular (Aug 2025); the Government Service Portal integrates with the Nigerian Digital Exchange so agencies can share data (Jul 2026); the Budget and Economic Planning Minister called for an end to multiple identity databases across public institutions (Jul 2026)Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)National Population Commission VitalReg online birth and death registration nationwide since 1 Jul 2026, sign-up with NIN; 4,011 registration centres in 774 LGAs, to rise to about 8,000; birth registration coverage about 57% of about 5 million births a year, death registration under 20%Partial
Business registryCorporate Affairs Commission (CAC): fully digital, end-to-end registry with an AI-driven portal; close to 10,000 registration requests a day (Feb 2026)Operational
Government services portalGovernment Service Portal (services.gov.ng) with single sign-on, built with KOICA and Galaxy Backbone: soft launch 28 Jul 2026 after a pilot; NIMC NIN pre-enrolment and verification, SMEDAN registration and Scamwatch scam reporting online, other agencies being integrated; user testing flagged problems with registration, image capture and OTP delivery (Nov 2026)Partial
Data protectionNigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (signed 12 Jun 2023) created the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC); General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 in effect from 19 Sep 2025, replacing the NDPR 2019; in 2025 NDPC had 38,661 registered data controllers and processors of major importance, opened 146 investigations and licensed 306 data protection compliance organisationsLaw or policy
CybersecurityCybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015, amended 2024 (signed 28 Feb 2024); party to the Council of Europe Budapest Convention since 1 Nov 2022; ngCERT in the Office of the National Security Adviser; National Cybersecurity Policy and Strategy launched 23 Feb 2021; National Ministerial Advisory Council on cybersecurity inaugurated Apr 2026, with a NITDA-led secretariatLaw or policy
AI governanceNational AI Strategy 2025–2029 published by the digital economy ministry (Sep 2025), non-binding; since Feb 2025 AI-enabled IT systems costing ₦10 million or more need NITDA approval; no AI law, with three AI bills before the National Assembly (SB 731 and HB 2567 of 2025, HB 1810 of 2024) as of Jul 2026Partial
