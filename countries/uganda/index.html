Uganda: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-14 · UN E-Government Development Index: 150 of 193 · 0.446 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of ICT and National Guidance; National Information Technology Authority (NITA-U), which runs UGhub, UGPass, the National Backbone and CERT.UG/CC; National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) for identity and civil registration; Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO)
Strategy
Digital Transformation Roadmap 2023/24–2027/28 (five pillars, UNDP support); National Data Strategy 2026/27–2030/31 (launched 20 Oct 2026, estimated Shs1,662.6 billion; UBOS objected that it oversteps the UBOS Act); NITA-U Strategic Plan FY2025/26–2029/30; World Bank Uganda Digital Acceleration Project–GovNet (IDA US$200 million, approved Jun 2021, scope cut in Jul 2025, closing date 30 May 2026 with an extension to Aug 2027 requested)

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identityNational ID (Ndaga Muntu), NIRA: about 35 million people in the register after the May 2025–Feb 2026 mass registration and renewal (14.3 million renewals, 6.4 million new registrations against a 17.2 million target, 28.6 million legacy records migrated); new cards went live for verification on 31 Mar 2026; by 7 Sep 2026, 15.4 million new cards printed and 10 million issued, with about 5.4 million uncollected at NIRA offices because of staff shortages; identity system being modernised on MOSIP; banks and other financial institutions must now verify customers online against the NIRA database by National Identification Number through NIRA’s National Security Information System (NSIS), but only 10 of 107 partner institutions were live by 28 Oct 2026Operational
Digital signatureUGPass digital authentication and legally binding e-signature service (NITA-U), onboarding with the National ID or e-Passport; also offers QR verification and an e-Wallet for credentialsOperational
Data exchangeUGhub integration hub (NITA-U): 135 platforms integrated (Oct 2026), target 300 by 2030/31 under the National Data Strategy; NITA-U plans to raise the share of ministries, departments and agencies connected from 37% to 73% by 2029/30; UGhub upgrade financed under the World Bank GovNet projectPartial
Government services portalUGOV single platform for public services, unveiled by NITA-U with AlphaX in Nov 2025 and still being developed; NITA-U targets raising active use of e-government services from 9.2% to 40% by 2029/30Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)Births and deaths registered by NIRA (civil registration moved from URSB); birth certificates free for children registered before six months, with community registration drives from Jun 2026; fees of Shs10,000–50,000 for older children and adultsPartial
Business registryURSB Online Business Registration System for business registration, insolvency and business management (launch event Apr 2024); Non-Individual Register giving every company, partnership, NGO and cooperative a Unique Entity Registration Number, unveiled May 2026 under the Tax Procedures Code (Amendment) Act 2025, full rollout expected from Jun 2026Operational
Digital paymentsBank of Uganda data: mobile money transactions worth Shs263 trillion (5.8 billion transactions) in Jan–Jul 2026, 72% of the 2025 total of Shs365.9 trillion; 38 million active and 59.8 million registered customers and 1.3 million agents (Jul 2026). Finance Minister: 36.7 million active accounts and 1.22 million agents at 31 Mar 2026Operational
ConnectivityNational Backbone Infrastructure (NITA-U): 603 government sites connected; Last Mile project has connected 575 of 700 target sites; backbone projected at over 3,156 km across 52 districts once complete; NBI extension, Last Mile and rural broadband removed from the World Bank GovNet project in the Jul 2025 restructuringPartial
Data protectionData Protection and Privacy Act, 2019; regulator: Personal Data Protection Office. 14,697 data controllers and processors registered of about 50,000 required, with over 35,000 unregistered (Aug 2026); PDPO working with CID and the DPP to prosecute non-registrationLaw or policy
CybersecurityNational CERT (CERT.UG/CC) under NITA-U. On 17 Mar 2026 the Constitutional Court nullified sections 11, 23 and 26–29 of the Computer Misuse Act and the whole Computer Misuse (Amendment) Act 2022; the government did not appeal and the Attorney General advised returning the law to ParliamentOperational
AI governanceNational AI and Emerging Technologies Strategy: consultations Mar 2026; UNESCO AI readiness report approved Jun 2026; draft completed Aug 2026 and awaiting senior ministry review before Cabinet (Oct 2026); National Data Strategy (Oct 2026) meant to lay its groundworkPartial
