Brazil: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-20 · UN E-Government Development Index: 50 of 193 · 0.840 · 2024

Lead agency
Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services (MGI), through its Digital Government Secretariat (SGD, Secretary Rogério Mascarenhas)
Strategy
National Digital Government Strategy 2024–2027 (Decree 12.069 of 21 Jun 2024), which also set up the Rede Gov.br network of federal, state and municipal governments (all states take part); MGI opened a public consultation to revise the strategy’s recommendations in Jun 2026

Building blocks

AreaWhat existsStatus
Digital identitygov.br single sign-on: nearly 180 million users (Nov 2026), 83.4 million with “gold” accounts (Mar 2026); CPF taxpayer number; new national ID card (CIN): nearly 70 million issued (Nov 2026), old RG cards valid until 2032Operational
Digital paymentsPix instant payments, Banco Central do Brasil: about 80 billion transactions worth more than R$35 trillion in 2025, up 25.7%; about 76 billion transactions in Jan–Oct 2026, up 17.6% on a year earlier; 154 million individual users (about 89% of adults) and 14 million businesses (Oct 2026); daily record of 319.7 million transactions (6 Nov 2026); 7.1 billion transactions worth about R$3.2 trillion in Sep 2026 and more than 1 billion registered Pix keysOperational
Data exchangeConecta gov.br (MGI): more than 60 APIs; 1.1 billion transactions between public bodies and R$6.31 billion saved in 2025; 2.55 billion transactions and about R$15 billion saved since 2020 (Jun 2026)Operational
Government services portalgov.br: more than 5,000 federal digital services plus more than 9,000 state and municipal services (Nov 2026); more than 80 million unique users a month (Mar 2026)Operational
Business registryRedesim integrated business registration network; 5.1 million new companies opened in 2025, a record, up 18.6% (Receita Federal data reported by Sebrae)Operational
Data protectionGeneral Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018); the ANPD became the National Data Protection Agency, an autonomous agency, under Law 15.352 of 25 Feb 2026Law or policy
Civil registration (CRVS)National Civil Registry Information System (SIRC, Decree 9.929/2019) collects birth, marriage, death and stillbirth records sent by civil registry offices and publishes registration counts; run by a managing committee (CGSirc) of federal bodies including the Ministry of Human Rights and Citizenship, INSS and IBGEOperational
CybersecurityNational Cybersecurity Policy (PNCiber, Decree 11.856/2023) with a national committee (CNCiber); National Cybersecurity Strategy (E-Ciber, Decree 12.573/2025)Law or policy
AI governanceBrazilian AI Plan (PBIA) 2024–2028, R$23 billion, presented Jul 2024; AI bill PL 2338/2023 passed by the Senate in Dec 2024 and still before the Chamber of Deputies (rapporteur Aguinaldo Ribeiro), where the government is pressing for a vote before the end of 2026 (Nov 2026)Partial
