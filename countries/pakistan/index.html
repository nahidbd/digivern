Pakistan: digital government profile

Reviewed 2026-11-19 · UN E-Government Development Index: 136 of 193 · 0.510 · 2024

Lead agency
Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA, chaired by Dr Sohail Munir), set up in Aug 2025 under the Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025 and given the lead role in national digitalisation, with a National Digital Commission chaired by the Prime Minister; Ministry of IT and Telecommunication (MoITT), National Information Technology Board (NITB) and NADRA
Strategy
Digital Nation Pakistan Act 2025 (passed Jan 2025); the PDA's National Digital Masterplan (pillars: digital governance, digital economy, digital society) went through provincial consultation from May 2026 and was still not approved in late Sep 2026, when the government sought to put it before a meeting of the Prime Minister and provincial chief ministers; the draft National Data Governance Policy 2026 (released by MoITT on 26 Jun 2026, to be overseen by the PDA) still awaited federal cabinet approval in Oct 2026; in Jul 2026 the Prime Minister set the goal of access to all public services under a single digital ID, and in Oct 2026 he ordered all federal ministries to digitise their data for a national central data centre; Digital Economy Enhancement Project (DEEP), World Bank, approved Mar 2024 at US$78m, now US$69.16m with 12.09% disbursed (Sep 2026), closing Jul 2028

Digital identityCNIC, National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA); chipless CNIC with a QR code readable through the PakID app launched in Aug 2026 (approved by the federal government on 23 Feb 2026); PakID app (launched early 2025, over 18 million downloads, 8.17 million of them Nov 2025–May 2026, and more than 1.36 million applications Mar–May 2026); version 5.8.0 (Sep 2026) added family digital IDs and QR verification of B-Forms; iris scanning for children under 18 at more than 5,000 countersOperational
Data exchangeNational Data Exchange Layer (NDEL), built by NADRA with MoITT, pilot announced for Dec 2025 under the NDEL Regulations 2025; 6 government entities integrated against a target of 40 and 1 private entity against 13; 6.5 million transactions in total and 650,000 in Aug 2026 (World Bank review, Sep 2026); the draft National Data Governance Policy (Jun 2026) proposes a national data exchange called WASL with a single authoritative source for identity, land, address and vehicle recordsPartial
Digital ID credentialsNADRA has issued 2.3 million digital IDs (up from about 850,000 by Dec 2025); PakID Vault offers 11 types of verifiable credentials against a target of 40 (World Bank review, Sep 2026)Partial
Digital paymentsRaast instant payment system of the State Bank of Pakistan, launched 11 Jan 2021 (bulk government payments first, then person-to-person and merchant payments), domestic transfers only; run by Raast Payments Pakistan (Pvt) Ltd, a wholly owned SBP subsidiary; about 742 million transactions worth Rs23.27 trillion in Jan–Mar 2026 (664 million person-to-person) and more than 2.6 million merchants onboarded for Raast P2M by end-Mar 2026; Raast QR codes on more than 45 million gas and electricity bills and accepted at all NADRA centres, and the finance ministry asked SBP for a working paper on a Raast QR merchant-fee (MDR) scheme (Nov 2026)Operational
Government services portalCitizen portal under DEEP with 18 services against a target of 20 (World Bank review, Sep 2026); 10 priority services shortlisted and a pilot citizen services portal tested by NITB and NADRA (Oct 2026), with provincial rollout from early 2027; NITB launched the Pak App for Islamabad residents in Jun 2026 (a revamp of the City Islamabad App), while procurement for a national unified super app under DEEP was extended to Apr 2026; the Islamabad Aasaan Khidmat Centre processed more than 171,000 applications Jan–Sep 2026 and the Prime Minister ordered centres nationwide (Oct 2026)Partial
Civil registration (CRVS)Births and deaths registered at more than 12,000 Union Councils, over 99% digitally connected to NADRA; NADRA hospital notification system piloted in 50+ hospitals (Oct 2025); birth, death, marriage and divorce registration with Union Councils through the PakID app in all Punjab districts, several Sindh districts and Quetta (Jan 2026), and online birth and death registration in Islamabad through PakID (Jun 2026); more than 32 million birth records still not linked to the national ID database (Apr 2026)Partial
Business registrySECP eZfile online corporate registry (replacing eServices, LEAP project); 5,003 new companies registered in Oct 2026, 99.9% of them online through eZfile, for a total of 321,794 registered companies; the DEEP Pakistan Business Portal (US$15m) had processed no registration or licence transactions and moved no B2G services online by Sep 2026Partial
Data protectionNo law yet: MoITT's Personal Data Protection Bill (2023 draft, consultation completed) had still not passed both houses of Parliament (Oct 2026); the draft National Data Governance Policy 2026 for public-sector data (released 26 Jun 2026) would let citizens see who accessed their data, require breach reporting to the PDA and keep sensitive data in Pakistan, but still needed federal cabinet approval (Oct 2026)Partial
CybersecurityNational Cyber Security Policy 2021; CERT Rules 2023 with a 14-member CERT Council; NADRA, FBR and telecom systems declared critical information infrastructure under PECA 2016; Pakistan Information Security Framework (PISF) 2026 approved by the federal cabinet as a baseline for public bodies (Sep 2026); a National Cyber Security Act to create a Cyber Security Authority was still being prepared (Mar 2026); the DEEP cybersecurity roadmap was not yet complete (Feb 2026)Partial
AI governanceNational AI Policy 2025 approved by the federal cabinet on 30 Jul 2025, with an AI Council to oversee implementation and a target of 1 million AI professionals by 2030; implementation stalled and the AI Council's set-up was delayed for restructuring (Feb 2026); the policy is non-binding and there is no AI law (Oct 2026), while the draft National Data Governance Policy proposes risk-based rules for government AI useLaw or policy
