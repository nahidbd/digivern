Information Systems Journal special issue on digital sovereignty: call for papers

Information Systems Journal (Wiley); coordinating senior editor Petter Nielsen, University of Oslo · Deadline: March 31st, 2027: Full paper submission deadline

This special issue asks how governments and other actors can shape the way digital systems are sourced, governed and used, and how they can avoid or exit risky technology dependencies. Topics include open source and digital public goods, public-sector procurement and vendor lock-in, digital identity, surveillance and Indigenous data sovereignty. Paper-development workshops will be held before the deadline. The hard deadline will not be extended.

Who can apply
Information systems and digital-governance researchers; qualitative, quantitative, conceptual and review papers are welcome
Where
Worldwide
Deadline
March 31st, 2027: Full paper submission deadline
