ITU Academy course: GovStack Workflow Building Block (free, self-paced)

ITU, with Germany’s BMZ and GIZ, Estonia’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs and ESTDEV (GovStack initiative) · Deadline: Registration deadline: 31 Dec 2026

Introductory course on the GovStack Workflow building block for automating and integrating government services, covering workflow design, service APIs and performance standards; ITU certificate on completion.
