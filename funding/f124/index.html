Unpaid Internship – AI and Smart Sustainable Cities Communications Intern (vacancy notice 2391)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Standardization Bureau · Deadline: 8 January 2027 (Midnight Geneva Time)

ITU-T Study Group 20 is looking for an intern to run communications on smart sustainable cities and AI-powered virtual worlds: social media and short videos, drafting the Digital Transformation and Cities Digest, updating web pages and supporting ITU digital-transformation events. The post is unpaid; the intern or their institution covers insurance, travel and living costs.

Amount
Unpaid
Duration
6 months
Who can apply
Students enrolled in a degree programme, or graduates within six months of the start date, in ICT, economics, business, international relations, development, public or social policy, communications or a related field; under 30 (exceptions for PhD candidates); nationals of ITU Member States; advanced English
Where
Geneva, Switzerland
Deadline
8 January 2027 (Midnight Geneva Time)
