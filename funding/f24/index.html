Talos Fellowship, Spring 2027 cohort

Talos Network · Deadline: Applications are open until 6 December 2026

A European AI policy fellowship that trains early-career people in AI governance and places some of them in policy institutions, mostly in Brussels.

Amount
About €3,000 a month during the optional placement; training and Summit unpaid, Summit travel and accommodation covered
Duration
7 weeks part-time training, 1-week Summit in Brussels, optional 6-month placement
Who can apply
Graduates (or students graduating within six months of the fellowship’s end) with the right to work in the EU or UK, or a clear path to it, for placements; applicants based outside Europe generally not accepted
Where
Remote training; Summit and placements mostly in Brussels (some in London, Paris, Geneva, Stockholm)
Deadline
Applications are open until 6 December 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-07. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
