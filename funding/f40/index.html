Tarbell Fellowship 2027 (AI journalism)

Tarbell Center for AI Journalism · Deadline: Applications close on Nov. 22, 2026

Trains journalists to report on artificial intelligence and places them in major newsrooms for nine months.

Amount
Fellows US$75,000–120,000 and senior fellows US$115,000–135,000, depending on experience and location
Duration
12 months, full time (remote training from 7 June 2027, newsroom placement September 2027 – May 2028)
Who can apply
Anyone aged 18 or over by 7 June 2027; no degree required; senior fellows need 7+ years of experience; mainly for people with the right to work where partner newsrooms are
Where
Placements in the United States, United Kingdom and Hong Kong; training remote
Deadline
Applications close on Nov. 22, 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-11. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
