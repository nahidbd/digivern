DDI South Asia sub-grants, Cohort 3 (RFA #003)

Accountability Lab, for the Digital Democracy Initiative (DDI) South Asia · Closed: Apply by 25th November 2026

Small grants for locally led projects on digital civic education, inclusive digital participation, public-service accountability, open government, civic space and digital safety. Applications go through Accountability Lab’s grants portal and awards are made on a rolling basis.

Amount
Up to US$5,750 per award (up to 20 awards)
Duration
Up to four months; activities must end by 30 April 2027
Who can apply
Registered or informal NGOs, CBOs, CSOs, civil society networks and social enterprises; Cohort 1 and 2 grantees are not eligible
Where
Bangladesh and Pakistan
Deadline
Apply by 25th November 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-16. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
