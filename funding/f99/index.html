Unpaid internship – Emergency Telecommunications Intern (vacancy notice 2406)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Development Bureau (BDT) · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 10 December 2026

Supports ITU’s work on warning dissemination under the Early Warnings for All initiative, national emergency telecommunication plans and emergency connectivity, including for refugees. Tasks include briefing materials, meetings and a repository of case studies and experts on ICTs for disaster risk reduction.

Amount
Unpaid
Duration
6 months
Who can apply
Students or graduates of the last six months, normally under 30 (exceptions for PhD candidates), citizens of ITU Member States, studying ICT, emergency telecommunications, regulation or a related field; advanced English
Where
Geneva, Switzerland
Deadline
Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 10 December 2026
