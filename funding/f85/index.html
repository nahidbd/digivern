Talos Incubator Europe 2027: found an AI governance organisation

Talos Network · Deadline: 6 December 2026

A full-time incubator in which 10–14 founders find a co-founder, choose a project and build a new AI governance organisation in Europe, with mentoring, workshops and help raising funds. Participants are paid a monthly stipend through the programme and for two months afterwards.

Amount
€4,000 monthly stipend (prorated in the part-time first weeks); seed grants possible but not guaranteed
Duration
About three months of building (March–June 2027) plus a two-month funding runway
Who can apply
Prospective founders who have built something from scratch or led a team, with the right to work in Europe (including UK and Switzerland) or a clear path to it
Where
Europe (remote matching, in-person sprint in Brussels, showcase in London)
Deadline
6 December 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-19. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
