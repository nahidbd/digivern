GSMA Innovation Fund for AI-Enabled Financial Health

GSMA (Mobile for Development) · Deadline: 17 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC (UK time)

Grants for AI-enabled digital financial services that improve the financial resilience, agency and long-term security of underserved people, especially women, girls and persons with disabilities.

Amount
GBP 100,000 to GBP 250,000 per grant; applicants must match at least 25% of the grant
Duration
15 to 18 months (implementation from October 2027)
Who can apply
For-profit small and growing enterprises (up to 250 staff) registered and operating in the country of implementation, with active users, commercial revenue and at least one year of audited accounts; governments, NGOs, UN agencies, mobile operators, universities and accelerators are not eligible
Where
One or two ODA-eligible low- and middle-income countries (see the fund’s FAQs for the list)
Deadline
17 December 2026 at 23:59 UTC (UK time)

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-16. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
