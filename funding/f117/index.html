Digital Policy and AI Consultant (job opening 288799)

UN Environment Programme (UNEP), Office of the Chief Digital Officer · Deadline: Apply by 3 December 2026 (UN Careers postings close at 11:59 p.m. New York time)

UNEP's digital office wants a consultant to help put into practice the UN Environment Assembly resolution on the environmental sustainability of AI systems. The work covers research and partner engagement, policy briefs on AI and digital policy, knowledge products for the Coalition for Digital Environmental Sustainability (CODES), and product support for AI tools such as Environment GPT.

Amount
Fee set per assignment
Duration
12 months
Who can apply
Holders of an advanced degree (or a first degree plus two more years' experience) with at least two years in digital policy, AI, data science or environmental policy, including policy research on AI, emerging technology or digital transformation; fluent English
Where
Nairobi, Kenya (on site)
Deadline
Apply by 3 December 2026 (UN Careers postings close at 11:59 p.m. New York time)
