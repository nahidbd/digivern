Government Analytics Fellowship 2027

World Bank · Closed: Call for applications close on November 16

A six-month World Bank programme for public officials on using government data and analytics to improve public administration, built around an analytics project endorsed by each fellow’s organisation.

Amount
For applicants from ODA-eligible countries, the World Bank covers travel, lodging and subsistence for the Washington DC stay
Duration
6 months, including a 2-week stay in Washington DC in March 2027 (online and regional-hub tracks also offered)
Who can apply
Mid-level managers and senior analysts in central government organisations, nominated by their organisation with an analytics project; up to 2 per organisation, 40 fellows in total
Where
Governments worldwide
Deadline
Call for applications close on November 16

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-12. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
