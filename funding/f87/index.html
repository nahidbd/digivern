Software Engineer (P-3), vacancy notice 2454

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Radiocommunication Bureau (BR), Informatics, Administration and Publications Department · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 11 January 2027

ITU’s radio bureau wants a full-stack engineer to build and run cloud-native applications and APIs and to move legacy scientific code (Fortran, older C/C++) onto modern open-source platforms, with AI-assisted coding and testing. ITU says it charges no recruitment fee.

Amount
Net annual salary US$71,335 plus post adjustment of US$59,707
Duration
Fixed-term, 2 years, with possible extension
Who can apply
External candidates of all nationalities; advanced degree in computer science, information systems, engineering or science and at least five years’ software experience (two international), including running services on a major cloud platform; advanced knowledge of one ITU official language and intermediate of a second
Where
Geneva, Switzerland
Deadline
Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 11 January 2027

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-20. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
