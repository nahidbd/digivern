GovAI DC Summer Fellowship 2027

Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI) · Closed: 11:59 pm EST on Sunday, November 22nd, 2026

A paid, full-time research fellowship on AI governance at GovAI’s Washington office, the US counterpart to its UK summer programme.

Amount
$24,500 stipend plus support for travel to DC
Duration
Three months, 7 June – 27 August 2027
Who can apply
Researchers and professionals authorised to work in the US (no visa sponsorship)

Washington, DC, United States (in person)
