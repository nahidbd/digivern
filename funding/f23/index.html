Unpaid Internship – AI for Programme Management and Process Innovation Intern

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Regional Office for the Arab States, Cairo · Closed: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 11 November 2026

The intern will find programme workflows that AI could streamline and build AI assistants, dashboards and automations for ITU’s regional office.

Amount
Unpaid; intern or sponsor covers insurance, travel and living costs
Duration
6 months
Who can apply
Students or recent graduates (within six months) under 30, citizens of an ITU Member State, with at least one year’s experience in AI, data, automation or programme management
Where
Cairo, Egypt
Deadline
Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 11 November 2026
