Roster: Software Developer and Metadata Engineer Consultant for Open Code Infrastructure (OCI), vacancy notice 2375

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Standardization Bureau (TSB) · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

ITU is building a roster of developers to work on the AI for Health (AI4H) evaluation platform and annotation tool. The work covers backend, API, frontend and database features. It also includes making datasets readable in both Croissant and Bioschemas metadata formats, possibly through a 'BioCroissant' extension, and keeping the technical documentation up to date.

Amount
CHF 1,000 to 1,500 lump sum, depending on work experience, paid in monthly instalments against deliverables
Duration
3 months
Who can apply
Software developers with a university degree in computer science, software engineering, IT or a related field and at least 3 years of software development and documentation experience; full-stack TypeScript (Node.js/NestJS, React/Next.js), JSON-LD and schema validation; Python; at least intermediate English
Where
Geneva, Switzerland (country of contract); open to external candidates
Deadline
Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-18. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
