Consultant – Digital and AI Safety for Women Entrepreneurs (job opening 288465)

UN ESCAP, Subregional Office for South and South-West Asia · Deadline: 30 November 2026 (UN Careers posting ends 1 December 2026, 04:59 UTC)

Write a plain-language manual and policy paper on safe use of digital tools, payments and AI for women entrepreneurs, test it with users and run two workshops in India and one regional session.

Amount
US$20,000 lump sum, paid in six instalments against deliverables
Duration
5.5 months
Who can apply
Experts with a master’s degree (or bachelor’s plus two years) in cybersecurity, IT, computer science or digital policy and at least 10 years’ experience in cybersecurity or digital safety, including work in at least two South Asian countries
Where
Remote, linked to New Delhi, India (work starts in India and is adapted for South Asia)
Deadline
30 November 2026 (UN Careers posting ends 1 December 2026, 04:59 UTC)
