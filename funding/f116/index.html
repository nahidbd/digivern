Cybercrime Law and Technology Specialist Intern (job opening 288811)

UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC), Regional Office for Southeast Asia and the Pacific, Regional Cybercrime Programme · Deadline: Apply by 6 December 2026 (UN Careers postings close at 11:59 p.m. New York time)

An on-site internship with UNODC's regional cybercrime team, doing comparative legal research on cybercrime and cybersecurity laws, electronic evidence, jurisdiction, data protection and international cooperation. The intern drafts briefing notes and analyses, supports work on digital evidence and incident response, and helps run capacity-building workshops for governments in the region.

Amount
Unpaid
Duration
6 months, full time
Who can apply
Graduate students, recent graduates or final-year undergraduates in law, international or criminal law, cyber or information law, public policy or a related field; fluent English and Chinese required; open to all nationalities
Where
Bangkok, Thailand
Deadline
Apply by 6 December 2026 (UN Careers postings close at 11:59 p.m. New York time)
