Roster: Technical Remote Participation Moderator (vacancy notice 1425)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Information Services Department · Deadline: 31 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)

ITU is building a roster of moderators to run the virtual rooms of its hybrid meetings: setting up equipment, testing audio, managing remote speakers and doing first-level troubleshooting. Contracts are daily and depend on the meeting calendar.

Duration
Daily contracts tied to the event calendar (roster)
Who can apply
Secondary diploma or equivalent technical studies (a degree is an advantage), at least 2 years of relevant experience, advanced English, and working knowledge of Zoom or Teams, MS Office, Adobe, macOS and Windows
Where
Geneva, Switzerland
Deadline
31 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)

Apply on the official page
