Health Specialist (Information Systems), P-4, fixed term (#00133458), Global Programme Division, Health – MNCAH

UNICEF · Deadline: Closing at: 8 Dec 2026 - 23:55 EAT

A senior UNICEF post advising country offices and governments on digital health platforms for primary health care, covering interoperability requirements, data governance and security, DHIS2 and patient registries. Suits digital health architects with Ministry of Health experience.

Duration
Fixed-term appointment
Who can apply
Professionals with a Master's in public health, IT, computer science or related field and at least 8 years in digital health, health information systems or interoperability, including advising Ministries of Health in low- and middle-income countries and work with platforms such as DHIS2 or OpenMRS; open to all nationalities
Where
Nairobi, Kenya (Kenya Centre of Excellence)
