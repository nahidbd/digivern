Senior Applications Administrator

TISA Bank Ltd · Deadline: Applications close at 05:00 PM, Wednesday 9th December 2026

TISA Bank is hiring a senior administrator to run, tune and troubleshoot its core business applications, manage incidents and changes, support integrations and APIs, and keep systems compliant with IT security, access-control and audit requirements. The role is advertised by the employer on PNG JobSeek, where applications are submitted.
