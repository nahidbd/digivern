Roster: ITU-FCDO Technical Support and Coordination Consultant (vacancy notice 2248, home-based)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU) · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

Coordinates the in-country workplan of ITU’s partnership with the UK FCDO Digital Access Programme, giving technical input on policy, connectivity, digital sustainability and digital skills.

Amount
US$230–250 per working day, depending on experience
Duration
60 working days
