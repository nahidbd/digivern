Roster for the Telecommunication/ICT Statistics Programme (home-based)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), ICT Data and Analytics Division · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

Consultants support ITU’s collection and verification of the global telecom and ICT statistics it publishes: validating data with member states, maintaining the database, computing estimates and writing short analyses.

Amount
USD 185 (3–4 years’ experience), 230 (5–6 years) or 280 (7–9 years) per day
Duration
Varies from 15 to 80 working days
Who can apply
University degree in statistics, economics, engineering, international relations or business and at least 3 years collecting and analysing statistics; telecom/ICT statistics experience an advantage; advanced English
Where
Home-based (remote)
Deadline
Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-17. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
