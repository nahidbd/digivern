Roster: Country Project Coordination Consultant, Africa National Broadband Mapping Systems project

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Development Bureau · Deadline: 31 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)

National focal points for ITU’s Africa-BB-Maps project, which builds national broadband mapping systems: coordinating daily project work, monitoring contractors and milestones, organising workshops and reporting progress.

Amount
USD 230–420 per working day
Duration
6 months, renewable
Who can apply
Nationals or legal residents of the country applied for, with a degree in ICT, GIS, engineering or project management and at least 5 years of project coordination; advanced English, or advanced French and intermediate English for francophone countries
Where
Benin, Botswana, Burundi, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya, Malawi, Nigeria, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe
Deadline
31 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)
