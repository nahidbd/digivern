Associate Protection Officer (Data Protection) (JR2666861)

UNHCR (UN Refugee Agency), Bangladesh operation · Deadline: November 25, 2026

Advises UNHCR units and partners in the Rohingya response on data protection: data-sharing agreements, data protection impact assessments and the data-processing inventory. The officer also trains partners and is alternate data protection focal point for the Bangladesh operation.

Duration
UNOPS IICA1 contract; target start 1 January 2027
Who can apply
Bachelor’s degree in international law, law or political science; 3–5 years in registration, identity management or data protection; experience with data protection tools such as OneTrust; English
Where
Cox’s Bazar, Bangladesh, with missions to Bhasan Char
Deadline
November 25, 2026
