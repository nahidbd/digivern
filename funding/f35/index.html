EDSAFE AI Fellowship, 2027 cohort (Catalyst, Women in AI and Vanguard tracks)

EDSAFE AI Alliance · Deadline: Applications are due November 30, 2026, at 10 p.m. EST

A virtual cohort of 30+ fellows working on safe and effective AI in education, with expert workshops and mentoring; each fellow builds and deploys a small capstone project in their own setting.

Amount
No honorarium; fully funded, no participation cost
Duration
12 months from January, about 2–4 hours a month
Who can apply
Catalyst and Women in AI tracks open worldwide; Vanguard track for K-12 educators and education-agency staff, with preference for US-based applicants
Where
Global, virtual
Deadline
Applications are due November 30, 2026, at 10 p.m. EST

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-25. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
