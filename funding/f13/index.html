National Consultant to develop a shock-responsive MSME finance prototype and deployment package (IC/TSR/2610/56)

UNDP Sri Lanka (Joint SDG Fund) · Closed: Apply by Nov 10, 2026

Designs a finance prototype that lets micro, small and medium enterprises cope with trade shocks, plus a package for deploying it, under the Trade-Shock Resilience for MSMEs and Vulnerable Households project. Individual consultant notice; offers go through the UNDP supplier portal.
