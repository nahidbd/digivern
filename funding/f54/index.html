Disaster Preparedness Consultant: National Emergency Telecommunication Plans and Mobile Early Warning (home-based)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU) · Deadline: 31 December 2026 (midnight Geneva time)

Consultants run national consultations and draft national emergency telecommunication plans and regulations, and assess early-warning and mobile alert systems, advising on specifications and cooperation with mobile operators. Vacancy notice 1152.
