Information Management Officer (Nutrition), Islamabad (LICA-9, UNOPS Individual Contractor Agreement)

UNICEF Pakistan · Deadline: Closing at: 4 Dec 2026 - 23:55 PKT

UNICEF Pakistan seeks an information manager for the nutrition sector to run data collection tools, databases, automated workflows and Power BI or GIS dashboards for emergency response. A South Asia data role for people who can turn field data into decision tools.

Who can apply
Individual contractors with a bachelor's in information management, information systems, computer science, statistics, GIS, public health or a related field, at least 2 years in information or data management, and fluent English and Urdu
Where
Islamabad, Pakistan
