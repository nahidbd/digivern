Nexa Lighthouse: Transition-to-Scale funding for climate and health innovations in Asia and the Pacific

Grand Challenges Canada and AVPN, with the Government of Canada and partner foundations · Deadline: All proposals must be submitted via the Fluxx portal by December 8, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. UTC)

Scale-up funding for innovations that help health systems cope with climate change: climate-informed early warning and monitoring of heat, air quality and mosquito-borne disease, and climate-responsive patient care. The deadline was extended from 17 November.

Amount
About US$250,000 to US$2,000,000 per innovation
Who can apply
Organisations incorporated and implementing in eligible Asia-Pacific countries with innovations ready to scale
Where
Eligible countries across Asia and the Pacific (list in the funding document)
Deadline
All proposals must be submitted via the Fluxx portal by December 8, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. UTC)

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-13. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
