Roster: Circular Economy Consultant, Asia Region (vacancy notice 1949, home-based)

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Development Bureau (BDT), Digital Networks & Environment Department · Deadline: Application deadline (Midnight Geneva Time): 31 December 2026

ITU is building a pool of experts to help Asia-Pacific governments draft e-waste, extended producer responsibility and right-to-repair policies, cost e-waste systems and brief officials, plus write ITU Academy course material.

Amount
USD 185–360 per working day, depending on experience
Duration
To be determined (roster)
