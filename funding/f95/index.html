NGI Restack fund: second open call (2027-01R)

NLnet Foundation, with funding from the European Commission (DG CONNECT, Horizon Europe grant agreement No. 101299072) · Deadline: January 3rd 2027 12:00 CET (noon)

Restack gives practical R&D grants for open, operational internet infrastructure across the technology stack. This covers core internet protocols, open alternatives to closed online services, secure devices, software supply-chain tools and standards work. Grantees can also get in-kind help such as security audits and accessibility scans. Most AI projects are out of scope.

Amount
€5,000 to €50,000 per project, with possible scale-up (€7 million in total to 2030)
Who can apply
Developers, researchers and organisations building open internet-commons technology. Eligibility follows Horizon Europe rules; see NLnet's eligibility page
Where
Countries eligible under Horizon Europe (EU member states and associated countries)
Deadline
January 3rd 2027 12:00 CET (noon)

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-22. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
