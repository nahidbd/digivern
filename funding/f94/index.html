Study Groups Advisor (P-4), vacancy notice 2425

International Telecommunication Union (ITU), Telecommunication Development Bureau (BDT), Projects, Partnerships and Digital Skills Department · Deadline: 15 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)

The adviser will coordinate the ITU-D Study Groups and act as expert and secretary to one or more of them, supporting chairs and rapporteurs on contributions and meetings. The role also covers study-group budgets, partnerships and resource mobilisation, outreach events, the Study Group website and fellowships.

Duration
Fixed-term, 2 years, renewable
Who can apply
External candidates with an advanced degree in law, economics, ICT/telecom policy, engineering, computer science or a related field and at least seven years' experience in telecom/ICT policy (three at international level); one ITU official language at advanced level and a second at intermediate level
Where
Geneva, Switzerland
Deadline
15 December 2026 (Midnight Geneva Time)
