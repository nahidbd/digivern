Advanced Analytics and Digital Product Specialist (JR128634)

World Food Programme (WFP) · Deadline: 15 December 2026-23:59-GMT+01:00 Central European Time (Rome)

WFP is hiring a specialist to build predictive, optimisation and scenario models on its financial and operational data, and to design and test AI-enabled digital products. The post also covers data governance and data-quality controls, oversight dashboards, and training colleagues to use analytics tools.

Duration
Fixed-term contract (one-year probation)
Who can apply
Professionals with an advanced degree in computer science, engineering, statistics or a related field (or a first degree plus two more years' experience) and at least five years in advanced analytics and digital product management, preferably in finance; fluent English; open to all nationalities
Where
Rome, Italy
Deadline
15 December 2026-23:59-GMT+01:00 Central European Time (Rome)
