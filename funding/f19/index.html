National Consultant: Advancing the Digital Safety of Women Journalists in Pakistan

UNESCO, Communication and Information Sector · Closed: 11 November 2026, midnight (UTC+5)

Maps protection mechanisms for women journalists facing technology-facilitated gender-based violence and produces a training toolkit, a resource guide, a guidance note and a roadmap for UNESCO’s work.

Duration
6 months (about 20–25 working days)
Who can apply
Pakistani nationals with a master’s degree, 7+ years of relevant experience and English (Urdu an asset)
Where
Islamabad, Pakistan
