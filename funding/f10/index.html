GovAI UK Summer Fellowship 2027, Research Track

Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI) · Deadline: 23:59 UK time (GMT), Sunday 22 November 2026

A paid three-month research fellowship on AI governance and policy, with mentorship and a desk at GovAI in London. Suits people whose main work is producing or using research or policy analysis.

Amount
£14,000, plus travel support to London
Duration
Three months, 7 June to 27 August 2027, full-time
Who can apply
Researchers and policy professionals from government, academia, industry or civil society, early-career to experienced; no strict degree requirement
Where
In person at GovAI’s London office; temporary work visas sponsored
Deadline
23:59 UK time (GMT), Sunday 22 November 2026

Apply on the official page

Checked 2026-11-18. GovTec lists opportunities free of charge and never asks for fees; apply only through the organisation’s own page.
